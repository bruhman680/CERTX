## What the mathematics permits

For fixed attention scores, alpha_j = exp(s_j)/sum_k exp(s_k). Equal scores give exactly 1/L. If all scores remain within a fixed spread Delta, alpha_j lies between exp(-Delta)/L and exp(Delta)/L. This is a conditional bound; varying the query, key scores, masks, or score spread with context length removes its universal force. A target score growing as log(L-1)+c against L-1 zero-score distractors yields alpha_target = exp(c)/(1+exp(c)), independent of L. Softmax normalization therefore does not prove monotone positional decay or an exponential session law. Multiple heads/layers, values, output projections, and residual paths also prevent identifying one attention coefficient with the complete functional contribution of a token.

Lost-in-the-Middle's reported primacy plus recency is direct pressure against the simple story that oldest tokens universally fade fastest. It establishes behavioral difficulty under its examined tasks, not its unique causal mechanism, a CERTX score, or every contemporary architecture.

At a fixed prefix, sampling temperature acts on vocabulary logits: p_T(v)=softmax(z/T). Its pairwise log odds are (z_blue-z_green)/T. The probe's variable named `log_odds` is the raw logit difference, equal to output log odds at T=1. Temperature cannot change that forward pass's attention. Once sampled tokens differ, later prefixes and attention may differ indirectly; that trajectory effect is a separate question.

## Matched-cache scope and a useful square

Let P be token prefixes with fixed model, positions, masks, runtime, and precision; K(P) their causal caches; Z(P) the next-token logit vector. Compare P→Z(P) by full recomputation with P→K(P)→Z_cache(P). The probe measures the infinity-norm defect for three constructed prefixes and accepts <10^-3. Passing establishes a bounded implementation comparison for those inputs, not uniform equality across lengths, architectures, cache truncation, quantization, precision, or training modes. The supplied GPT2-style unpadded split relies on model-managed positions; new implementations should make the position/mask contract explicit. A square is sufficient: no third operation requires a cube.

Editing an earlier token requires rebuilding its causal descendants' cached representations; retaining a stale cache is a different input, not a fair cache-toggle control. Eviction changes accessibility; recomputation and exact reuse of the intact prefix should preserve it. Sliding-window, quantized, or approximate caches need their own reference computation and error declaration.

**Recommended, not implemented:** retain the current probe as an implementation control; add fixed-budget position/length/distractor factorials, shuffled assignments and nonce mappings, head/layer attention traces, and predeclared held-out contexts. Measure raw-logit contrast and retrieval accuracy separately from sampled trajectories. A serious rival is local pattern completion/recency without any durable external wake; positional encoding, distractor interference, and truncation remain alternatives to normalization-only dilution. A successful relation-swap effect alone cannot distinguish them.

The open continuation is therefore narrower and more useful: which refresh preserves the dependency needed for a later question, under which readout and horizon? This leaves room for play while making future mechanistic claims capable of failing.
