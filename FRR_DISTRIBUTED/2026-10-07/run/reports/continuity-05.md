# Continuity 05 — stigmergy and adaptive transport

2026-10-07. Source weave, not a new experiment or recovered genealogy.

## Surviving relationship and its boundary

A changed accessible carrier can alter a later response after focal-state reset. This survives both the garden transplant and the reconstructed transport replay, conditional on their declared boundaries. It does not establish that the carrier contains the erased message, that the recipient recognizes historical provenance, or that every field-mediated effect is swarm communication.

The garden's carrier is a genome-keyed numerical fitness bonus coupled naturally to a genotype archive. The ring's carrier is edge conductance prepared from a separate profile and then frozen. These have different read paths: additive fitness evaluation versus a weighted diffusion operator. The latter carries a rule for transport, independent of written message polarity. Its zero state remains zero without an input. This distinction should travel with every downstream use of “wake.”

## Primary contacts and jobs

- **Stigmergy as an intervention/control relative.** [Boldini, Civitella and Porfiri (2024)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39233720/) model swarm and environmental traces as continua and derive trace distributions for desired formations, with analytical and numerical examples. Inspected the author abstract and figure captions through PubMed; full PMC article was blocked by a browser challenge. This supplies a concrete inverse question: which environment modification produces which behavior? It does not validate a central garden engine as a swarm or imply that ring conductances encode communications. The reference list supplies Grassé (1959) as an older genealogy contact; that paper was not directly inspected here.
- **State/topology feedback relative.** [Gross, Dommar D'Lima and Blasius (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.208701), inspected publisher abstract: susceptibles rewire away from infected nodes, with topology/disease interplay and local bifurcation analysis. This is a live feedback example. Frozen preparation in the ring is a different operation, so epidemic transitions cannot be imported as its thresholds.
- **Time/structure rival.** [Delvenne, Lambiotte and Rocha (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.4155), inspected author abstract: relaxation depends on both network structure and event timing, under their stated absence of temporal-structure correlations. This pressures any spectral-gap-only retention claim. Their stochastic relaxation result does not determine sign-decoder accuracy for a particular two-bit alignment.
- **Failure-to-repair contact.** [Catté, Lions, Morel and Coll (1992)](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0729012), inspected publisher search-return abstract and metadata: their modified Perona–Malik formulation has noise stability and existence/uniqueness results. This provides a repair lineage if preparation becomes noisy or adaptation stays live. Full proofs were not inspected and their guarantees are not transferred to the thresholded finite ring. The Perona–Malik author PDF opened but supplied no extracted text; no detailed PDF claims are based on that contact.

These are different source roles, not four independent replications. Local README already cited these works; renewed contact establishes inspected scope, not independent discovery.

## Serious rival and evidence construction

Experiments-03 reports the donor history and locally isolates the target entry. Its strongest rival remains an explicitly additive bonus at a deliberately selected promotion boundary: an equal manually inserted entry would give the same recipient response. Historical origin is established by preparation, not recovered by the recipient. This is carrier causality and a local existence witness; it is not historical semantics or a prevalence estimate.

For the transport reconstruction, a hand-constructed weak-link field aligned with two message regions can suffice to protect them. The writer relocation result sharpens this rival: the same boundaries obstruct writing at spikes and protect messages inside arcs. A lower gap can coexist with no holding benefit at spike writers. “Adaptation creates useful memory” therefore needs comparison with static geometry, alignment and noise exposure before attributing advantage to an adaptive mechanism.

## Concrete improvement, recommended only

Preserve three separate endpoints: retained-carrier impulse response, retained-message decoding after holding, and recovery after erasing message state. Predeclare independent recipient/message distributions and horizons. Compare history-prepared carrier with a numerically identical manual carrier; they should agree under the present read rules. A claim requiring historical identity needs an added, independently motivated observable or read path, not a new label on the same numbers.

For the ring, cross writer placement, boundary placement and frozen versus live adaptation; share written states for holding comparisons. Include random fields matched by mean/histogram and, where feasible, spectrum. Search the noise-stable nonlinear-diffusion repair before implementing live adaptation, then establish conservation/stability for the actual discretization separately. Report chance-level long-horizon failure rather than select a favorable horizon. The garden should use a frozen recipient distribution and report status-change frequency alongside its selected witness.

A cube adds little to this contact. A typed reset/replay comparison suffices: joint states `(x,e)` comprise message values and conductances (ring), or candidate/table/RNG and field/archive (garden). `Reset_x` restores the declared focal state, `Restore_e` restores the carrier, and `Replay_H` uses identical input/noise for horizon H. Compare `q Replay_H Reset_x(x,e_altered)` with `q Replay_H Reset_x(x,e_baseline)`, using impulse L2 response for the ring and promotion-history/status for the garden. This contrast establishes an effect relative to those interventions; it neither proves message identity nor localizes causality beyond the controlled carrier distinction.
