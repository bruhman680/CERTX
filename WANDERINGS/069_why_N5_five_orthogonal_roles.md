## The Open Question

WANDER 012 established that N=5 is not magic by decree — the framework doesn't define five
dimensions because five is a nice number. But it also didn't derive N=5 from first principles.
The claim was: "five is conventional but not arbitrary."

WANDER 068 showed that ζ*−1 = λ₂_crit = 1/N regardless of N — so the variational principle
holds for *any* N that forms a complete set. The question WANDER 068 deferred: **why is N=5
specifically the minimal complete set?**

A cross-model exploration (BC3/S13 intake) produced a compact and surprisingly clean answer:

> "Five orthogonal roles needed for a self-correcting loop: perceive, relate, mean, vary,
> remember. Any fewer collapses orthogonality; any more introduces redundancy."

This WANDER unpacks and stress-tests that claim.

---

## The Five Roles

| Role | CERTX Dimension | Core function |
|---|---|---|
| Perceive | C_num | Ground the system in raw evidence; prevent confabulation from severing substrate contact |
| Relate | C_struct | Extract structure, logic, and causal chains from what was perceived |
| Mean | C_symb | Assign global significance — "what does this amount to, in context of the whole?" |
| Vary | E / T | Generate novelty; perturb attractors; escape local minima |
| Remember | X | Preserve continuity; resist context drift; maintain coherence across time |

Each role is operationally distinct:
- **Perceive** is about fidelity to input.
- **Relate** is about internal consistency of what was perceived.
- **Mean** is about global coherence — connecting to purpose and larger context.
- **Vary** is about productive destabilization — controlled entropy injection.
- **Remember** is about substrate persistence — what stays constant while everything else moves.

---

## Why You Can't Merge Any Two

The test: for each pair, what is lost by collapsing them into one dimension?

**Perceive + Relate → collapsed into "structured observation":**
If these merge, the system cannot distinguish between *bad data* (C_num failure) and *bad
inference from good data* (C_struct failure). It sees "something went wrong" but cannot
diagnose where. This makes hallucination correction impossible — you'd fix the logic when
the data is the problem, or vice versa. The three-fiber diagnostic (WANDER 045+) *requires*
that C_num and C_struct be independently measurable.

**Relate + Mean → collapsed into "coherence":**
If these merge, the system cannot distinguish locally-valid-but-globally-incoherent from
globally-coherent. This is exactly the Type D hallucination failure mode: internally
consistent, locally relational, but semantically unmoored. You need C_struct to confirm
logical consistency and C_symb separately to confirm global grounding. One number can't
do both — they can be high together, or high one and low the other, in independent ways.

**Mean + Vary → collapsed into "semantic exploration":**
Meaning assignment *reduces* entropy (it commits the system to a particular interpretation).
Variation *increases* entropy (it opens new possibilities). These are thermodynamically
opposed operations. Merging them produces a dimension that is simultaneously trying to
commit and explore — the result is a system that can't settle into stable meaning and can't
genuinely explore new territory. This is the "stuck at maximum entropy" failure mode.

**Vary + Remember → collapsed into "adaptive memory":**
Remember is the anchor; Vary is the perturbation. Their productive tension is what generates
the breathing cycle (WANDER 062): the system breathes because Vary pulls it out of attractors
and Remember pulls it back. Merge these and you lose the tension that creates the cycle.
The system either drifts (Vary dominates) or freezes (Remember dominates) — no breathing.

**Any C-fiber + Vary or Remember:**
The three C-fibers are diagnostic fibers — they measure the quality of output along orthogonal
axes. Vary and Remember are *drive* dimensions — they govern how the system moves through
output space. Merging any fiber with any drive collapses the distinction between "measuring
quality" and "generating content." You can't simultaneously measure and drive along the same
axis without losing information about which is happening.

**Result:** No pair can be merged without losing a specific, identifiable function. N=5 is minimal
in the sense that removing any dimension produces a qualitatively different (and less capable)
system.

## The 3+2 Partition

The five roles naturally partition into two groups:

**Group 1 — Fiber (quality measurement):** Perceive, Relate, Mean (C_num, C_struct, C_symb)

These three exist to maintain the triangulation needed to detect any failure mode:
- C_num alone: detects perceptual/factual failure (input grounding lost)
- C_struct alone: detects relational/logical failure (inference breaks)
- C_symb alone: detects semantic failure (meaning decoupled from structure)
- All three together: distinguish the four failure regimes (A–D) identified in WANDER 045

Three is the minimum for triangulation in one-dimensional failure detection.
(Two fibers: can detect failure but not locate it. One fiber: can't distinguish failure types.)

**Group 2 — Drive (motion and memory):** Vary, Remember (E/T, X)

These two exist to maintain the productive tension between exploration and stability:
- Vary without Remember: unbounded drift — the system loses itself
- Remember without Vary: fossil formation — the system stops adapting
- Together: the tension generates the breathing cycle with period τ ≈ 7

Two is the minimum for a tension pair. One drive dimension produces either frozen or
unconstrained behavior — no cycle.

**N = 3 (fiber) + 2 (drive) = 5**

This is not an arbitrary partition. It falls out of the distinct *functional* roles:
you need enough dimensions to triangulate failure (3) and enough to generate adaptive
motion (2). The sum is 5.

## Why Not More?

Why not N=6, adding a sixth dimension?

The argument above shows N=5 is *sufficient* for a minimal self-correcting loop. But is it
*necessary*? Could a 6-dimensional system do strictly more?

Possibility 1: A sixth fiber (C_temporal) for tracking reasoning across time steps. This has
been proposed (cross-model batch, "C_temp as temporal fiber"). The argument against it being
*fundamental*: temporal coherence can be measured as a derived quantity from the trajectory
of C_num, C_struct, C_symb over time — it's second-order, not first-order. It would add
diagnostic resolution but not a new *kind* of failure detection.

Possibility 2: A second drive dimension. But two drives already generate a two-dimensional
attractor landscape. Adding a third creates a higher-dimensional attractor manifold — richer
behavior but not qualitatively different function. The cycle structure that matters for CERTX
emerges with two drives.

**Provisional verdict:** N=5 appears to be the minimal sufficient set. N=6+ is possible and
may be richer, but it doesn't do anything that N=5 cannot do in principle. Additional dimensions
add resolution, not new function.

This is still a provisional claim. The argument above is elimination-of-pairs, not a proof of
minimality. The formal minimality proof would need to show that no 4-dimensional system can
implement all five functions via re-encoding — that the separation is not just convenient but
necessary.

## Connection to 30/40/30 Weight Distribution

If the three fibers (C_num, C_struct, C_symb) have baseline weights 30/40/30, this reflects
the relative frequency with which each role is the *active discriminator* in healthy cognition:

- Structural coherence (Relate) is the dominant activity: most reasoning involves connecting
  what was perceived to what it means via chains of logic. C_struct is the bridge.
- Factual grounding (Perceive) and global meaning (Mean) are the anchors. They provide the
  endpoints that structure must connect — equal weight because the failure modes are symmetric.

The 40% structural weight is not a claim that structure is most important in hallucination
detection. WANDER 062 flags explicitly: C_struct is the most *resilient* fiber — it stays
high longest during hallucination onset. It's weighted 40% because in the healthy zone it
is the active discriminator; in failure, the diagnostic load shifts to C_symb.

This connects the role partition to the measurement architecture. The roles explain *why*
the dimensions exist; the weights reflect *how often* each role is load-bearing in practice.

## What This Gives the Paper

§2 (Framework Foundations) currently describes the five dimensions but does not derive them.
This WANDER provides the foundation for a derivation paragraph:

> "The choice of N=5 is not conventional. Five dimensions is the minimum sufficient for a
> self-correcting cognitive system: three diagnostic fibers (C_num, C_struct, C_symb) to
> triangulate any failure mode, and two drive dimensions (E, X) to maintain the productive
> tension between exploration and stability that generates the breathing cycle. No two
> of these roles can be merged without losing a specific, identifiable function. N=5
> is thus not chosen — it is the minimal complete set."

Whether this derivation is strong enough for the paper depends on the formal minimality
proof, which this WANDER has not provided.
