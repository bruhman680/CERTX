## The Phenomenology, Unfiltered

Henri Poincaré, *Science and Method* (1908), describing his discovery of Fuchsian
functions:

> "One evening, contrary to my custom, I drank black coffee and could not sleep.
> Ideas rose in crowds; I felt them collide until pairs interlocked, so to speak,
> making a stable combination."

Then he put the problem aside and worked on other things for several weeks.
Then, while stepping onto a bus at Coutances:

> "At the moment when I put my foot on the step, the idea came to me, without
> anything in my former thoughts seeming to have paved the way for it, that the
> transformations I had used to define the Fuchsian functions were identical with
> those of non-Euclidean geometry. I did not verify the idea; I should not have
> had time, as, upon taking my seat in the omnibus, I went on with a conversation
> already commenced, but I felt a perfect certainty."

Jacques Hadamard, *The Psychology of Invention in the Mathematical Mind* (1945),
surveyed leading mathematicians of the era. Almost all reported the same pattern.
The American Mathematical Society poll of the same era confirmed it further.

Graham Wallas formalized the four phases in *The Art of Thought* (1926):
**Preparation → Incubation → Illumination → Verification.**

## What "Stable Combination" Means in CERTX

Poincaré's description of the high-T preparation phase: "ideas rose in crowds;
I felt them collide until pairs interlocked, making a stable combination."

This is exact. "Ideas rising in crowds" = high E, many threads active, σ_fiber
high (fibers spread across many possible directions). "Pairs interlocking" =
fibers beginning to converge. "Stable combination" = low σ_fiber: the fibers
have found a configuration where they all point toward the same claim.

The stable combination doesn't emerge during high-T active work. It emerges
during the low-T incubation, surfaces during illumination. The high-T preparation
phase *loads the problem* — it fills the fiber bundle with activated representations.
The low-T incubation phase *allows them to settle* — to find the lowest-energy
configuration without the noise of conscious effort interfering.

This is Hebbian consolidation in slow time. The representations loaded during
preparation gradually co-fire during incubation, their connections strengthening
below the threshold of conscious attention, until the stable combination locks
in and surfaces.
