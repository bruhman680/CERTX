## Independent falsifications / qualified confirmations worth keeping

1. **Algorithmic search is not a series of separate discoveries.** The adaptive society's 40 optimizer iterations selected a threshold learner that won a small-world short holdout but lost on larger/longer fresh worlds to fixed .50, while also leaving more cells depleted. This is why counts were reduced.
2. **Targeted actions beat randomly allocated actions at matched quantity.** `SOC_PHYSICS_REPORT.md` matched *exact per-tick harvest counts* and found a large energy difference, exposing resource–action covariance as a causal operational relationship in the synthetic world.
3. **Energy-sensitive manual rules produce a welfare tradeoff.** Long-horizon energy-aware rescue beats a fixed threshold, but reverse rescue earns even greater mean energy while leaving more cells at the energy floor. A single aggregate score is insufficient.
4. **A teacher-free neural cell can learn a functional energy input.** `SOC_ENERGY_AWARE_AUDIT.md` masks only the learned energy observation with fixed identical weights; final energy drops in both stable and mildly changed tests. This is a narrow causal demonstration, *not* online adaptation or general intelligence.
5. **Memory can repair predictive compression loss.** Macro-state history substantially improves next-microstate predictions, especially in a phase-locked fixed-rule world; these bins are not `C_num`, `C_struct` or `C_symb` measurements.
6. **Acquired inheritance is mechanically real but not yet useful evolution.** Acquired thresholds reached offspring; neither acquired-vs-germline inheritance nor mutation demonstrated robust population fitness gains over the relevant controls.
