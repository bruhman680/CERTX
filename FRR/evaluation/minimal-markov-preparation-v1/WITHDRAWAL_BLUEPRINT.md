# Minimal world preparation, not active replacement

The user wants to withdraw researcher-authored behavior and let organization arise within an understood small world. The user's chosen material is the eight-state Markov puzzle located in their subreddit. Pattern matching/recognition can be a capacity the model uses; it is not an assigned answer or required behavior.

## What withdraws from the active model view

- FRR/CERTX behavioral instructions, play charter, next-move coaching and operator-written exploration recipes.
- Prebuilt Scheme/calculator/source-reader tools and action-by-parenthesis routing. Old artifacts, runtimes and logs remain archived.
- Researcher classifications, expected groups, formulas for success, approval labels, prescribed solutions and novelty requirements.
- Repeated promotion of older instruction seeds as current policy. Older seeds remain historical records; a new explicit user decision can introduce material later.

The added canonical LoRA also needs explicit accounting: it was trained on a supervised positive/zero/missing to YES/NO/WITHHOLD mapping, not a general reasoning or FRR acquisition. Its broad effects are not established. A proposed stripped version would use the unchanged pretrained base checkpoint without attaching this added adapter, retaining the original adapter and every prior record. That is an effective-model condition change, not erasure of the base weights or removal of inherited language knowledge. No model switch has happened yet.

## Minimal material and persistent effects

Two data-only candidate views are prepared. Neither contains a behavioral framework, solved grouping, macro-state names, answer key, task score or advice to reason in a specified way.

- TABLE_VIEW_DRAFT.json: the original post's three eight-by-eight transition arrays as finite material, plus an initially empty marks field. The inherited array order/number encoding is a presentation choice, not a claim of neutrality or an agent-created language.
- TRAJECTORY_VIEW_DRAFT.json: an example observation sequence from the unmodified exact kernel, plus marks. Its initial state, RNG and step count are retained externally. No resets were added to keep the stream interesting.

The proposed primitive effect is that every raw output becomes retained material. Parentheses, invented notation and errors of interpretation are not rejected as programs. Future patterns can influence subsequent model behavior through its own marks. No host interpreter silently gives executable meaning to invented text. Tools that truly execute outside the model would require additional fixed mechanics; we will not call written descriptions working machines.

This is a preparation, not a final prompt/actuation design. The existing live history is still at 750 and has not been reset, replaced, imported into another garden or rewritten. Before activation, the chosen representation and its continuity with retained model-authored material need to be recorded. Prior researcher scaffolding must not be quietly reintroduced through a full old prompt dump.

## What remains external

The chosen kernel/version, exact macro partition, quotient matrices, analytic reference values, observer hypotheses and evaluations. Our preferences for tool use, novelty, helpfulness, success, efficiency or even a particular kind of structure are not a model-facing reward or instruction. Interpretation errors remain material; broken storage or exceeded host resources remain genuine operational boundaries.

The pretrained model and its tokenizer already carry language and patterns. New constructions would develop from that inherited capacity; this is not emergence from a blank nervous system. Removing scaffolding alone cannot guarantee that useful tools, language or reasoning habits will appear.
