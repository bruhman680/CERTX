# What the existing garden teaches this build

5 November 2026. Targeted synthesis of the linked records, not a new audit of every CERTX experiment.

## Roots: what persists and what does not

The current small garden is a fixed 135M-parameter model with a previously trained adapter. Its ongoing continuity lives in externally saved observations, outputs, tool receipts and a bounded history view. The weights are not being updated during this continuous run. Restarting inference therefore does not mean erasing its external history; conversely, a persistent notebook does not prove persistent neural learning. See [original continuous setup](../source-access-three-cycle-v1/REPORT.md).

The primary is one continuing selected environment, not a merger of whichever experimental forks performed best. Its original 48 entries and 257 subsequent generation records are preserved. The current cursor is 305. Earlier branches and controlled studies are separate evidence.

The fundamental shelf and the user's curiosity sentence were introduced together. Their separate causal effects were not measured. Historical descriptions should stay attached to the policy version that produced them.

## Lessons from outside this one plot

The [soil/reset toy](../soil-reset-robustness-v1/REPORT.md) made carrier attribution depend on what an intervention actually changed, including leakage and off-target effects. That is a finite constructed system, not proof of where this model's memory lives. For this patch we can state the carrier precisely: its number is recovered from host-owned action receipts.

The [decision-loss comparison](../decision-loss-comparison-v1/REPORT.md) showed that changing a training weight did not automatically solve semantic binding; superficial completion and field-position controls mattered. Here, grammatical command use must not be promoted to reasoning competence.

The [verification layer](../../modules/verification-layer.md) separates a claim, its scope and its evidence. Byte equality and replay establish integrity, not correctness of every interpretation. The [bounded experiment loop](../../modules/bounded-experiment-loop.md) keeps changes versioned and failed trials visible. The [carrier intervention guidance](../../modules/carrier-interventions.md) cautions against inferring a memory mechanism from its name. The [selective observation guidance](../../modules/selective-observation.md) keeps complete raw history while concentrating discussion on changes rather than flooding the conversation with every ordinary entry.

## Resulting design

The notebook already gives the model language about exploration. Add a small piece of changing terrain: a number, two objects and consistent consequences. Expose enough grammar to act; leave the transformation rules for discovery through actual observations. Keep writing, legacy tools, revisiting and doing nothing available. Supply no reward, destination or demand for novelty.

This is a practical extension of the environment, not a claim that a finite numeric world supplies every ingredient for emergence. A larger world would add interpretive ambiguity before we know whether this model uses the smaller one. Failures, repetitions and non-use remain legitimate observations.

The patch is built alongside the primary. Its host-authored demonstrations are not experiences of the model. Introducing it later will be an explicit new environmental version at the existing boundary, preserving the same historical garden. Because the stop labels also have no model exposure yet, an immediate introduction would be a combined experienced change, not a clean single-factor causal comparison.
