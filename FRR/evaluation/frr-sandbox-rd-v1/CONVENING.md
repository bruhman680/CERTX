# Role-separated R&D decisions

14 November2026, America/Chicago. Five parallel trajectories: interface, FRR presentation, baseline/readout, targeted sources, and peripheral sources. Reports and messages informed the decisions below. Shared ancestry/exposure means this is collaboration and artifact review, not five independent replications.

1. Retain the tested physical Python/shell runtime for the first comparison. Do not simultaneously replace the world and add FRR, then attribute outcomes to FRR.
2. Replace only the cloned WORLD_RULES text with one accurate mechanical document instead of another stack of old overrides. Preserve its parent bytes externally and retain all other world files.
3. Fresh conversational framing is common to every arm. No-FRR must not inherit the repeated eight-example conversation while FRR gets a reset. Fresh conversation does not erase inherited writing.
4. Freeze a201-word declarative FRR adaptation and the unmodified source separately. The adaptation omits human-workflow/tool assumptions and is not renamed as the whole framework. Supply context; no neural training or installation claim.
5. Compare empty system, compact and full source for three continuous replies each. Shared mechanical user text has no curiosity/productivity command, task, demonstration, solution or success target. All actual branch history remains; no pruning. Equal reply opportunities are not equal compute cost.
6. Record exact exposure, actual operations, automatic storage, changed paths, errors, and later opportunities. Parsed cells and attempted cells are distinct. No actual error means repair not elicited; same-reply cells are generated before feedback and cannot establish feedback-conditioned model revision.
7. Freeze M0 observations before an adaptive M1 contrast. Supply a truthful initial directory snapshot to all arms, not just FRR. Use the same pre-model supported world, not the three different post-M0 notebooks. Preserve names/form/length/position confounds; no snapshot refresh or implied live observation.
8. Both pilots complete their declared budgets regardless of repetitive or unhelpful text. Do not escalate automatically to more hints, rewards, tasks, sampling changes or longer runs after M1.

## Counterarguments retained

- Three replies do not establish a plateau or all future behavior. M1 is justified by an explicitly identified perception factor, not a proof that M0 cannot act.
- The system prefix and chat mechanics remain causal design choices. Neither empty system nor JSON facts are neutral.
- Automatic notebook growth changes every world hash. That must not be called novel exploration or self-created tooling.
- The compact phrase that cell count can vary is different from the false assertion that its fixed cap is unenforced. A repeated release statement is not automatically a claim about the current interpreter.
- Source papers include larger trained bodies, explicit goals/rewards and extensive scaffolding. They supply contrasts and controls, not a guarantee that FRR prompts create those mechanisms.

## Next design alternatives

The interface role ranks truthful live observation versus initial-only sensing first because it better matches an inhabitant encountering consequences. The baseline role emphasizes testing equivalent explicitly scoped facts before attributing semantic binding failures to the world. These address different uncertainties; neither is a proven remedy. The next blueprint records both with matched local controls and no imposed solution or behavior. Alternative action channels and autonomous world dynamics are later, separate factors.

No voting or apparent convergence is treated as validation of FRR efficacy. Actual model and world records remain the evidence.
