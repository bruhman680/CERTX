## What to observe without turning it into a task

FRR-like words, extra prose, declared uncertainty or paraphrased principles are exposure/lexical outcomes, not evidence of better reasoning. Distinguish complete cells, execution, actual changed files, observed error response, later artifact use and supported consequences. Self-correction or retention may be **not observed or not elicited** when no relevant opportunity occurs; that is not zero ability.

An unchanged repeated response is also an outcome. No prediction is made that the compact text will cause exploration, completion, recovery or improvement. No inference about durable learning follows because no weights change. A3-reply pilot provides a trajectory witness and feasibility information, not a reliable benchmark or a general intelligence increase.

Primary-source scouting also supplies caution: ReAct and Voyager-style results use different bodies, demonstrations/training or substantial curriculum/retrieval/critic scaffolding. Their success does not establish that a bare135M model plus this broad orientation will work. Those components should not be imported silently into this pilot.
