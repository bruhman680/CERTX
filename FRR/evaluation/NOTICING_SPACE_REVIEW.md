## Surviving relationship

A separate space can make a frame change easier to express and preserve. It can also generate performative novelty, automatic opposition, anchoring through repeated retrieval, and task distraction. 'Depressurization' is a useful design metaphor for changing the output/task constraints; no internal pressure field, emotion, or universal relaxation mechanism was measured.

FRR already allows open play without advance justification and rest without invented residuals. The optional module therefore preserves a practice rather than expanding core law. A new noticing can arise within shared context and be useful without being evidentially independent.

## Proposed discriminating comparison

Use independently prepared true/false/ambiguous claims and tasks where either a distant connection helps or is distracting. Cross endorsement/doubt/neutral framing with no-noticing/optional-noticing/compulsory-novelty instructions. Keep evidence, actual task objective, model, tools and budgets matched; report realized extra tokens separately. Freeze rubric and hold out complete task/dependency families. Use both a competent ordinary baseline and the unchanged FRR runtime if comparing FRR efficacy.

Score artifact/task accuracy, useful alternatives, false dissent, unsupported transfers, appropriate silence, preservation of dormant branches, and cost separately. Do not equate number of notes with benefit or require playful associations to demonstrate immediate utility. Distinguish explorational allowance from consequential adoption.
