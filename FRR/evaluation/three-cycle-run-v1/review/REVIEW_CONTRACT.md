# Three-cycle run review contract

Reviewer role: challenge protocol/result alignment and attribution using saved artifacts. No participant-model calls or fresh external sources. Reviewer shares parent context/platform and is not an independent laboratory replication. Review guidance is checked against artifacts, not treated as a semantic truth engine.

Before final audit, inspect:

- Protocol frozen before its run, model/evaluator versions and predicted consequences.
- Draw/resource accounting: outcome0/outcome1, witness calibration, sham, repetitions and discarded/failed attempts; elapsed time where feasible.
- Calibration uncertainty included in inference; rank1 population designs not promoted to full rank by finite-sample witness fluctuations.
- Unresolved/false-attribution/needless-abstention outcomes separated; zero contribution and uncertainty behavior made explicit.
- New stresses chosen after earlier results labeled adaptive development, not sealed confirmation.
- Candidate law versus response-summary identifiability kept distinct.
- Actual code/data agreement; matched marginals do not establish independence; model extension fit alone is not unique mechanism proof.
- Negative/rival outcomes and failures retained rather than overwritten.
- Historical artifacts preserved, current handoff updated without silent scientific queue completion.
- Harness adherence is observed behavior of this run, not evidence of improvement versus no-harness baseline or durable habit formation.
