## What changed in our understanding

The predecessor's successful binding-only readout had a supplied coordinate map. We now separate information preservation, access to the relevant subspace, supervised estimation of that subspace and actual retrieval/status success. An invertible rotation preserves the complete vectors' information. A readout that stays at old coordinates can nevertheless perform badly. A correctly transported readout retains its products and task results. Those are distinct statements.

Two further results matter. Four clean training pairs can suffice for every clean task query here while recovering only four of twelve binding directions. And a geometrically closer estimate can perform worse on the finite task. Perfect answers do not certify complete recovery of the supplied subspace; smaller structural error does not universally order task performance.

## Supervised structure and task performance

The clean m4 estimate has four principal-angle cosines near1: its four fitted directions lie in the true subspace. It still lacks eight of twelve directions. Its normalized full-projector distance is sqrt((12-4)/(2*12))=1/sqrt(3)=.57735. Every clean query nevertheless has joint completion1 for both context ratios and every rotation. Full recovery is not necessary for this finite clean task. With noisy queries, its dense-Q joint drops to.3333 at r=.5 and.0833 at r=2, versus oracle.875 and.28125 respectively.

Clean m12/m48 span the true binding subspace to numerical precision and match oracle task results. This follows the supplied linear model plus observed full training rank; it does not establish twelve examples as a general learning threshold.

## Useful practice and remaining gaps

Before interpreting a change as lost memory, ask what operation moved with the representation. When an encoder/layout changes, a dependent readout may require transport, refitting or reopening; successful historical metrics alone do not establish current compatibility. Where a known invertible transformation exists, test full-information invariance and operation transport before assigning a semantic cause. This is a conditional reusable check, not a new proven general reasoning habit.

Measure at least two targets separately: fidelity to the intended structural quantity, and the actual warranted task outcome. A narrow task may tolerate a large structural gap; a small average geometric discrepancy may affect a consequential distinction. This connects to earlier omitted-clause/status-fidelity work without proving transfer to human/agent reasoning. No core/runtime/schema or queue status is promoted.

The next sharp upstream boundary is the paired-supervision assumption: shared context cancels exactly here. A separately frozen mismatch/anisotropic-noise contact could test whether more pairs reduce sampling error while retaining systematic nuisance. A larger rank sweep is less informative than testing that assumption. The broader memory contact remains prospectively stratified historical/status/criterion queries plus retained-clause fidelity; actual learned-language decompositions and group reasoning remain open.
