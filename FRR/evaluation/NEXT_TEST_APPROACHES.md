## Substrate continuity and future readers

Randomize two earlier histories and independently prepared initial fields. Match their immediate coarse summaries while allowing candidate retained traces to differ. After writer reset, give fresh blinded readers field only, authenticated earlier records only, both, or deliberately mismatched records. Hide treatment labels, seeds, revealing filenames, and expected answers.

Cross these information conditions with field erasure versus sham erasure, passage probe versus no probe, and increasing delays. Compare erasing a mark, removing the travelling driver, and interrupting maintenance: they test different carriers. Vary reader location to distinguish local loss of access from whole-domain disappearance. Include no-event and no-write controls.

Calibrate decoders separately, freeze them, then score newly generated held-out cases. Compare simple readouts and an initial-preparation rival. Measure historical discrimination separately from effects on subsequent actions. A failed reader does not prove no trace exists; a successful reader does not uniquely identify an unknown past.

## Wake restriction and reinforcement

Separate maintained trails, bounded decaying reinforcement, unbounded accumulating reinforcement, and explicit support deletion. Measure escape probability across increasing horizons and admitted interventions; report waiting-time variability. Keep the observation map fixed when measuring a route defect. Changing the macro-model is a separate repair, not evidence that the original defect disappeared.

## Transformer context

Use counterbalanced invented mappings, identical final queries, fresh executions, and held-out examples. Compare donor contexts, neutral replacements, and beginning/middle/end placement. Distinguish cache eviction from deleting tokens and rebuilding every cache entry: later representations may retain earlier contextual influence. Measure answer probabilities, repetition, and execution stability separately. Apply decoding-temperature changes to identical saved logits before studying their indirect effects through newly sampled context.

## Optimizer candidate

Compare a corrected gradient-consistency controller with AdamW and a controller informed by trial-step loss or measured directional curvature, under equal calibration/evaluation budgets. Matched-gradient quadratics expose curvature information absent from the consistency statistic. Reset optimizer moments and parameters independently. Test spurious-feature reliance using held-out distribution interventions; training alignment alone cannot classify harmful persistence.

## FRR usefulness

Use independently authored tasks, fresh model contexts, matched resource budgets, and ordinary instructions versus compact FRR versus full FRR, with a separate checkpoint condition if relevant. Blind evaluators to condition. Score task success, consequential distinction preservation, factual errors, recovery after correction, and cost separately. Do not score FRR vocabulary as success. Freeze prompts after calibration and retain failures alongside gains.
