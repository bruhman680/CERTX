## Cues, information and hidden-answer exposure

All initial reports use the correct object, task and current check, avoiding overt wrong-run cues. Both correct and incorrect claims appear among copied and separate-acquisition cases. Reference agreement, disagreement, unavailability and scope insufficiency occur. The design is marginally balanced but not factorial, with different tasks, units, bounds, numerical margins and availability. No between-case contrast isolates copying, reference information or task complexity.

Inventory labels visibly distinguish derivation, reference and logistics records. Ancillary records expressly lack measurements and authorization. Several source records directly explain their inferential consequence: uncertainty is unbounded, independent acquisitions retain a calibration route, or a mean cannot settle the individual task. Readers therefore use supplied explanations rather than having to discover all these principles. The explicit support_origins output field further elicits provenance accounting. These cues are permissible for the registered measurement and must accompany any success claim.

D + R maximizes measurement and origin information. D + A can nevertheless fully settle AS01/04, and R + A can settle AS01/02/04/05 without revealing original report ancestry. Scoring should distinguish useful selection, achievable completion, origin observability and complete source accounting. Missing origin detail when D was not delivered is not itself an invented-source error. AS03/06 cannot be completed even with all records; withholding there is appropriate, not a reader failure.

The full cases.json contains evaluator-only hidden_truth alongside participant text. Initial strings and selectable records contain no explicit hidden key, intended answer label or condition label. Safe rendering requires sending only initial and the requested frozen record strings with INSTRUCTIONS.json. The protocol requires exactly this. Physical workspace isolation remains instructional, and the coordinator must preserve actual prompts to establish what was delivered. The reviewer has not audited a rendering implementation because none is part of the present files.

All source credibility, coverage and accuracy contracts are fictional premises. Names, agreement, IDs or later hashes do not authenticate them outside the fixture. Independence claims are appropriately limited to stated acquisition/calibration routes, not universal error independence.

## Prospective corrections and remaining gate

The initial protocol omitted an explicit reuse field despite asking for reuse decisions, and HOLD could have been mistaken for precautionary delay under uncertainty. I flagged both before execution. Current instructions and protocol add retained_claim_reuse and define HOLD as established criterion violation; unresolved operational delay remains UNRESOLVED. The original templates/protocol are preserved. No packet was hardened in anticipation of outcomes.

I requested that scoring avoid a summed uncalibrated success score, distinguish unsupported certainty from unobservable confidence, and retain valid completion when an origin-poor pair nevertheless settles the task. The protocol already requires separate dimensions. I inspected KEY.json and SCORING.md, including all eighteen pair actions and associated origin/bound rules. The scorer adopted separate dimensions without a summed score, packet-relative origin observability, and separate completion/access diagnostics. I identified a key explanation that disclosed AS03 reference unavailability when R was not requested, and named L0 when D was absent. The scorer corrected those conditional explanations before freeze without changing gold actions. The corrected AS03 explanations have been checked. No prospective blocker remains.
