# What the probes locate, and what they leave open

The small model's behavior should be separated into questions rather than condensed into a single success/failure label.

1. **Express a command.** Both short LOOK trials and both adaptive short use-A trials did so. The full-context requested trials did not produce an accepted complete command. This locates an elicitation difference in these examples, not a universal capability boundary.
2. **Execute it through the host.** The exact short commands produced actual diagnostic receipts. LOOK observed0; TOUCH A changed each independent copy0→1. No parser repair is indicated by these successful cases.
3. **Predict the consequence.** Not tested. Producing TOUCH A does not show that the model predicted+1.
4. **Infer a rule, revise an error, or transfer it.** Not tested. No trial asked it to infer B, generalize across numbers, or retain discoveries across a sequence.
5. **Select controls during continuing open play.** Not established by the explicit diagnostic requests. Their outputs are not imported into the primary or counted as its discoveries.
6. **Explain the full/short difference.** The short prompt removed history, seeds, charter and archive instructions together. A subsequent controlled ablation could isolate components; this diagnostic cannot attribute the difference solely to length, old memories, distraction, or instruction order.

The initial eight calls and the adaptive two-call follow-up are preserved separately. Two seeds per arm do not supply a stable accuracy estimate. Exact output under matched conditions is reproducibility, not independent confirmation. The original garden continues with the full package unchanged so the diagnostic does not silently turn into an intervention on its history.
