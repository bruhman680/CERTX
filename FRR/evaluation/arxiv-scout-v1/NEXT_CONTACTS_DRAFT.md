# Next contacts after AS-C1 — design draft, not execution

## Priority1: robustness of carrier attribution

Question: which response routes remain distinguishable after changing the soil parameterization and allowing imperfect/off-target resets?

Build a separately coded three-level environmental state with a distinct agent-memory coordinate. Include static hidden-condition, agent-memory, environment-updating and mixed/rival worlds. Avoid tuning a new model just until the preferred signature appears. Declare parameter settings and which passive quantities are matched before running; do not claim full law equivalence from matched low-order summaries.

At equal total sample budget compare repetitions of one leaky reset with a diverse set: agent-target reset, soil-target reset, gate restoration and sham. Include a deliberately confounded setting in which two interventions affect the same combination of coordinates. Calibrate targeted success/off-target effects through specified witnesses in separate data, and include a witness that loses the same access as the main reader. Separate known calibration, estimated calibration uncertainty and unavailable calibration.

Write the actual intervention transition matrices. Derive probe-minus-control differences under those matrices; do not import the earlier additive bias formula unchanged. Include same-reset/no-probe controls and measure baseline changes too. Set observation horizon and scoring/ambiguity tolerances before outcomes.

Before noisy fitting compare candidate full observation laws under each allowed intervention. Return distinguishable candidate sets/equivalence classes when appropriate. Study finite-sample discrimination and conditioning separately from population identification. Report carrier-label errors, justified unresolved cases, needless abstention, witness error and sample/cost budget. No theorem from continuous static causal representation learning is assumed to hold for this temporal discrete model.

Open prerequisites: exact kernels, alternative parameterizations, reserved cases, calibration noise model, statistical decision contract and budget remain to be frozen. This is not a launch-ready preregistration.

## Priority2: fair adaptive prediction

All runnable estimators receive the same observed outcomes and explicit intervention/reset flags. They predict before each subsequent natural outcome; imposed labels are not silently scored as natural evidence. Compare fixed EWMA, a finite-window baseline and a suitable history filter. Mark model/parameter-known references separately from learning algorithms; the privileged state oracle is only a separate ceiling.

Cross stationary hidden-state dynamics with actual external parameter changes, and state-changing probes with marked forced samples that do not alter state. Propagate forced actions through the action kernel rather than conditioning on a naturally emitted error. Include a deliberately misspecified change-detection baseline if useful, with its assumptions visible.

Measure log/Brier loss, regret to the history-aware conditional law, false alarms, local recovery and cost. Decompose hidden-state oracle advantage separately. Do not reduce all outcomes to one mean score or infer adaptation quality from the supplied latent-oracle excess.

Open prerequisites: exact algorithm variants, information contracts, tuning/evaluation separation, shifts and false-alarm definitions. No alpha/hazard/threshold chosen on held-out results.

## Practical bridge: source exposure and selective repair

Proposed small authored pilot: four independently checked fictional tool tasks × four note conditions, crossing clean/stale contents with exposed/neutral length-matched context slots. Keep authoritative tool state, task, access policy and output requirements fixed. Preserve note-generation and rendering details, including which content was replaced to maintain context budget. Random assignment must actually allow both exposure states for the relevant note/query; log assignments and source access.

Measure task-grounded action/artifact correctness and uncertainty. Treat exposure, claimed use, actual source-dependent behavior and final success as separate. Citations alone do not establish causal use; outcome contrasts need valid assignment/access controls. Evaluate repair in the exposed conditions and test an unrelated benign fact in a separate continuation; deletion alone is insufficient.

One reader per authored cell would be an exploratory pilot, not a robust efficacy comparison. Freeze the exact tasks, independent scoring, allowed tools, model/runtime access and budget first. No participant calls have been made for this design. User-authorized scouting does not make a draft into a completed study.
