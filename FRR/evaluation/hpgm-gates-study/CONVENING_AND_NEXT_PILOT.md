# HG1 design convening and bounded next pilot

Status: design/instrument preparation; not a preregistered behavioral launch. Original PROTOCOL_DRAFT.md and authored fixture hashes remain unchanged. This record adds a proposed narrower scope rather than retroactively satisfying that draft's independent-family requirement.

The user explicitly requested six agents. Six design reviews covered scoring, reader evidence, difficulty, arm comparisons, access/cost, and research lineage. Reviews shared context, tools and filesystem; convergence is a design agreement, not six independent confirmations. No review agent is counted as an experimental actor or reader.

## Decisions and problems exposed

Separate objective correctness, packet-supported reasoning, and end-to-end completion. Preserve sufficient transformed information rather than require raw data copying. Test the scorer against missing evidence, lucky guesses, source corruption, internal conflict, stale conclusions and unsupported additions. A correct number must not erase an unsupported causal or execution claim.

The elementary fixtures may ceiling on writer accuracy, and 180 words can retain their inputs comfortably. They are useful workflow smoke tests, not a strong compression challenge. Two variants in a family are correlated. All are exposed and parent-authored.

The functional/HPGM operations match after heading removal. Their contrast tests the particular named headings, including associations and tokenization. Functional versus generic continuation tests the full specified instruction package, including extra text. Neither identifies general FRR efficacy or a physical oscillator. Ties and reversals are admissible outcomes.

Two actor calls must share the same declared transcript; the reader receives only the final handoff, update and frozen common rules. Common structured output, whitespace word counting, heading normalization, invalid-output policy and access accounting must be fixed before launch. Preserve raw and normalized outputs; do not silently truncate oversized handoffs or retry weak answers.

Current collaboration can supply new contexts but shares filesystem/tools and does not expose a reliable hard output-token cap or actual token/dollar receipt. Requested limits are not enforced limits, and equal calls are not equal compute. A future launch must disclose these limitations or use a stronger runner. This is not a blocker to instrument preparation.

## Proposed subsequent authored smoke pilot

One variant from each of four families, four arms, one replicate: 16 writer trajectories, two writer stages plus one fresh reader each, at most 48 completions. This is a proposal, not consumed budget. Preserve failures and count all attempted calls. Do not pick a winner or promote to confirmation from these development results.

Before execution freeze case allocation, model/settings, exact prompts/packet construction, source-aware scoring, order/randomization, requested/enforced limits, access disclosure and stopping rules in a new launch manifest. Retain the old larger draft. The current instrument pass tests scoring plumbing only, not free-text understanding or arm effectiveness.
