# HG1 fixture readiness: concrete preparation, not a behavioral result

Eight development fixtures and separate answer keys/reader updates are now generated by [prepare_hg1_fixtures.py](../../tools/prepare_hg1_fixtures.py). The four families cover weighted comparisons under a changed target mix, integer-time resource scheduling, identifiable versus underdetermined linear quantities, and probability forecast scores under updated labels. Each includes a continuation update so a future reader must use retained information appropriately.

[development-fixtures/MANIFEST.json](development-fixtures/MANIFEST.json) pins all packet, key, update and arm-template files. The named condition templates have the same functional operations after removing stage headings; labels are the intended difference between those two arms. Common competent instructions and requested handoff limits are present throughout. Raw task data and expected answers are separate files.

These fixtures are authored by the same parent system that designed the comparison. They are development/software examples, with possible ceiling effects, not independent task authorship and not untouched confirmation. All eight are exposed in this repository. They do not meet the draft's independently prepared task-family requirement, and none is silently assigned to a confirmation split. Keys on a shared/public filesystem are physically accessible unless a future runner provides stronger isolation; separation of files alone is not blinding.

The scheduling keys are computed by exhaustive search in the explicitly defined integer-time domain, with expected optima checked; the probability keys use rational arithmetic. Reader evaluation must distinguish a handoff omission from an unwarranted guess: an appropriate statement that required facts were omitted is different from falsely asserting a value. A reference continuation answer assumes the needed original facts survived; do not judge missing evidence against an omniscient key without inspecting the actual reader packet.

Remaining before a prospective HG1 run: separately prepared development/confirmation families or a clearly amended authored-pilot scope; review task difficulty; finalize prompts, parser and source-aware scoring; set actual model/settings and cost instrumentation; enforce or document access and output limits; freeze the launch protocol and hashes. The existing draft is retained unchanged. No actor or reader responses were collected by preparation.

[Reader packet contract](READER_PACKET_CONTRACT.md) documents actual-evidence and sufficient-representation requirements. [Scoring contract](SCORING_CONTRACT.md) and SCORING_CHECKS.json exercise exact structured LINEAR-2 equality records only; they do not extract natural-language handoffs, score other families or constitute actor outcomes.
