## What the world did

The changing law pairs adjacent bytes (a_i,b_i) and simultaneously updates them to (b_i, a_i XOR b_(i-1) XOR b_(i+1)), with periodic neighbors and any odd trailing byte unchanged. Static uses the identity update. There is one separate physical tick attempt after each completed reply. Nothing advances during a pause. Ordinary Python and shell cells remain the actuator; prose is not automatically written into the world.

Deletion, symlink/directory skip, hardlink alias effects and actual permission errors were declared in the model-facing laws and previously tested in disposable fixtures. Those features were not exercised by the model in this pilot. All six tick receipts returned exit code0. The actual snapshots establish three world-only material changes in changing, and none in static. No actor-generated source was executed. The actual histories and full model replies remain external raw records; the repetitive prose was not appended to a notebook file.

This linear material is still a modest test substrate. The earlier mechanics study found62-tick returns for two particular unmodified31-pair states. Three ticks here do not alter that finding or establish sustained novelty.

## Interpretation and next strongest direction

**High confidence:** the model was actually run, the new world/sensor/tick coupling worked in these cases, outputs repeated as described, no actor action occurred, weights stayed fixed under the implemented digest checks, and reference histories remain intact.

**Unestablished:** self-directed exploration, contextual learning, FRR efficacy, causal benefit of motion, an internal developmental phase, or that a longer block would break the repetition. Output loops do not establish subjective state or the topology of hidden reasoning. A successful physical tick is environmental change, not a learning update.

The next strongest narrow uncertainty is whether deterministic greedy decoding contributes to the repeated output. A detached decoder comparison could hold exact initial world/context/body/FRR fixed and compare greedy with a predeclared seeded sampling policy, without prescribing any behavior or modifying these preserved pilots. This is a proposed diagnostic, not an established remedy or an activated change. Less repetition or a sampled command would still not by itself demonstrate learning or improved reasoning. No more source/world changes or model calls are queued.

Garden translation: the soil now changes honestly and the gardener receives that change. In this short season, the gardener keeps reciting a description rather than working the soil. We have recorded that distinction without making the recitation count as cultivation.
