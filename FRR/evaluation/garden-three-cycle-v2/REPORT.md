## Cycle1 — what a witness fails to show

[Cycle1](cycle1/REPORT.md) tests216 finite observation models with survival S and a readability gate G. A zero-coded readout S*G can conflate unreadable information with absent information. Explicit values1,0,missing recover that distinction in some examples. However, state-dependent readability still permits different survival rates to produce identical ternary observation laws.

For readable-survivor probability p1 and readable-nonsurvivor probability p0, unknown gate selection permits survival v anywhere in[p1,1-p0]. The bound is sharp in the model: every point is attainable. The example law[.2,.2,.6] permits v in[.2,.8]. Assuming state-independent readability with positive access identifies v through the conditional readable mean; the same visible law does not establish that assumption.

Two restoration operators open the gate. One preserves S and reveals its prior survival; the other rewrites survival to.6 and reveals that new value. Successful reading after a procedure does not by itself establish what survived before it. ## Cycle3 — what changes in our practice

[Cycle3](cycle3/REPORT.md) convenes the [review](review/REPORT.md), scoped source weave and documentation refinement. We retain missingness, selective exposure, restoration side effects, compatible-state sets, explicit decision criteria and observation costs as separate considerations. The [verification layer](../../modules/verification-layer.md) receives a small optional addition; no core prompt/schema is changed.

Our useful movement is from “which story is exactly true?” toward two linked questions: “what remains compatible with the evidence?” and “does that uncertainty change the next permitted action?” Neither question replaces the other. A good action does not certify a causal story, and an unresolved mechanism need not force every action to stop.

The work demonstrates these particular calculations and recorded practices. There is no objective of proving FRR superior, and no actual-agent efficacy, physical-memory law or universally best decision rule follows. The author-chosen costs are a visible boundary, not a discovered moral or engineering constant.
