# Useful noticings that remain questions

**Metadata can become material for a false world description.** In the normal-scaffold filler trial at seed120507, the model wrote current number438and invalid bounds−8to−8. The authoritative world value was0with bounds−8to8. The retained archive banner contained438as the start of the recent-entry range. The matching number suggests a possible field-binding confusion, but one match does not identify its cause. A later controlled metadata-label/number substitution could test that explanation without modifying primary history.

**A success sentence can accompany no action.** The padded-world-only trial at seed120505 asserted successful delivery. The actual receipt was a nonexecuted record. This supports retaining separate expression, execution and consequence fields; it does not require treating the statement as intentional deception or suppressing the failed text.

**Removing visible history is different from erasing history.** The no-history probes changed only their rendering. All450external entries and every primary byte were retained. Future view-design tests can preserve a complete archive while varying what appears in the active field of attention.

**A compact instruction success is only one layer.** TOUCH A expression and copy0→1execution are observed. Prediction, rule discovery, transfer, self-correction and spontaneous control selection remain untested. Improving elicitation must not silently become a claim of those abilities.

These are future leads, not additional completed experiments. Current primary rules/view were left unchanged. The component and filler results are limited to this snapshot, these prompt constructions and three seeds per arm.
