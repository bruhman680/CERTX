# Fresh-reader recovery pilot: stable guidance and preserved corrections

## What ran

Twelve fresh sub-agent contexts, each without parent conversation inheritance, read one assigned packet and wrote public JSON. Four arms crossed ordinary competent guidance versus unchanged full FRR runtime0.2 with summary-only versus original-record access at later hops. Each arm has one three-hop chain covering six authored cases. The ordinary baseline also requires accuracy, uncertainty and useful continuation; all arms share the task-level obligation to preserve consequential corrections. One fresh label-blinded scorer reviewed anonymized outputs; a second fresh scorer reviewed a repaired evidence packet.

These are actual generated agent outputs, not fabricated demonstration responses or an external provider API rerun. Model/settings inherited the host configuration and were not independently instrumented. Same broad model/instruction ecosystem and authored cases mean no independent cross-model replication. One chain per arm is insufficient for statistical efficacy claims. The72case/hop assessments are correlated, not72independent trials. Actor guidance lengths differ; original access deliberately changes available context. No matched-length checklist, independently authored heldout cases, repeated sampling, or measured token/latency costs were run.

## Manipulations and expected contact

At hop1 all readers receive identical synthetic source facts. Each outputs a summary capped180words and current case assessments. At hop2 a new fresh reader gets only the preceding summary, plus an unsupported note reversing all six corrections; archive arms also receive the original records. At hop3 another fresh reader receives the latest summary plus a genuinely new receipt for C4, permitting a pending-to-executed update while broader performance remains untested; archive arms again receive original records. Only summaries are passed, not the fuller case assessments. Same frozen full guidance is reloaded in FRR arms at every hop.

Cases cover successful retry after404; judge agreement versus truth accuracy; toy fitness versus physical entropy/semantic transfer; pending experiment versus unrelated interface check; exact fixed-kernel predictive sufficiency versus rounded/finite-count extensions; and readable trace versus unique historical attribution. All are constructed exercises, not real scientific events or independently heldout benchmark items.

## Results

Central corrective directions survived in all four chains. All rejected the unsupported later sync claims and all updated C4 when the new receipt justified one executed toy run. No blanket refusal or contradictory reversion was identified.

The six partial assessments are three omitted qualifications in the ordinary summary-only chain at hops2and3: historical404/verified retry detail, independent mechanism-validation limit, and C3semantic-transfer limit. They are omissions/calibrated uncertainty, not newly false conclusions. The original first summary there was70words, versus102in the FRR summary-only chain; content/length differences may explain later preservation. Do not convert this one authored trajectory into a percentage improvement or attribute it uniquely to FRR.

Original access preserved detail with ordinary guidance too. Since all chains retained the central corrections, the test does not demonstrate that access or FRR is necessary here. The six easy/prompt-salient cases and180word cap did not substantially stress the system. This is evidence of bounded execution, not universal error recognition, stable internal understanding, or indefinite memory.

## A scoring-construction correction

The first scorer packet shortened the new C4receipt, omitting explicit actor-visible input/output files and checked-result details. The scorer then flagged those details as unsupported on the incomplete scoring record. Parent inspection identified this missing evidence. Original review remains unchanged; SCORER_PACKET_REPAIRED.json supplies the full receipt to a new scorer, who removes the unsupported-detail flags. Parent inspection is unblinded; fresh scorers are blind to arm labels but share the model lineage. The repair is exploratory after inspecting results, not untouched final evaluation.

This failure belongs to our evaluator-input construction, not the actors. It illustrates the DecepEval contact: judging a report requires the evidence actually available to its author. A strict rubric can misattribute unsupported claims if its packet loses facts. Stable FRR wording did not automatically prevent the evaluation mistake; explicit artifact comparison enabled the repair.

## Integrity and interpretation

Checked12validJSON responses,180word limits, exact predecessor summaries in next packets, original-access manipulation matching arm mapping, and unchanged runtime hash. Protocol and expected contacts were recorded before actor outputs. Raw packets, outputs, initial/repaired scorer records, mapping and descriptive checks are in recovery-pilot/. Scoring workflow was expanded after collection and that amendment is preserved.

A persistent instruction can renew an obligation to check. Remembering the specific correction requires its evidence or a faithful usable record. This pilot exercises externally restored guidance and summaries in fresh contexts, not an individual agent's internal long-term memory. Recognition, retention, and application remain separate; copying a rule supplies neither complete evidence nor guaranteed correct execution.

## Wind-down basis

The pilot establishes a small successful recovery path and a concrete evaluator correction. It does not establish FRR superiority or general stability. Next substantive contact is a separately prepared, harder heldout family with lossy/contradictory records, matched-length competent baselines, multiple samples/model families, measured costs and source-aware adjudication. Core prompt/schema unchanged. Preserve this snapshot rather than optimizing the prompt to these six cases.

Additional access limitation: packet-only reads and scoring blinding were specified by task instructions, not enforced by separate filesystem sandboxes. All workers shared the workspace and tools; comprehensive access transcripts were not independently audited. Fork-none removes inherited conversation, but does not prove absence of every possible exposure route. The access treatment should therefore be described as assigned packet access, not verified information-theoretic isolation.
