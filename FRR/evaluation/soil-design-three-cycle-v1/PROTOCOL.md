# SD-C1/2/3: sampling design, rival soil, and reset calibration

Frozen questions before new numerical results; motivated by CO-C1, not independent confirmation. Local exact-distribution calculations, no agents/model inference/training/paid resources. Coordinator review only.

Cycle 1: Fix 64 Bernoulli observations. Partition them into k independent soils, n observations/soil, kn=64, n in 1,2,4,8,16,32,64. Each soil rate P=.2 +/- sqrt(.032), equally likely. Enumerate the total-count distribution by convolution. Compare exact variance with .032/k+.128/64 and with naive iid .16/64. Evaluate coverage of the fixed interval mean +/- 1.96 sqrt(.16/64) for the population mean .2 (not a confidence procedure optimized for this mixture). Expect less independent soil sampling to worsen population-mean precision. This is a fixed-budget design result, not a universal recommendation to reset more: reset cost is omitted.

Cycle 2: Add a genuinely different latent-rate distribution: Beta(.8,3.2), held fixed per soil. It matches .2 mean and .032 variance but is continuous, versus the two-point soil. Compute exact count distributions using beta moments at n=1,2,3,4,8,16,32,64; total variation (TV) and equal-prior one-soil Bayes risk=(1-TV)/2. Counts suffice for these exchangeable models only. Predict equality through n=2 and separation at n>=3; do not promote moment matching to mechanism identification. Check four independently sampled 16-observation soils versus one 64-observation soil by finite log-likelihood convolution/distribution enumeration if tractable; otherwise leave that comparison open. The primary required comparison is the n grid.

Cycle 3: Hidden persistent propensity can be stored externally or internally with identical passive output laws. Define a per-run carrier: internal with probability pi, external otherwise, and independently a +/- soil sign. After observing X1=1, resample the relevant propensity to a fresh fair sign with probability q_A for agent reset, q_S for soil reset. Irrelevant reset has no effect, and reset has no direct emission effect. Enumerate joint X1,X2 probabilities under no/agent/soil resets. Expected excesses over .2 are .16, .16(1-pi*q_A), .16(1-(1-pi)*q_S). With known nonzero q, recover pi. With unknown q in [0,1], retain pi interval [a,1-s], where a=1-D_A/D_0 and s=1-D_S/D_0. Compare (pi,q_A,q_S)=(.4,.5,.75),(.25,.8,.6),(.5,.4,.9): predict identical three readouts, different allocations. Zero reset efficacy must leave allocation unidentified. These are observing-condition effects, not do(X1=1) effects. No wake mechanism or actual-agent attribution is claimed.

Validation: probability normalization/nonnegativity, variance versus analytic formula, explicit binary-word enumeration for small lengths, independently summed two-event reset distribution and forward identities, prescribed rival witnesses. Float tolerance 1e-11 (not interval certification). Freeze code hashes before running. Preserve protocol failures or deviations; make only warranted optional guidance refinements after results. No old findings relabeled.
