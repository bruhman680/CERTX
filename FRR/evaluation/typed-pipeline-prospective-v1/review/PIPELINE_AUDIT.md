The model-generated measurement responses repeat/copy full records rather than emit requested lists. A human role-aware reading recovers all seven distinct source observation sets: criterion numbers remain explicitly requirements, and natural 'no measurement' clauses remain explicit absence. The simpler surface parser treats requirement quantities as measurements and requires a literal MISSING token for absence. ## Scope and next boundary

No blocking saved-data arithmetic or action-gating defect found. The run demonstrates a specific semantic interface failure: permissive quantity harvesting can erase the roles that a human can still read in the generated text, and a formally correct checker cannot restore them. A future role-aware or stricter syntax representation needs a new prospective test, not retrospective prompt tuning or silent repair of these frozen outcomes. The current copied-note source assertions, failed direct answers, caps and parser limits must remain preserved.
