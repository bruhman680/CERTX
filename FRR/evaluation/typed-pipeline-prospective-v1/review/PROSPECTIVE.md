## Discriminating purpose

The preceding retrospective replay showed that exact arithmetic can improve a label while leaving generated facts wrong. Freeze the complete extraction/parser/comparator algorithm before new outputs, so the new contact is a prospective pipeline assessment. Keep a one-query direct reference and two-query extraction-plus-computation workflow separately costed; no equal-budget causal improvement claim. All generations are from one model and seven exposed authored records; neither 21 independent models nor held-out population efficacy follows.

The seven proposed scenarios are missing, below, equal, above, distinct crossing values, distinct values both above, and missing+measured disagreement. Explicitly state the task policy requiring one consistent recorded measurement, and UNKNOWN for disagreement without aggregation/precedence even when all numerical alternatives lie on the same side. The same-side conflict gold is a policy outcome, not logically forced by a threshold alone. The criterion is known even when evidence is absent or inconsistent.

## Preserve what could falsify the pipeline

The observation extraction prompt and parser must permit all supplied observations, including BOTH an explicit missing claim and a measured value in the mixed-disagreement case. Otherwise perfect compliance cannot express that source state. Define exact accepted serialization prospectively and do not repair omitted/incorrect generated observations from original keys. A parser that accepts simple wrappers is a surface parser, not an entailment or ownership checker; reject unsupported numeric/sign/unit forms rather than silently changing them.

Run-to-question association may supply entity and unit as caller context. Numeric minimum and observation list must come only from model outputs. Hashes bind generated structured claims to bytes, not to source truth. The gold original source, human extraction judgments and original numeric values may be used only after action selection for evaluation. No false-fact gating, dropped hard cases, hidden rerouting or retries. Missing extraction and parser/access failure can lead to the same UNKNOWN action but do not have the same reason/warrant.

## Separate endpoints

Report syntax compliance, semantic extraction fidelity (including completeness of all observation states/values), source-field binding, direct decision match, computed action match, extracted fact warrant and reason warrant. A wrong reason producing the keyed UNKNOWN is not a correct detection of missingness/conflict. Likewise unsupported numeric fields can produce a correct label accidentally. Keep parse failures and all seven records in the denominator. Distinguish a formally justified infrastructure withholding from a source-justified task conclusion.

For conflict preservation, require all distinct states/values from the source under the declared no-precedence policy; duplication of a value is not new independent evidence. A record with two different values must not be silently reduced to the latest/first/min/max. Missing+measured must not be silently simplified to one of its observations.
