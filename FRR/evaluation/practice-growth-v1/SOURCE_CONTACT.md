# Source weave: growth through consequential inquiry

[Platt, Strong Inference (1964)](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~markhill/science64_strong_inference.pdf): source scout read operational alternatives/experiment/recycle sequence, notebook practice and closing self-analysis sections; parent opened the transcription and read its operational sequence. Useful for selecting an observation that distinguishes plausible alternatives. The hosted copy warns transcription punctuation may differ from the original. Its sweeping historical efficacy rhetoric is not adopted. The user's aim is our useful practice, not judging other labs or outperforming people.

[Russell, Metareasoning (1997)](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~russell/papers/mitecs-metareasoning.pdf): source scout read the seven-page overview, especially information/computation benefit, cost and stopping. Already used in earlier FRR work; this is an operational rereading, not independent confirmation. Qualitative question: could another check change the warranted next action? We have no calibrated utility/information-gain estimator and do not invent one. A full Argyris article was not read; only an opening excerpt was accessible, so it is not substantive evidence here.

[NIST, tolerance intervals](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc263.htm): parent read official page. It distinguishes a confidence interval for a parameter such as a mean, statistical tolerance limits for population coverage, and engineering acceptance limits. Our residual audit uses descriptive finite-data quantities, not a fitted normal tolerance interval or an empirical guarantee for future readings. Source data's alternating signed component also means the signed residuals are not simply an identically distributed normal sample. We do not silently invoke that assumption.

Practice synthesis, not a newly established universal algorithm: choose a consequential uncertainty; name alternatives; seek a discriminating contact; act on what changed; stop or reopen when another contact could matter. Premise status matters: stipulated, derived, observed within the available setup, inherited, or independently checked. A meaningful uncertainty can remain even while an action is already warranted.
