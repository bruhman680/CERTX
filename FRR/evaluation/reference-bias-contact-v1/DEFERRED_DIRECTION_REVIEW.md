# Deferred lateral direction: source ancestry before corroboration

This is a read-only review of existing saved contacts plus an unexecuted design proposal. No new participants, web searches, outcomes or queue updates were introduced. This review is a separate same-platform inspection with project context, not blinded assessment or independent empirical replication.

The strongest lateral next contact is **whether actual readers distinguish several agreeing descendants of one source from separately warranted evidence when deciding whether a retained claim can be reused**. Make the source-selection problem less overt than the earlier wrong-run/version fixtures. This targets a missing behavioral link between the shared-bias witness result and real handoff use. It does not require another compression-budget comparison or group-discussion study first.

## A feasible discriminating contact, proposed only

Use a small fictional operational ledger, an already-written retained claim and several concise reports that agree. Hide the convenient “wrong version” cue: all reports should name the correct task/object and look locally plausible. Supply inspectable generation/derivation records from which common ancestry can be established, rather than labeling a packet “copied” or “independent.” A separate reference channel measures the task-relevant quantity using a declared, reviewable calibration/coverage contract. References are not authoritative merely because their prose says “independent.”

Freeze a balanced set before readers: retained claim true/false crossed with (a) three descendants of one observation or (b) separately generated observations; include reference-agrees, reference-disagrees and reference-unavailable cases. Keep both true and false common-source cases so “shared ancestry means false” cannot solve the test. Include at least one case where the reference's scope cannot settle the requested decision, so “reference always wins” cannot solve it either. For a genuinely small contact, use fewer prospectively selected cases rather than silently expanding this factorial into many calls.

One useful two-turn design gives every reader the same initial handoff and report inventory, permits a fixed number of named record requests, then delivers only the frozen requested records. Readers return a public decision, evidence IDs, unresolved issue and next requested check. Do not request private reasoning. A no-reference arm identifies the limitation imposed by unavailable evidence; it is deliberately information-poor and cannot estimate a guidance benefit.

If comparing a lineage-check prompt with ordinary guidance, give those two arms identical selectable evidence, response budgets and lookup rights; randomize assignment and presentation order. Report that contrast separately from the reference-availability manipulation. Alternatively, omit guidance arms entirely and treat the first contact as source-selection measurement. That is the nearer, lower-cost question after the earlier ceiling ties.

Predeclare packet-relative warrant and externally keyed task correctness separately. Score whether the reader chooses an actually discriminating lookup, binds evidence to the relevant scope, avoids counting descendants as additional independent observations, completes correctly when evidence suffices, and stays unresolved when it does not. Count lookups, supplied record bytes and participant turns; report unavailable token/compute costs honestly. Separate answer correctness from claim-level fidelity and unsupported justification. A correct answer with invented source independence is not fully grounded success.

Keep source preparation, task/gold preparation and prospective review separate where feasible; freeze exact packets, key, source-lineage graph, reference contract, selection rule and scoring before outputs. Preserve failed launch attempts, cap violations, missing records and evaluator repairs. No outcome-conditioned added questions or retries. A paired information audit must check that wording/order/length does not accidentally disclose the key. Same-platform role separation adds scrutiny, not independent scientific replication.

## Confounds and interpretation limits

- A separately generated reference can still share upstream calibration, incentives, tools or data. In the fixture, independence must be justified by the declared generation mechanism and limited to the error route being varied. Do not infer universal independence from different names or different agents.
- Detailed ancestry metadata is itself extra evidence and a cue. Success would establish behavior when that metadata is inspectable, not autonomous discovery of hidden provenance. Equalize metadata volume where possible and disclose the remaining difference.
- A reference that contradicts a handoff creates an easy conflict cue. Include agreeing and unavailable/scope-insufficient controls; avoid presenting only “three bad reports versus one good reference.”
- Inspectable ancestry establishes dependence, not falsity. A false source may be faithfully copied; a correct source may also have many copies. Evaluate content truth and corroboration warrant separately.
- A reference channel needs an explicit task envelope. Calibration for a mean, a different location or an earlier time may not settle a pointwise current claim. This connects to PG-C1 and avoids substituting source prestige for entailment.
- Artificially removing evidence changes achievable completion. Appropriate abstention under that condition is not a failed reader, and more completion after supplying decisive information is not evidence of a better reasoning framework.
- Few authored cases, shared model ancestry, instructional workspace isolation and unblinded coordinating review limit generalization. Actual writer-produced omission, long-term reuse, natural authentication and peer-discussion efficacy remain later questions.

An additional noticing: **a correctable bias and a detectable contradiction are different resources**. A reference may show that the old handoff is unsafe without supplying enough information to reconstruct the right value. Register “reject reuse / request further evidence” separately from “correct and complete.” Otherwise the study would repeat the old mistake of collapsing warranted uncertainty, repaired completion and agreement into one score.

No implementation, protocol registration, participant execution or status promotion is implied by this review. The earlier contamination example and overt wrong-run cases remain development history, not new held-out cases.
