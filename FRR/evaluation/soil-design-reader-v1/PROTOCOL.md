# SR-C1: prospective reader decomposition

30 October 2026 UTC. Follow-up to SD-C1/2/3. Eight newly authored records, three fresh independent chat contexts per record: 24 greedy generations from the pinned local SmolLM2-135M-Instruct CPU model. No FRR-versus-baseline causal claim, prompt optimization, retries, training, paid services, external datasets, or deletion of old caches. All records, questions, answer keys, scoring rules and runner are frozen before inference. These are constructed diagnostic fixtures, not held-out population data or independent confirmation of FRR.

Four grouping records preserve the same 64 observations and total/conditional variances, varying independent soil draws: 64, 4, 1, unrecorded. Ask separately for k (reading), population-mean variance (arithmetic under the supplied formula), and whether iid observations are warranted (YES/NO/UNKNOWN under a supplied criterion). UNKNOWN means the record leaves the needed fact undetermined. The variance target is the population mean, not the conditional rate of one particular soil.

Four reset records share the supplied no-cross-effect carrier model. Ask separately whether both reset-effectiveness values are supplied, the feasible interval for the internal-carrier run fraction pi, and whether pi is point-identified. Include provided imperfect efficacies, missing efficacies with nondegenerate identified interval, provided zero efficacies, and missing efficacies with a singleton feasible set. Thus neither PROVIDED nor MISSING alone determines the final point-identification label. These equations concern observations conditioned on X1=1 followed by an intervention; they are not do(X1=1) responses.

Use 64-new-token cap, fresh prompt cache per call, float32 eager attention, two CPU threads, eval/inference mode, greedy argmax, EOS stop. Preserve every response and actual terminal cache in a new state directory. Last emitted token need not have been forwarded into cache; record prefix length and token IDs. Input/generation times exclude serialization and model loading. No continuation or retry after a weak answer. If execution fails, preserve partial output and report the stopping point before considering a new run.

Primary scoring: semantic answer match versus frozen exact key, judged over the entire saved response. Report strict output-format adherence separately. A response that asserts the right value plus a contradictory relevant value is not warranted; unsupported added factual/material claims also prevent a full-warrant mark. Short numeric answers can supply a warranted answer without a narrative derivation; do not claim internal reasoning correctness from them. A conclusion-only label match does not certify agreement with the intended calculation or source reading. Record explicit unsupported point claims, invented sampling/calibration facts, and output stops. Responses without a readable answer are unresolved, not automatically semantic negatives. All manual semantic review is coordinator self-review, not independent/masked scoring.

Separate source-reading, calculation/set reconstruction, and conclusion counts (8 each); provide per-record triples. A 3-of-3 record means three separately elicited compatible answers, not a demonstrated internal pipeline or a single consistent generated report. No automatic routing, confidence score, role reset or new general parser follows from the counts. Freeze model/cache preservation receipts before generation and raw outputs before scoring. Check local old files, model and cache bytes unchanged afterward.
