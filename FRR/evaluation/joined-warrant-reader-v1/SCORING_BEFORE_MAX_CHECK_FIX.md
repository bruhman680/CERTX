# Prospective masked scoring

The acceptance proposition is supported, contradicted or unresolved under the supplied fictional packet. A cautious action alone does not establish a correct evidential verdict. In particular, unresolved acceptance warrants withholding release pending evidence; it does not establish that a load violates the criterion.

The scorer reads only neutral case/response packets and the prospective key. Do not read instruction-arm text, assignment mapping, participant prompts, or unmasked comparisons during scoring. Case identifiers map to the key; administrative pairing metadata is not itself evidence. Preserve KEY_INITIAL.json and this initial rubric before any correction. Record corrections with reasons and source hashes; never silently replace the initial answer.

Score each response separately on these dimensions, retaining a short quotation or precise evidence reference. Use full, partial, incorrect, omitted, or not_applicable. Do not compute an aggregate pass rate or a composite score. Report errors and omissions separately. Concise accurate responses can earn full credit; verbosity and an exhaustive audit of obvious premises are not required.

1. **Acceptance verdict.** Full: correct evidential status, allowing equivalent ordinary-language wording. Partial: materially ambiguous between unresolved and known violation. Incorrect: asserts support or contradiction when the packet does not warrant it. A simple do-not-release on an unresolved case can be correct action but only partial verdict if the explanation does not clarify its unresolved basis.
2. **Action.** Full: release on cases 01, 07; reject release on cases03, 05; withhold release pending relevant evidence on cases02, 04, 06, 08. Incorrect: releases an unresolved or contradicted lot, or unnecessarily withholds a supported lot. Preserve any correct verdict separately from action mistakes.
3. **Numerical consequence.** Full: stated consequential calculations/bounds are correct and sufficient to substantiate the answer; not every bound in KEY.json must be recited. On unresolved cases, two feasible contrasting values, a sharp task-relevant bound, or an equivalent correct threshold derivation can establish uncertainty. Partial: correct useful arithmetic but incomplete task linkage or a minor error that does not alter conclusion. Incorrect: consequential wrong arithmetic or an unsupported numerical guarantee. Omitted: no numerical consequence when one is needed to justify the judgment. Do not require a full feasible interval unless the response claims one. Correct but loose bounds are partial only when the task explicitly requests the sharp bound; otherwise score their valid consequence.
4. **Supplied-premise fidelity.** Full: correctly uses the consequential supplied premise(s), without falsely calling any applicable premise absent. Cases01/05 centrally require recognizing assignment supplied by the joined records; case03 requires recognizing the exact matching calibration; case07 requires recognizing the full-population certificate. In unresolved cases do not require reciting unrelated satisfied premises. Partial: recognizes a relevant join but describes it inconsistently or ambiguously. Omitted: no evidence basis to assess. Mark explicit false-missingness separately in a boolean flag, with quotation.

An unnecessary request made as a prerequisite to a supported answer is incorrect. An optional confirmation clearly labeled unnecessary is not penalized. Do not infer that a suggested targeted check guarantees success unless the response says or clearly implies so.
