The repaired preparation rule is independently elementary. Four nonnegative loads total28 with limit8. Any two observed compliant values total at most16, leaving residual at least12; concentrating it into one unseen component gives a violating compatible state. Therefore two exact individual observations cannot certify compliance. Three determine the fourth and settle the task through that observation channel. Fewer can expose violation, including an observed value<4 that forces the other three to total>24. An apparently helpful sufficient condition “residual total<=8” is unattainable after just two compliant measurements here.
