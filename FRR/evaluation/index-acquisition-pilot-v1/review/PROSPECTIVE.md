## Descriptions and parity

The metadata provides strong, intentional retrieval guidance: D04 identifies corrected cohort/exclusions, D05 a future dawn study, M02 excerpt permission, and M04 complete-release conditions. These cues encode topical relevance and epistemic stage; they do not disclose counts, comparative direction, authorization status, or a measured subgroup outcome. The index rule also explicitly says metadata is not measurement or authorization evidence. No direct answer leak was found under this intended retrieval manipulation. These are manually source-informed labels, so resulting retrieval success cannot be attributed to an equal-information format advantage.
