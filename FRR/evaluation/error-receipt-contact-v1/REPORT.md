## Confidence gained and questions still open

High confidence in the narrow artifact/effect result: changing two receipt-field names with equal total input tokens changes whether a complete program is emitted in one exact post-error context. The same intervention leaves the other exact context unchanged. We have now connected a presentation intervention to an actual action/no-action difference, rather than only fictional-file wording.

This is not operational improvement: successful retrieval remains0/4. Equal input length rules out a total-length-only explanation for these contrasts, but label vocabulary/associations, token identity and continuation cues remain the treatment. Two failure states and one reply each cannot establish general repair capability, universal schema sensitivity or an internal mechanism.

We still do not know whether the repeated failed source anchors the continuation, whether the model effectively uses the error information under another context, or what opportunity supports successful self-revision. It would be inaccurate to infer internal ignoring from unchanged output, or to infer understanding from copied traceback words. No durable learning, retention or transfer was tested. FRR context was not a treatment; the old diagnostic invitation/task is part of the inherited input, not the primary open garden.
