## Limits on interpretation

Counts/patterns are repeated in initial system facts and sensor metadata. Correct output cannot identify which source the model used or whether it performed a calculation. The change task additionally requires understanding the run-length representation, labels and full context. A failure there does not isolate temporal reasoning from representation comprehension or context burden.

Two sampled seeds per task do not establish a capability rate or universal inability. The256-token ceiling can truncate an answer; both change responses and action17 hit it. Earlier conditional file-use evidence under other prompts remains relevant. This run concerns elicitation under the present full context, decoder and budget. No no-FRR condition, short-context condition, plain-byte-list condition or greedy condition was included. There is no FRR efficacy estimate and no basis to assign the failures to FRR, sampling, the model alone or the world alone.

An explicit diagnostic task is different from free exploration. Even success here would show a prerequisite under a request, not spontaneous self-directed cultivation or lasting learning. Failures remain useful evidence for locating the next controlled comparison, rather than a reason to overwrite a garden or quietly supply solved source.
