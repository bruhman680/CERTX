# Writer-handoff review contract

Review role: separately inspect the authored source, hidden-from-writer queries, writer/reader delivery records and outcomes. This role shares platform ancestry and parent context; it is not a blinded independent empirical replication. No participant recontact, global edits or new agents.

Prospective checks:

- Source ledger and authority/correction semantics are internally consistent; event identities, units, effective dates and missing data are unambiguous enough for the declared task.
- Questions and rubric are saved before writers, while writer prompts contain neither questions nor answer labels. Instructional isolation is disclosed rather than called a security boundary.
- Writer word budgets and actual lengths are recorded, including over-budget outputs; no rewriting, truncation or outcome retries silently remove failures.
- A shorter/longer writer output is one draw per budget. Differences cannot identify a general budget effect or controlled compression algorithm.
- Reader conditions preserve each actual writer output; summary-only versus source-access deliberately changes available information. This is not a pure formatting comparison.
- Query budget, lookup payload and final-turn policy are fixed. Parent replies follow requests, not desired outcomes. All preliminary/final replies, failures and counts remain visible.
- Score source-grounded warrant separately from lucky correct answers, appropriate uncertainty and conclusive completion. A truthful old summary can become insufficient for a new question without becoming false.
- Questions, artifact truth and authority are stipulated by the fixture; correct fixture use does not establish real source authenticity or naturalistic memory reliability.

Final checks:

- Parse delivery artifacts and compare to frozen packets/actual handoffs; verify manifests where applicable.
- Inspect every final response semantically, not only keyword scoring.
- Disclose parent authorship/unblinded review, one-context-per-cell and decisions clustered within contexts.
- Preserve original and repaired evaluators/outputs if a concrete fault is found; no post-outcome difficulty tuning.
- Distinguish using optional FRR practices from proving an advantage or durable reasoning improvement.
