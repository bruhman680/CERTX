# Three cycles: actual writer handoffs and future reader decisions

User authorized three cycles with appropriate swarm roles, intercommunication and optional upstream source scouting. [Plan](PLAN.md) and [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) bind the work. Two writers and four readers were fresh participant contexts; reviewer/scout shared coordinator history and exchanged method findings. The goal is learning useful distinctions, preserving failure and success, not proving a ranking. Stop after these three cycles for user review.

## Cycle1 — let agents write the handoff

[Two actual writers](cycle1/REPORT.md) summarized an18-event fictional ledger without seeing four fixed future questions. The90-word-cap writer produced78words. The180-word-cap writer produced186, violating its cap by6. We kept both unchanged; there was no retry or trimming. These are whitespace counts, not tokens, and one writer per budget cannot identify a budget effect.

Both handoffs retained the evidence for all four future tasks, including a correction, a derived balance, an uncertainty interval and inaccessible content. The longer handoff's 'unknown, not zero' phrase remained a possible semantic-hardening risk, recorded before reader outcomes rather than silently repaired.

A prospective reviewer also caught an upstream evaluator fault: the initial Q4 asked whether evidence establishes zero, while its key interpreted an answer about the count itself. The [pre-launch wording repair](PRELAUNCH_REPAIR.md) and original files are retained. The change occurred before any participant outcomes, not to improve a score after the fact.

## Cycle2 — what do fresh readers actually need?

[Four readers](cycle2/REPORT.md) cross the two unchanged handoffs with summary-only or optional original access. They produce16final answers clustered in4contexts:8conclusive no answers and8grounded unresolved answers. Both access-enabled readers requested the original and got it; summary-only readers received no new evidence. All first answers already matched the fixed outcomes.

All16final answers are warranted under actual exposed evidence. Readers use current82% humidity against80%, derive103litres against105, recognize that a guaranteed N8 interval straddles a narrower question, and avoid inventing a decoded archive count. Old acceptance remains scoped to its old band. None propagates the longer handoff's ambiguous wording into an unsupported nonzero count.

The handoffs were already sufficient for these tasks. Thus the tied result is not evidence of retrieval rescuing lost information, a budget advantage, or general raw-record redundancy. We did not add new questions after seeing summaries to force a failure. Scope and useful no-change findings remain part of discovery.

## Cycle3 — review changes the working habit

[Convening and review](cycle3/REPORT.md) separate factual fidelity, future-task sufficiency and reader warrant. The [upstream scout](scout/REPORT.md) read task-oriented compression methods whose guarantees depend on a specified task/model; these are design context, not a guarantee for arbitrary language handoffs. One older primary lead failed access and was not used. [Communication](COMMUNICATION.md) records how these findings affected decisions.

The [context-checkpoint guidance](../../modules/context-checkpoints.md) now makes the three handoff outcomes and actual budget adherence explicit, keeping uncertainty/negation, source scope and other decision-relevant relations visible. Core/runtime/schema stay unchanged. The readable-code/history idea remains deferred.

## Evidence and limits

Two writer turns, eight reader turns, four parent replies, two original requests/supplies; no outcome retries, exclusions or new sampled data. Exact backend model/effort/token costs were not exposed. Source authority is stipulated, tasks are authored, physical isolation is not enforced, and one reader per cell supports only an exploratory observation. Separate review knows the source/key and shares platform ancestry; it is not blinded empirical replication.

Public payloads, original and repaired fixtures, hashes, source-access failures and review are retained. Mechanical scoring was implemented after replies against the earlier criteria; semantic review is separate. Broader MEM-04 stays partial. No framework efficacy, robust compression policy, natural source authentication or long-history result follows.

The next useful boundary could be independently authored questions across a longer history, with declared sampling and writer-produced omissions preserved. That remains proposed, not an automatic fourth cycle. This run ends with a synced record for user review.
