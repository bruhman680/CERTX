## The important mismatch: queried success and summary fidelity

W360 states: “March26 cover-damage hold implied no contamination.” Source S050 says the hold does not assert contamination. S056 later says no visible contamination. Neither establishes that the hold implied clean material. Root flagged this before readers; both reviewers agree it is an unsupported strengthening on its ordinary literal reading. We do not infer writer intent or actual contamination.

The fixed Compost question asks administrative availability, not whether contamination was absent. Correct answers therefore neither validate the unqueried clause nor demonstrate a reader missed an assigned task. We retain the error separately and do not retroactively add a convenient failing question. This is an actual compression-fidelity concern despite tied queried performance, not a causal claim that larger handoffs are worse.
