# Roles, communication and visibility

Ten agent roles plus coordinator: two fresh preparation contexts, six fresh participant contexts, and two inherited-context reviewers. Participant budget was2writer+8reader turns. Preparation/review/coordinator operations are additional work, not included in that count. Provider token/compute cost and account usage are not exposed. No configured external model API or model installation was used.

- lh_source_preparer: fresh source-only author; no question/gold/summary visibility.
- lh_question_preparer: different fresh author; source-only input, no summary visibility; wrote all8queries/gold before writers.
- lh_design_review: inherited-context protocol/preflight and subsequent outcome auditor, knows source/gold.
- lh_w180/lh_w360: fresh writers, one supplied source/task-family packet and one turn each, no specific queries/gold.
- lh01/lh02/lh03/lh04: fresh readers, two turns each, actual handoff/questions and conditional original; no gold or other participants.
- lh_lateral_review: inherited-context interpretation review, actual artifacts and known gold, not a participant.

Design reviewer and coordinator resolved category overlap and packet-intake transport before freeze. Reviewer passed source/query/protocol preflight before any writer launched. Root flagged W360's contamination wording before reader launch and sent it to reviewer. Additional writer/packet review proceeded alongside the reader run; do not imply every supplemental audit finished before every participant turn. No correctness feedback or reviewer interpretation was sent to writers/readers.

Root supplied only the declared second packet to each reader after the first request. No extra participant turns/retries, new questions, trimmed summaries or revised source were introduced. Raw public answers and supplied packets are captured as files, with hashes; private reasoning is neither requested nor recorded.

In cycle3, outcome and lateral reviewers communicated directly about the unqueried fidelity issue, closing-state sampling and future strata. They agreed, then informed coordinator. This is genuine role communication within one system, not independent empirical corroboration. Isolation of test participants is deliberate so convening does not coach their answers.

One exact packet-file cat read per participant turn was permitted as the declared intake exception. We bind paths and supplied bytes and record the policy; we do not claim enforced security, complete read telemetry or guaranteed attention. Whole-original delivery is separate from search/index retrieval. Source authority is fictional and stipulated.
