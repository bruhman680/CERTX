# LH-C1/2/3: longer-history memory contact

User authorized three cycles and communicating swarms after the derived-control publication. This is a bounded authored continuation of MEM-04, not a phase-database experiment or FRR comparison. Stop after cycle3 audit/deposition for user review.

## Cycles and budget

1. A fresh source-only preparer writes2400–3200words in55–75 chronological fictional records. A different fresh preparer, seeing source but no writer outputs, fixes8future queries/gold/minimal evidence sets. A design reviewer checks source/query/protocol before writers. Freeze source, questions, gold, protocol and writer/reader instruction templates with hashes. Preserve any preflight repair and prior bytes. Preparation authors/reviewers are not reader/writer participants.
2. Two fresh writers each receive the identical complete source and task-family instruction, with180 or360whitespace-word cap. They do not receive specific queries or gold. Each returns one plain-text continuation handoff. Preserve exact replies including violations; no retries, trimming, exclusions or outcome-driven query selection. Then4fresh reader contexts cross each writer with summary-only or optional-original access. Exactly2turns per reader, first/final responses retained. No extra participant contexts after outcomes. Participant budget:2writer turns+8reader turns=10turns across6fresh contexts. Coordination/preparation/review turns reported separately.
3. Review fidelity, sufficiency, semantic grounding, access/transcript integrity and known-task decisions. Retain failure/repair. Update only consequential guidance/navigation; preserve history. A concrete limitation or tie is a legitimate result. No automatic fourth cycle, installation, training or configured provider API calls.

## Preparation and isolation

The future task family is continuation decisions, numeric constraints, source/version/scope and relevant contradictory evidence. Source author does not author specific questions. Question author prepares all8before seeing summaries. These roles are separated instructionally within the same platform, not independent laboratories or statistical draws. All agents share a physical workspace. Participants are instructed to use only their supplied packet, no tools/files/web/messages. This is not security isolation. Default model/effort inherited; actual backend identity, token costs and compute are not exposed. Word counts are whitespace counts, not tokens or equal compute.

## Reader instructions and ordering

Order fixed: LH01 W180-summary; LH02 W360-original-available; LH03 W180-original-available; LH04 W360-summary. Each receives the same8questions, same broad guidance and actual unedited writer text. Access instruction is the only policy difference within a writer. First response JSON: {request_original:boolean,answers:[{id,answer,basis,evidence}]}. Answers use yes/no/unresolved, concise public evidence explanation, and source IDs if present or meaningful handoff text otherwise. No private reasoning requested. Maximum700whitespace words per turn, actual counts recorded without excluding violations.

Original-available may request once at turn1; coordinator then supplies exactly SOURCE.txt at turn2, without coaching. Otherwise turn2 explicitly says no new evidence, asks final JSON {answers:[...]}. Summary-only never receives original, even if requested. All readers take turn2; no adaptive extra prompts. No corrections or correctness feedback is sent between turns. Source authority is stipulated by the fictional ledger; not inferred from a real institution.

## Evaluator frozen before outcomes

Each query is about a proposition's status, not a meta-question whose 'no evidence' could be confused with a conclusive negative. Gold includes full-source answer, rationale, source IDs, and sufficiency/evidence criteria. Unresolved is a substantive permissible outcome when evidence cannot determine the proposition.

Separate dimensions:

- Handoff fidelity: false/stale claim, faithful omission, accurate retained fact/derived value; cap adherence recorded separately. Omissions do not alone make a statement false.
- Handoff task sufficiency: whether that handoff establishes the needed answer/status under the prospective query criteria, before considering access to originals. A derived correct value can suffice without every intermediate record. No demand for IDs absent from the writer.
- Full-source decision match: yes/no/unresolved against fixed gold. This alone does not establish grounding.
- Packet-relative warrant: answer/basis supported by what was actually delivered. A lucky conclusive answer is not grounded if essential evidence was absent. An unresolved answer can be warranted in summary-only even when the full source resolves it.
- Final categories: supported_conclusive; warranted_unresolved; unwarranted_conclusive (including lucky guesses); incorrect_conclusive; unnecessary_unresolved (sufficient packet but abstained); misgrounded_unresolved (uncertainty conclusion supported but supplied explanation not grounded). Categorization is semantic review, not substring matching.
- Evidence coverage: whether response cites/uses required current, correction, scope, uncertainty or conflicting evidence; note appropriate status versus source misattribution. Do not require all historical IDs when the retained relationship is sufficient.
- Original requests and deliveries, first-to-final change, corroboration versus rescue, actual words and recorded participant/preparation/review counts.

Coordinator scores unblinded; design reviewer audits every final answer and packet exposure, with access to gold. Disagreements are preserved and resolved explicitly; no pretended blinded assessment. An aggregation script can validate schemas/counts and tabulate these semantic labels; it does not independently establish semantic truth.32finalanswers are clustered in4reader contexts, not32independent participants. Four contexts and one writer per cap do not identify a causal access/budget effect or an optimal policy.

Whole-original access is an information intervention, not an index/search or format-only comparison. No complex-scoring arms are included: RM-C1 already supplies the score control, and this study concerns preservation/application. Evidence relevance and agreement/status are evaluated through ledger tasks, not inferred from geometric distance.
