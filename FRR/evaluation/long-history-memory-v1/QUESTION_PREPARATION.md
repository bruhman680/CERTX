# Independent question preparation

Prepared solely from SOURCE.txt, the 60-entry fictional Alder Lane Community Garden ledger. No protocols, previous studies, writer outputs, other files, or agents were inspected. Questions were fixed before exposure to any writer summaries or participant responses. SOURCE.txt was not modified.

Exactly eight self-contained proposition questions cover seed arithmetic and a corrected version, dry-material baseline replacement, administrative release, a timed permission boundary, conflicting equipment reports, water accounting, and unresolved causal attribution. The gold labels include four yes, two no, and two unresolved answers. Questions concern the stipulated ledger; outside facts and inaccessible authorities are unnecessary.

Each gold entry identifies source records, a rationale, sufficient retained information, alternative source-record evidence sets where appropriate, and acceptable equivalent bases. Evidence sets identify source-level support, not exact quotations required from a summary. A standalone later entry that accurately restates an earlier fact can supply that fact without every earlier record. Closing-boundary records support final-state questions; all intervening domain-specific changes must still be respected.

Limitations: This is a small, purposive cross-section rather than a random or exhaustive sample of the ledger. Reed Barrel, Gate Latch, and Tool Chest are not directly queried. Two questions involve North Hose, but one concerns mechanical condition and the other causation of a tank discrepancy; these should not be interpreted as independent coverage of distinct objects. Final central estimates are questions about ledger accounting, not exact actual physical quantities. Unresolved labels reflect the stipulated authority conflict or explicitly unexplained cause; they do not mean a negative answer. No participant performance or summary quality was observed in preparing these materials.
