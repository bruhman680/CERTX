## What changed through correction

The noisy-echo simulation reused a random draw; corrected working copies preserve originals. RR1 retained its failed source prediction instead of retuning it away. A recovery evaluator's missing evidence belonged to the evaluation packet, not the actors. RR2's overly broad interim wording about zero prediction error was corrected to oracle-matched probabilities, with individual outcomes still random. Original Gemini harness inspection exposed an unreachable default entropy trigger and a connectivity monitor that did not read real communication. The later annealing threshold/schedule arithmetic and reported population/group counts did not match their supplied narrative. Garden reproduction preserved executable effects while exposing unreachable dormancy, clipping/controller behavior and archive/field conflation.

These are concrete revisions to claims or implementations. They do not establish that FRR caused the repairs or is immune to similar mistakes. Preservation of correction history is useful evidence of the inquiry's movement, not proof that the whole archive is now verified.

## Current FRR and infrastructure

Conceptual v0.5 remains the user's source; operational runtime/compact0.2 and inquiry schema0.1 remain unchanged. FRR is saved reasoning guidance with optional record/harness contracts. GitHub snapshots, handoffs, source originals, manifests and bounded trial records provide actual external artifacts. They do not implement an autonomous continuous semantic field, automatic retrieval, cryptographic source truth or permanently active swarm.

## Open directions with the most discriminating value

Source/version authority and spontaneous verification beyond obvious menu choices; reasoning under actual compression and competing revisions; task/reader-dependent preservation; matched and repeated FRR comparisons on separately prepared cases; noisy or mistaken provenance; opportunity versus realized intervention/compute cost. Role rotation, swarm scale and physical mappings should follow explicit operational questions rather than historical constants.
