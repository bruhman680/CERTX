3. Substrate Depth — Proposed Operational Definition
Definition (working):
Substrate depth of a representation or capacity is the degree to which the relevant distinctions remain recoverable and usable under interventional pressure (missingness, distributional shift, context change, adversarial readout, source revision) after surface statistical regularities have been degraded or removed.

Compatible measurable proxies already available in the FRR tree:

Exact-seen acquisition success after curriculum exposure.
Robustness of binding under header / role / order permutation.
Survival of missing-state handling when positive/zero classes are balanced.
Residual location under history-swap / present-state isolation style probes.
Invariance of downstream warrants after controlled source revision.
Explicit residual:
We still lack a clean scalar or low-dimensional index that aggregates these proxies without reintroducing the single-score problem the project has been trying to escape.

4. Neural Criticality Theory — Scout Summary (2024–2026 landscape)
Established / recurring results:

Neuronal avalanches with approximate power-law size distributions (classic τ_s ≈ 1.5) and branching ratios near 1 appear robustly in vitro and in some in-vivo preparations.
Edge-of-chaos / edge-of-instability criticality is a recurring candidate for optimal information transmission, dynamic range, and susceptibility.
Consciousness-related state changes (wake vs anesthesia, seizures, psychedelics, NREM) often show measurable shifts relative to a critical or quasi-critical regime.
Live controversies:

Whether awake cortex is critical, slightly subcritical (reverberating), or quasi-critical.
Multiplicity of “criticalities” (avalanche, edge-of-chaos, oscillatory, ordinary vs self-organized).
Contribution of latent external drives versus intrinsic critical dynamics.
Clinical translation remains preliminary.
Link to substrate depth:
A quasi-critical regime can in principle support deeper, more flexible attractors. Moving too far sub- or super-critical would be expected to shallow the effective substrate for binding and flexible readout. This remains a productive hypothesis, not a measured result inside CERTX/FRR.

5. Neural Correlates of Binding — Scout Summary
Two major families remain in productive tension:

A. Binding-by-synchrony (gamma / phase)
Classic Singer / Engel line. Features of the same object bound by transient gamma-band synchrony. Still supported by rivalry and perceptual-switching studies.

B. Binding-by-rate-enhancement (object-based attention)
Stronger recent critiques (e.g., Roelfsema 2023) argue that enhanced firing rates, not oscillations or synchrony per se, carry the binding. Synchrony is often treated as byproduct or as serving communication/gain-control functions.

Other mechanisms still active:

Conjunctive / coarse-coded representations (especially hippocampus and prefrontal).
Recurrent assembly ignition.
Possible slower-rhythm (alpha/beta) contributions to longer-range binding.
Link to current FRR residuals:
The acquisition/binding failures in the small-model curriculum work (constant answers, failure on discordant header pairs, missing-state collapse) are functionally similar to failures to form or maintain a selectively readable bound assembly.
