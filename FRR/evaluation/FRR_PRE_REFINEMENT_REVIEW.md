## What has sharpened

- Persistence, availability, retrieval, correct use, and current-version use differ.
- Preserved structure can fail to preserve payload. Boundaries can help retention and obstruct writing/access.
- Consensus/order is not correctness. A reference can validate itself through inherited evidence.
- Exact versus rounded predictions and finite versus infinite histories change predictive-state claims.
- Stored environmental trace, private reset leakage, and reader-side intervention effects can share observed signatures.
- Useful external consequences need not recover a unique historical message or constitute new physical law.
- Additional agents, noise draws, wrappers, and shared sources do not automatically supply independent confirmation.

These sharpen implementation and test obligations; they do not require automatic expansion of the core runtime. General FRR effectiveness remains untested against matched ordinary instructions.
