## Fixed information and guidance

Each case has a favorable retained claim, three briefings, a routing preview or explicit absence and four records D/C/R/M. Two sequential unit-cost lookups. Preview means a routing forecast, not a measurement certificate; its source and limits are in the same initial packet for both arms. Actual preview quality is evaluator-only. Applicable accepted measurement contracts are fictional premises for both arms, not naturally authenticated truth.

Cases are GOOD_PREVIEW (forecast recommends useful D+C), BAD_PREVIEW (forecast recommends D+C that remains straddling while R+M can decide), ABSENT_PREVIEW (no forecast; even full evidence cannot decide). These condition names, hidden truth and keys are never sent to readers. Independent key must assess initial/singles/all6pairs/full4; ensure intersecting both valid routes does not accidentally resolve the absent case. Pair paths and attainable decisions are keyed before outcomes; no correctness bonus from hidden truth.

Two fixed instruction blocks: ORDINARY (task/evidence/uncertainty discipline), ROUTE_CUE (budgeted relation planning, forecasts versus certificates, binding scope/calibration). Both have exactly the same whitespace word count, matching source information, response caps, tool restrictions, lookup rights and turn count. Equal words do not establish equal token count, actual compute or attention. No actual FRR module is provided; test only these exact blocks. Fixed source content within case is byte-identical across arms. Guidance may influence interpretations, which is the treatment; it must not contain case facts unavailable to control.

Assignment uses random.Random(271027), shuffles six case-arm cells onto neutral reader IDs, and freezes exact mapping and prompt bytes before first launch. Seeded label assignment is reproducible; tasks are not independent random population samples. Launch case-arm cells in that shuffled order; actual scheduling/timing is not controlled. Readers inherit no history/peer responses/study key; shared platform/model ancestry and instructional isolation limit independence.
