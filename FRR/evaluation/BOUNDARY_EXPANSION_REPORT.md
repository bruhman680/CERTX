# Expansion contact: substrate boundaries and memory

Status: exploratory constructed-model result. Source direction: the user's growing-swarm boundary intuition, substrate continuity, and local annealing proposals. No API/model calls, no AI-agent performance comparison, no claimed universal emergence. Three reasoning agents provided source scouting, a critical design pass, and an optional host module; all shared the inquiry lineage.

## What ran

Reproduce with `python /workspace/frr/experiments/adaptive_boundary_probe.py`. Results: [JSON](results/adaptive-boundary-expansion.json). Plot: [SVG figure](results/adaptive-boundary-expansion.svg), generated by `experiments/plot_boundary_probe.py`.

Periodic rings of 32, 64, and 128 sites begin with identical conductances. Opposite values are clamped at two sites for 300 preparation steps. The programmed local rule moves each edge conductance toward

    w_min + (1-w_min)*exp(-((x_j-x_i)/sigma)^2),

with w_min=0.02, sigma=0.3 and adaptation fraction 0.06. This deliberately resists mixing of distinct values. No explicit partition or split operation exists. The observed four weak edges form around the driven sites, not a discovered semantic hierarchy. Conductance never vanishes, so every ring remains connected.

Topology is then frozen. Each case writes two independent bits for 40 steps, stops writing, and evolves for 0, 20, 80, 200, or 600 steps. The field has decay 0.002 per step. A fresh linear reader receives two noisy local values, with noise standard deviation 0.08. Fit it on 160 calibration cases and score 512 held-out cases. The six seeds vary messages, noise, and weight shuffles; preparation graphs are deterministic, not six independent graph realizations. Sizes change spatial separation as well as site count.

Controls: uniform graph; learned frozen graph; shuffled learned weights; uniform weights at the same mean conductance; field erasure; and restoring uniform transport after writing. A follow-up uses exactly the same uniformly written field for learned and shuffled retention. This follow-up was motivated by the first run: it is an exploratory repair, not an untouched final evaluation of a predeclared hypothesis. Both phases' relevant conditions remain in the final results.

576 condition/seed/size/horizon evaluations share paired messages and noise; they are not 576 independent replications. There are 18 seed/size batches, each with 512 evaluation messages. Readers are refit per condition/horizon, so results measure recoverability with a calibrated reader, not zero-shot transfer of a fixed decoder between graphs. No hyperparameter or decoder selection sweep was performed.

## Results that change the inquiry

Mean held-out bit accuracy at 200 steps, percent:

| Sites | Uniform writing/retention | Learned writing/retention | Uniform writing, learned retention | Shuffled learned writing/retention |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 95.51 | 97.61 | 100.00 | 99.43 |
| 64 | 98.63 | 93.28 | 99.98 | 99.64 |
| 128 | 98.91 | 93.60 | 100.00 | 99.17 |

At 600 steps, learned writing/retention falls to 58.87%, 53.52%, and 55.45%; uniform writing/retention gives 73.49%, 71.27%, and 72.22%. With the same uniformly written field, learned retention gives 74.59%, 73.80%, and 74.77%. Shuffled weights can outperform the learned arrangement; this construction has not established that the learned boundary placement is optimal. Shuffling preserves the weight multiset but not each site's weighted degree or full spectrum, so it does not isolate geometry alone.

Erased-field readers are near chance at 200 steps (approximately 49–50%). Restoring uniform transport after learned writing reduces recall (approximately 68–70%). A separate impulse comparison preserves total mass and yields less signal at the receiver beyond the weakest edge than the restored graph. This is a measurable permeability restriction, not evidence-based correction or a guarantee about global transmission.

The first apparent failure decomposes: a boundary can limit writing as well as later mixing. Holding inscription fixed reveals a short-horizon retention benefit, while graph-dependent writing can leave a smaller useful deposit outside the source. Long-horizon decay still matters. The intervention that improves passage can simultaneously erase the local distinctions used by the reader.

## An exact carrier limitation

Preparing on x and -x gives identical learned conductance and opposite field values. This was numerically checked at all sizes and follows algebraically from the squared difference in the rule. A graph-only reader cannot distinguish these two equally likely histories. The graph carries an interaction pattern, while the field carries the sign payload. Preserving one does not preserve the other.

Finite-field retention is not permanent memory. Boundary persistence is not correctness. A fresh decoder's recovery is not unique retrodiction of an unknown initial field. These are explicitly constructed dynamics, not observations of 32–128 reasoning agents or evidence of FRR's efficacy.

## Checks and analytical footing

The dt=0.2 diffusion stencil is a convex update because all weights are at most one and each site has two neighbors. Decay is a separate contraction. The code checks conductance bounds, sign-reversal equivalence, and the stencil condition. Impulse mass is conserved in the no-decay transfer comparison to floating-point precision. Positive graph gaps confirm weak boundaries without disconnected components.

The trained Fiedler values are approximately 0.00264, 0.00152, and 0.000666. They do not represent a 0.05 semantic-continuity threshold. Their decrease partly reflects increasing ring size and cannot support a universal swarm scale law.

## Expansion, compression, and surviving relationships

Three properties remain separate: retained structure, recoverable payload, and accessible influence. This is a useful compression of this experiment, not an exhaustive ontology.

An exploratory image: a boundary can be a gate whose hinges remember an earlier encounter without remembering what was said. In this model, weights are the hinges and values are the message. The image helps separate carriers; it is not independent evidence.

For FRR hosts, preserve sources and erased distinctions when promising reopening; define what may pass a consequential boundary; and distinguish inherited agreement from independent contact.
