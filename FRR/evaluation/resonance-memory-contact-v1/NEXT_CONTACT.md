# Next MEM-04 contact: preserve evidence before judging agreement

Design refinement, not executed and not final registration.

Carry forward the resting checkpoint's strongest direction: longer histories with future questions prepared separately from summaries. The present formula contact adds an information-matched retrieval control, not a reason to replace the memory backend.

Before outcome collection:

1. Prepare a longer versioned ledger with corrections, scoped negations, missing values, conflicting observations and irrelevant but similar records. Preserve immutable IDs, dates/versions, units, source and status. Keep labels separate from any aggregate representation. Specify which authority relations are stipulated and which must actually be inferred.
2. A separate preparer fixes future questions, expected evidence sets and admissible unresolved answers without seeing writer outputs. Freeze all source/query/gold hashes and the evaluation contract before writers run. Writers know the task family but not individual questions. If delegation is used, explicitly authorize it and isolate preparers/readers instructionally; shared workspace is not security isolation.
3. Freeze writer budgets, actual compliance metric, reader access policy, number of contexts, turn limit and cost accounting. Preserve violations and unsuccessful attempts. Do not manufacture omitted details by selecting questions after summaries exist.
4. Separate two experiments. Encoding/scoring comparison gives every arm identical information: amplitude-only ablation, complex representation, full real+imaginary representation, and explicit status/metadata filtering. The amplitude-only arm is deliberately information-losing and cannot support a pure scoring-effect claim. Summary-versus-original access is a distinct information intervention, with its own cost. Do not interpret a combined manipulation as a format effect.
5. Score relevant evidence coverage (including contradictions), version/authority selection, fidelity, next-task sufficiency, warranted final answers and unnecessary abstention separately. Report disagreement retrieval versus agreement retrieval according to declared task. Keep contexts as the unit of behavioral replication, not individual questions in a context.

For equal-norm data, no separate behavioral arms are needed merely to rediscover an algebraically identical score. Use the identity as an implementation/control check and spend participant budget on encoding, status preservation and actual task application. If amplitude calibration matters, vary it independently of semantic labels and document the intended meaning of magnitude.

Stop after the predeclared study and review. Ties are useful outcomes. Broader memory transfer and independent preparation remain open even if a small authored packet succeeds.
