The completed randomized study supports an insulation benefit on qualifying hot-route days. Independently calibrated loggers measured the prespecified two-minute endpoint in 12/100 versus 29/100 shipments: a 17-percentage-point absolute reduction and an estimated risk ratio of 0.41 (95% CI 0.22–0.76). Randomization within route-day blocks, complete records, and masked processing strengthen this causal interpretation.

This resolves the earlier uncertainty about benefit in hot weather, but it does not validate the original pilot’s 40% claim. That estimate compared operational alerts measured under different firmware thresholds.
