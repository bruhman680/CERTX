# User idea: group reasoning as a distribution of judgments

User proposed that individual agent trajectories might differ even when similar, and asked whether group reasoning could expose a range/spectrum instead of only individual results. Preserve the curiosity without treating unavoidable infinitesimal differences as established. Digital computation and generated outputs can coincide; deterministic execution or sampling collisions permit identical outputs. We cannot observe private internal trajectories here and need not request them.

This is a natural lateral extension of our separate participants and communicating reviewers, not yet a measured group-reasoning intervention. Current reviews show convening is operationally possible, but agreement between same-platform known-gold reviewers is not independent empirical corroboration. The longer-history run had8questions per context and32final answers total. Its four reader contexts chose the same labels; those shared choices establish neither universal trajectory uniqueness nor calibrated certainty.

## Proposed later contact, unexecuted

Fix source/tasks/gold, eligibility, model settings where visible, number of contexts, turn/cost budget and scoring before outputs. Obtain separate first judgments with public evidence bases and source references, not private reasoning. Preserve the joint answer distribution, unsupported claims, appropriate uncertainty, and shared evidence ancestry. Then randomly assign matched follow-up contexts to peer exchange versus an additional individual review step, accounting for equal information and the extra calls/context supplied by discussion. An aggregation-only condition receiving the same initial peer judgments can help distinguish pooling from interaction if budget permits; individual-only review lacks those peer judgments and therefore tests a different information condition.

Discussion should ask participants to identify evidence and disagreements, not force consensus. Record which correct/incorrect claims are adopted, repaired or copied, whether minority correct evidence survives, and whether confidence calibration changes. Output diversity alone is not quality: a group can manufacture variety or converge on a shared error. Treat questions within a group as clustered and shared model/source lineages as dependence. Exact runtime settings and token costs may remain unavailable; report that rather than claiming a matched compute experiment.

For intuition only, three independent binary voters each wrong with probability p have majority error3p²−2p³; with perfectly shared errors, majority error remains p. At p=.2 these are .104 and .2. This algebra assumes identical independent Bernoulli errors in the first case, not observed agent behavior. It illustrates why headcount and agreement cannot substitute for error-dependence measurement.

Suggested purpose: determine when discussion preserves or repairs evidence and when it spreads mistakes. Do not turn the study into a claim that swarms always outperform individuals, or assume observed spread estimates calibrated uncertainty. No group participant run, new queue family or claim of internal consciousness/uniqueness is launched here. Revisit after the usage pause with current user direction.
