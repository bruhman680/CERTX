## Primary contacts

**[Locatello et al., ICML2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/locatello19a/locatello19a.pdf).** Read sections1,3 and selected experimental/model-selection passages in4/5. Theorem1 constructs entangled latent alternatives preserving observations under its factorized-density assumptions. Their setting observes data without latent-factor labels; their empirical study separately examines regularized VAE methods and model selection. This does not imply that every structured representation or supervised factor recovery is impossible.

**Our implication:** the fixture's named, separately accessible context/binding blocks are supplied structure. A successful projection demonstrates a readout under that assumption, not discovery of semantic factors. Declare who provides the partition, labels and codebook. The theorem does not directly apply to this constructed known-factor experiment.

**[Ovadia et al., NeurIPS2019](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/file/8558cb408c1d76621371888657d2eb1d-Paper.pdf).** Read sections1–3 and selected section4methods/shift examples. They evaluate classification probabilities under matched tests, increasing input shifts and out-of-distribution inputs. Metrics include accuracy and probability-quality measures; good matched-condition calibration does not establish robustness to shift. Their study is about probabilistic neural classifiers, not phase-centroid estimation.

**Our implication:** matched and mismatched noise/mixture conditions should be named separately. Estimating an angular centre is not probability calibration. Circular concentration or distance to a centre should not be presented as a calibrated probability of truth. Encoded U is a source-status label; decoder abstention is a different output.
