# UB-C1/2/3: upstream bundle, actual retrieval and stance under shift

2026-10-25. User asked whether our phase example is a fiber within a broader bundle of dynamics, and authorized3cycles plus targeted/lateral scouting. All3cycles are complete. We tested a narrower operational question: which supplied encoding, structural access, readout or fitted codebook causes an end-to-end evidence/status failure?

## Outcome first

The same low joint score can arise from different stages: relevant evidence never reaches the decoder; a stance centre cannot be fitted; A/N centres collide; or a shifted yet operational codebook emits wrong labels. A known binding projection repairs the clean scoped cases in this fixture, but can discard useful context under noise. There is no universally best readout established here.

This is a constructed oracle-coded numerical study, not learned language, a database benchmark, agent-group reasoning or physical semantic memory. The known context/binding blocks and A/N/U phase labels are supplied. Their meaning and separability are not discovered by the experiment.

## Fixed experiment

Three seeds;8evaluation entities×2times,4separate calibration entities×2times;3statuses A/N/U.24context and24binding coordinates; phase U=pi/3. GLOBAL rotates the whole record; SCOPED rotates only the binding block. All records/queries are normalized. Calibration and evaluation ratios independently take.5,1,2; calnoise0/.75, evalnoise0/.75/1.5. Two noise repetitions per claim, with paired noise reused across methods/conditions.

Four methods: full realified cosine+angle; full complex modulus+angle; exact real A/B modulus+angle; binding-block modulus+angle using supplied coordinate structure. The binding method receives the actual noisy block, not gold entity/time/status at inference. No perfect metadata arm is counted; it would be an oracle ceiling, not a fair uncorrupted rival to noisy vectors.

The single run produced10368paired query-condition rows and41472method records. There are48latent evaluation claims,1728generated query IDs and720distinct raw query-array hashes. Counts represent substantial reuse and zero-noise duplication, not independent experimental subjects. No significance tests or population confidence intervals. Runtime3.64seconds is execution provenance, not a retrieval-performance claim.

Status U means the source's encoded “not established” label. ABSTAIN means the decoder cannot choose a label. A missing centre makes the declared3way decoder abstain for that condition; a zero product has undefined phase; tied closest centres cause an ambiguity abstention. Those are numerical/decision policies, not calibrated probabilities of truth. Resultants are saved as diagnostics, not confidence scores.

### What the zeros and successes mean

- SCOPED ratio.5 has perfect stance decoding **when given oracle-relevant records**, yet joint success.9167: ranking can omit relevant evidence. Conditional stance accuracy is not a pipeline score.
- At SCOPED ratio1, N's own-scope full product is zero; its centre is unavailable. Our conservative missing-any-centre policy abstains on all3classes. This does not mean A/U disappeared from the full vector. Separately, modulus ranking omits the zero-comparison N record.
- At SCOPED ratio2, A and N share an angle; matched codebook decoding abstains on the tie. Training the codebook at.5 and evaluating at2instead yields definite wrong labels without those abstentions. Codebook availability is not correctness under shift.
- The clean binding projection succeeds because the correct partition is supplied. Under noisy GLOBAL inputs, full vectors retain useful context that the projection discards. The observed.6458versus1.0000 comparison demonstrates a conditional tradeoff, not a universal projection policy.
- Modulus ranking deliberately makes status variants tie; its agreement@1 can depend on last-bit tie order even when evidence and decoded statuses are all correct. Do not interpret that ranking statistic as lost stance information.

## Four primary sources, deliberately bounded

Two scouts used2queries/2primary works each and communicated methods/assumptions with reviewer. [RotatE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.10197v1) and [ComplEx](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/trouillon16.pdf) concern learned relation-conditioned knowledge-graph operators, not a universal phase=stance rule. [Ovadia et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/file/8558cb408c1d76621371888657d2eb1d-Paper.pdf) tests predictive probabilities under shift, not our angular-centre estimator.
