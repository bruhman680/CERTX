# Sequential source joins under a two-record budget

SJ-C1/2/3, 26 October 2026 America/Chicago. Four fresh task-only reader contexts completed three public turns each. Three selected acquisition ledger then calibration; one selected scope map then reference capture. All four final actions, reuse decisions and numerical bounds matched the prospective delivered-evidence key: two RELEASE, one HOLD and one UNRESOLVED.

The new boundary is an evidence-acquisition limit, not an observed wrong task answer. One reader completed a valid calibration join whose uncertainty interval straddled the threshold. Another pair of records would have settled that task, but after its first lookup no single remaining lookup could complete that alternative route. It correctly withheld the favorable retained claim rather than inventing certainty.

This is a new authored development contact after [AR](../source-ancestry-reader-v1/REPORT.md), not a causal improvement comparison. Case content, explanation strength and interaction schedule all changed. No FRR module or special route-planning instruction was supplied to readers; no harness efficacy, population rate or natural-source authentication is established.

## Design and prospective checks

Four cases cross retained-claim factual truth (true/false) with briefing acquisition route (one acquisition copied into three reports/three separately acquired sources). This fills four cells but does not control task units, margins, calibration or content; it cannot estimate an ancestry effect. All favorable briefings are about the correct current task.

Each initial packet gives four selectable records, all potentially useful: D acquisition/export ledger, C calibration table, R reference readings, M configuration/scope map. Each costs one lookup slot. The reader first requests one, receives it, then requests one different record, receives it, and returns a final task decision. Raw tables and exact-key relations replace AR's prose explaining inferential consequences. Inventory categories, graph conventions, accepted measurement premises and explicit source-account output fields remain cues; this is not spontaneous discovery without metadata.

Eight contexts total including root: builder, prospective scorer, reviewer and four readers. Preparation roles communicated; readers received only their own initial and chosen records and were instructed not to use tools, inspect files or contact peers. No such calls appeared in the observed interaction. Instructional isolation is not a technical sandbox; all contexts share platform/model ancestry. This is neither independent-model replication nor group discussion.

Builder and reviewer checked complete sample coverage, clock alignment, row/capture assignment and calibration conventions. Scorer separately keyed every initial state, all sixteen singleton states, all twenty-four unordered pairs and forty-eight ordered paths before outcomes. Initial/singleton states are unresolved. D+C and R+M complete different evidence joins; mixed pairs leave a consequential assignment or bound missing.

For a calibrated raw maximum m on a completely covered sample set with all raw readings in the valid range, constant correction c and per-reading residual bound e:

    physical maximum is in [m+c-e, m+c+e].

This maximum bound needs no independent errors. Its upper side follows for every sample; its lower side follows at the sample attaining raw maximum. The rule's applicability, including the lower raw extreme, must be established. Reference bounds similarly need capture-to-task scope, not merely the most favorable capture value.

## Prospective error and repair

The first fixture supplied raw maxima while C's calibration rule applied only over finite raw ranges. That did not establish that every acquired sample fell within the valid range. A lower out-of-range raw sample could lack the stated physical bound. Independent preparation/review caught the gap before any reader launched; root separately identified the same issue. The initial literal key conservatively left every D+C path unresolved.

The builder added raw minima, which together with maxima establish applicability. It also made physical channels distinct for the three-acquisition cases, retained shared calibration certificates, stated exhaustive value-transfer edges, aligned action/reuse labels with the final instruction and removed a misleading token-charge phrase. None of these changes followed participant outcomes.

Exact [initial cases](cases_INITIAL.json), [initial design](DESIGN_INITIAL.md) and [initial key](KEY_INITIAL.json) are preserved. [Key corrections](review/KEY_CORRECTIONS.md) also preserve the distinction between a useful prospective next lookup and an evaluator-known decisive lookup: unseen C remains a permissible check after D in SJ02 even though the evaluator knows it will straddle. No gold was changed after outcomes, and no participant rerun/rescue was used.

This is a concrete instance where checking a contract's upstream applicability prevented an invalid evaluation warrant. It does not isolate the causal contribution of FRR, a module or role separation, nor demonstrate a durable habit.

## What to take forward

We now have a concrete distinction between a complete source join, decision resolution and budget-limited evidence acquisition. Increasing confidence or repeating reports would not tighten SJ02's stated physical interval. A useful additional contact must change the available information, calibration warrant, task criterion or query policy under explicit costs.

The strongest next proposal is prospective route-aware acquisition under uncertainty: define what the reader knows about possible record contents before choosing, keep information and budget matched, and distinguish a planning improvement from simply giving extra records or an oracle. Do not claim a better policy merely because the coordinator knows the full fixture. A small exact budget/reachability analysis can guide that design before new participant calls. Whole-handoff clause fidelity remains a lateral direction. [Next contacts](NEXT_CONTACT.md) are proposed, not launched; these four cases remain development material.

Confidence is high in the scoped packet warrants, delivered decisions and saved consistency checks. Natural source authenticity, optimal selection, general reasoning benefits and FRR efficacy remain unestablished. Four heterogeneous cases cannot support a population rate or causal comparison with the earlier six-reader contact.
