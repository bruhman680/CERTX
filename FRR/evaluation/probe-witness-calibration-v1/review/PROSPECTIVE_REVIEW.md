## Required distinctions

With actual `D=n+s` and `Dprobe=alpha*n+beta*s`, coefficients with distinct values identify `s=(Dprobe-alpha*D)/(beta-alpha)`. A supplied compatible coefficient rectangle excludes singularity only when it is nonempty and its gap interval excludes zero under the declared numerical rule. This does not certify that the true coefficients belong to the rectangle, that the mean estimate lies inside it, or that an actual task decision is correct. All three questions require separate fields and reports.

EXACT, BOUNDED, BETA_GAIN_ERROR and INVALID_SHARED_BIAS must retain their declared information differences. Root clarified before outcomes that the earlier working label BETA_ONLY is replaced by BETA_GAIN_ERROR: four exact alpha witnesses (zero error and epsilon) and four beta witnesses with +0.1 error and declared epsilon 0.12; neither role is missing. Shared bias can yield internally consistent, sharply bounded, nonsingular apparent coefficients while excluding the truth. Independent role-pure anchors, their selective access, error-law bounds, number and measurements are privileges and costs.

Arithmetic mean of measurements is not generally inside the intersection of per-observation error intervals. The fixed deterministic error construction must be stated; point/set compatibility should be checked or any discrepancy disclosed. Empty intersection must be distinguished from a set admitting singularity. Neither should silently become a recovered zero operator.

Task evaluation must preserve separate states for operator-unidentified/unavailable, gated withholding and ordinary decoder abstention. An unavailable arm's zero projector, if used for storage, is a placeholder. Five arms must compare the same source queries/candidates/training prefix and separately fitted calibration under the inherited norm, product and centre policies. No task-label tuning of witness errors, estimates, gate or thresholds.
