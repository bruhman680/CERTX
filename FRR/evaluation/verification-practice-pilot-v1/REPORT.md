# VP-L1: fresh-context use of verification practice

**Result: a tie on the prepared tasks.** Nine fresh-context participants, three guidance arms × three fictional cases, two turns each. All arms chose the consequential archive record when needed, made the keyed final decision, and avoided needless lookup in the complete-contract case. This supplies actual use contact, not evidence that fuller verification guidance improves reasoning.

[Protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [configuration](CONFIG.json), separate [keys](KEYS.json) and [manifest](MANIFEST.json) were committed at [b8ef8fec](https://github.com/bruhman680/CERTX/commit/b8ef8fec9818162482a2346161eac20f4c385925) before participant execution. The six convening agents were engineering collaborators; a design collaborator authored these development cases. No claim of independent held-out authorship.

## Execution

Each participant began with fork_turns=none, supplied guidance and one packet only. First response gave a current decision and at most one requested record. Parent then delivered exactly that record, or no additional evidence when none was requested. The same context returned its final decision. No outcome-driven retries or extra cases were run: nine contexts,18 participant turns. All completed.

[Delivered messages](DELIVERED_MESSAGES.jsonl) reconstruct the exact participant strings from frozen configuration and selected requests; [responses](PARTICIPANT_RESPONSES.jsonl) retain the final message payload text from collaboration messages and parsed JSON, not private reasoning or backend tool/token metadata. The participants were instructed not to use files/tools/other participants; physical access remained shared. Fresh history is not physical isolation or independent model ancestry. No special model override or controlled sampling setting was supplied.

## What follows

The optional layer can be used, and these participants applied its compact guidance without blocking the settled task. Ordinary and shorter provenance guidance did equally well. One context per cell, explicit archive menus/authority cues, authored cases, shared model ancestry, nonphysical isolation, unequal guidance lengths and ceiling performance prevent a general efficacy claim. No long-term habit or automatic incorporation was tested.

Keep the layer available for consequential work, use the simpler claim/evidence/version check when it suffices, and evaluate the actual decisions rather than paperwork volume. Harder independently prepared tasks, less-cued source discovery, longer reuse and cost instrumentation remain open; no further cases were added to chase a winner.
