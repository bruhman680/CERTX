## What the current framework actually asks an AI to do

Preserve an idea's originating seed while allowing its representation and role to change. Let open play, directed exploration, and claim engineering coexist. Select useful forward, backward, and lateral movements rather than walking through five lenses mechanically. Preserve ambiguity until a distinction changes a consequence. Define mathematics locally; each substrate supplies its own laws.

When intuition becomes an artifact or claim, inspect construction, serious rivals, meaningful failure, and source ancestry. Compression is behavior-relative, with scope, horizon, intervention family, and tolerance. Persistence needs carrier diagnosis; a changed environment differs from a remembering focal state. Reject only what was actually tested, retain useful instruments and inactive possibilities, and trace obsolete claims through downstream records. Stabilization may be followed by reopening or rest.

The runtime additionally separates first-person meaning from attached external factual explanations, prohibits invented measurements, defines exposure when claiming independent passes, and names consequential stopping bases. These are active 0.2 obligations, not merely retrospective commentary.

## Optional infrastructure, rather than universal reasoning laws

Bounded inquiry supplies branch/resource accounting; context checkpoints distinguish storage, availability, retrieval, application, and revision; substrate boundaries specify recoverable compression and local reopening; role switching requires actual changes of action rather than labels; carrier interventions distinguish operation effects from carrier sufficiency and unique mechanism attribution.

These are host contracts. The prompt itself does not store durable memory, implement concurrency, guarantee access, control thermal physics, create autonomous agents, or prove independence. They should remain conditional modules rather than mandatory steps in open-ended conversation.

## What is not established

There is still no controlled actual-model comparison establishing that FRR improves AI reasoning, multi-agent performance, semantic memory, or transfer. Authored evaluation cases and synthetic probes are useful checks of particular constructions; they are not independent efficacy evidence. Separate agents with inherited context provide additional scrutiny without constituting independent replication.

Earlier reported cycles, universal thresholds, swarm evolution metrics, and cross-substrate equivalences remain qualified or unreproduced. Canon inclusion preserves provenance; it does not confer truth. Later mathematical counterexamples must not be erased from a favorable public narrative.

## Omissions and current uncertainty

1. The runtime compresses detailed mathematical and Source-Weave constructions. The archive is needed when their exact definitions become consequential; naming lenses is not a substitute.
2. The record structure emphasizes claims and artifacts. An implementation could unintentionally turn open play into claim engineering; no evidence yet settles how well the prompt preserves regime fluidity in practice.
3. Full and compact prompts may behave differently. Their comparative behavior is not evaluated.
4. The host contract leaves conflict handling, access, durability, selective retrieval, and rollback implementation to an application. Recoverability is conditional on actual surviving artifacts and working access.
5. No calibrated universal coherence, entropy, crystallization, temperature, or spectral threshold belongs to the core. Agreement and smoothness can erase the distinctions the inquiry needs.
6. Source archives record historical order of receipt, not necessarily conceptual chronology. The comparison explicitly warns that upload order does not establish dates or direct descent. A public timeline should use verified repository events or dates of experiments/intake and label reconstructed origins as such.
7. The latest predictive-state pass qualifies an earlier caution: exact one-step predictions identify the full future in the supplied K because disjoint ranges identify last symbol and hidden belief. Rounding and generic models do not share that guarantee. This refinement belongs in exploration evidence, not as a universal core clause.
8. Imperfect reset experiments motivate operational controls; they do not establish universal memory-location detection. Off-target effects and unavailable interventions can leave attribution unresolved even with unlimited samples.

## Refinement recommendation

Do not expand the core merely to incorporate every new toy or source. First publish an accurate current-framework account with explicit version and evidence status. Put latest findings and unresolved tests in a companion timeline or exploration section. Any later runtime change should respond to a demonstrated behavioral omission, preserve the prior release, and be evaluated against the existing version and a simpler baseline.

Public-post surviving relationship: **let ideas move, let artifacts push back, preserve what makes their history recoverable, and reopen a distinction when the next consequence requires it.** This captures the current framework without promising final ground or demonstrated AI improvement.
