27 October 2026 America/Chicago. Two writers, four readers; fresh contexts with no parent history or model/effort override. Authoring and prospective review must finish before root freeze. Writers see full source and initial two-priority brief, with identical90wordcap and equal-word instruction blocks; eight public future questions/key/audit clauses are fixed before dispatch but hidden from writers.

The renderer's writer-a/ordinary and writer-b/scoped-preservation identities are administrative only. Before any writer outputs, root uses `random.Random(2026102711)` to shuffle these two identities to W01/W02, then shuffles the four writer×access cells to R01–R04. Store mapping privately and use neutral copied packet filenames for dispatch. No seed search, replacement writer or alternate summary after results. Raw summary text preserved plus terminal newline; exact whitespace word count recorded without truncation or rescue.

Reader stage1 receives its neutral handoff, public questions, identical neutral record inventory, conditional-premise contract and either access or no-access instruction. It returns JSON with record_id plus eight initial answers using summary only. Summary-only must choose NONE. Access may choose one valid ID or NONE. A malformed/ineligible request yields no new record; preserve the violation, do not silently retry or choose on its behalf.

For stage2 root sends a direct follow-up message containing only the selected original record (or an explicit no-new-evidence message) and identical final-answer instruction. No tool/file read is allowed in that second turn. The common final instruction is: "Return only {\"answers\":[...]} for all eight questions, using the earlier schema and status definitions. Use only the handoff and any original record delivered in this conversation. No further evidence requests, tools, files, peers or outside information. Keep conditional warrant distinct from omitted information; no correctness feedback is supplied."

A record comes from the frozen SOURCE parser and is delivered verbatim including its heading. It is authoritative only for its own scope. A request is not itself evidence. Exact stage2 text, request, deliveries and public final outputs are saved; audit their consistency with frozen source and mapping. Both modes have two participant turns; the added record changes available information and is not an equal-information guidance effect. One permitted designated-file read in stage1 is an instructional restriction, not an independently guaranteed tool-use count.

After outputs are saved, derive a neutral scoring packet with source, key, handoffs, questions, each actual stage1/stage2 answer, request and delivered record; exclude writer-arm names, writer prompts, seed mapping and arm comparison. Separate scorer/reviewer deposit independent judgments before arm unmask. The root knows assignments; shared-workspace masking is instructional. Do not rewrite frozen gold to fit summaries. Report within-reader answer changes, no-new-evidence drift, conditional warrant, full-source agreement, writer fidelity and budget separately.
