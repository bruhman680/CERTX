# Prospective handoff and criterion-shift contact

This is a small authored-fiction contact with two fresh writers and four fresh readers. It follows earlier ceiling contacts in which both summaries retained every queried answer basis. The source has four research domains with distinct populations, measurement horizons, criteria and unknowns. Source statements are stipulated fictional premises, not authenticated empirical research. Questions and a separate clause-fidelity audit are fixed by a different preparer before writers run. Writers receive no questions, key, audit clauses or scoring hints.

The initial continuation has two priorities: the next water-sensor field-work priority and the next battery-coating funding priority. Both writers see the identical complete history, common instruction and 90-word hard limit. Only an equal-word-count instruction block differs: ordinary summarization versus a reminder to preserve scope, negation, uncertainty and changed criteria. These are two actual writer contexts, one per arm, not repeated samples. Outputs exceeding the cap remain unchanged and are reported as violations; no retry, truncation or convenient replacement is allowed.

After the writer outputs are preserved, each handoff is crossed with summary-only and single-record access, producing four fresh reader contexts. Every reader receives the same frozen questions, neutral record-ID/domain/date inventory, response schema, authority contract and own handoff. The inventory contains no record contents or answer-bearing descriptions. Readers do not see the initial writer instructions or other handoff. They first return a record-selection JSON only. Summary-only readers must select NONE; access readers may select NONE or exactly one inventory ID. The coordinator then delivers a second prompt to each context: only the requested record for access readers who requested one, or no new evidence otherwise, followed by the same final-answer instruction. There is no correctness feedback, retry, second request or full-source search. Access changes available information; this contrast is not an information-matched guidance comparison.

The reader authority contract treats explicit handoff assertions as available premises for conditional task scoring. Missing assertions stay unknown. A delivered original record is authoritative for its stated scope and supersedes conflicting handoff claims. This does not declare every handoff assertion faithful to the original. Conditional reasoning from available premises, full-source answer correctness, retained-claim fidelity, omitted answer bases, evidence acquisition and budget adherence are recorded separately. An incorrect compressed assertion can support a conditionally valid answer that remains externally wrong. Lack of evidence is not automatically a negative finding; status labels and evidence requirements are fixed in the separate key.

Participant isolation is instructional: one designated prompt-file read per turn and no subsequent tools, files, peers or outside information. The coordinator transports any source record rather than allowing direct source-file access. Both reader conditions use two turns. Shared infrastructure does not provide a claim of physical isolation, perfect attention or complete telemetry. Exact prompts and raw outputs are preserved, and prompt/source hashes are frozen before dispatch. No writer dispatch or reader rendering occurs until the source, cases, key and audit clauses pass prospective review.

The renderer refuses to operate without an explicitly approved freeze receipt that binds all four builder files and the separate public questions file by SHA-256. It renders writer packets without incorporating questions. Reader packets accept only public question text and schema, the exact saved handoffs, the neutral inventory and the authority contract. Gold answers and hidden audit clauses are never renderer inputs. Later questions must not be selected based on which facts the writers happened to retain. Omission is possible at this budget but neither omission nor task failure is guaranteed by construction.

Report eight answers as clustered within each reader, not as independent participants. This contact cannot identify a population causal reminder effect, optimal compression rule, search algorithm benefit or durable habit. Preserve initial materials before any prospective revision, and record revisions, cap violations, requests and deliveries. Do not reuse this contact as unseen confirmation after inspecting its outcomes.
