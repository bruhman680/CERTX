# Optional practice: trace a consequential recurring error

Use when a mistake recurs, several outputs fail together, or a correction could affect inherited conclusions. A failed hypothesis can still be useful research; name the task and the actual mismatch before treating every unsuccessful outcome as a reasoning defect.

1. **Locate:** What failed against which evidence, criterion or task? Is it an error, uncertainty, outcome luck or missing completion?
2. **Trace:** What inputs, difficulty, assumptions or peer outputs are shared? Agreement and repeated errors do not identify their causal route.
3. **Separate:** Which rival explanation would change the repair? Change source correctness separately from peer-answer exposure where feasible; include a sham and check that blocking a channel did not remove evidence. Record intervention timing.
4. **Repair and reopen:** Fix the relevant source or process, then revisit already derived conclusions that depended on it. A source correction alone does not update cached work. Flag dependents for reassessment rather than automatically declaring them false: some can remain valid or have independent support. Preserve the earlier record and why it changed.
5. **Check useful recovery:** Count correct completed work, appropriate withholding, recurrence, affected dependents and cost. Fewer reported errors can reflect silence or less exposure. Use denominators and comparable tasks before inferring improvement.

Keep this brief and proportional; it need not become a new form or block an already warranted action. Record unresolved routes as unresolved. Errors can share causes, spread, interact or occur separately. A real dependency map needs evidence; the current tools do not infer it automatically.

Evidence: [EL-C1](../evaluation/error-lineage-v1/REPORT.md) demonstrates these distinctions in constructed models and replays saved sensor data. Its twelve checks do not establish better real-agent behavior or durable habit formation. For one changed assessment, [reopen_assessment.py](../tools/reopen_assessment.py) can classify recorded changes and bindings; semantic downstream repair remains a human/agent task.


[RB](../evaluation/reference-bias-contact-v1/REPORT.md) adds a repair boundary: a witness can invalidate the warrant for reusing an answer without supplying enough information to replace it. Keep withdrawal of support, a proposed correction and a verified replacement distinct. Trace common upstream calibration or role assumptions even when reports have different authors; shared ancestry establishes dependence, not falsity. Use a separately warranted, task-relevant reference when available, and retain unresolved status when its authority or scope is insufficient. This is an optional inference discipline motivated by authored cases, not a demonstrated behavioral repair.
