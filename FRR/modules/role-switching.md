# Optional role-switching contract

Design candidate informed by [executed gap probe](../evaluation/ROLE_ROTATION_GAP_MAP.md). Compatible with runtime 0.2. Roles are selectable actions, not a mandatory sequence or a measured cognitive architecture.

Use a coherence/decoherence description when it changes the inquiry, with local definitions. Distinguish organized agreement, valid inference, reliable evidence, and task progress. Do not substitute entropy, lexical agreement, graph connectivity, or a shared reference for all four.

EXECUTOR continues a declared task. AUDITOR checks a particular claim, derivation, reference, or discrepancy. WANDERING introduces alternative representations or contacts where a residual warrants opening possibilities. A host must actually implement and record these actions before claiming role rotation.

Trigger changes from the consequence at stake: excessive dispersion may require locating conflicting assumptions; coherent stagnation may require a serious rival; a contradiction may require auditing; a valid stable result may require no rotation. In open play, movement need not carry a numerical trigger.

Before a reference drives correction, preserve its provenance, scope, and status. Agreement inherited from one source is not independent confirmation. When possible, use a comparison capable of rejecting the proposed correction. Keep a coherent wrong-reference condition in evaluations.

Record event time, available observations, chosen action, effect, and costs. Bound repeated switching; distinguish a shared cap from equal realized work. Keep an unchanged or ordinary-policy comparator. If only labels change, report no implemented intervention.

Local reopening should track effects on retained obligations and unrelated memory; use the [boundary contract](substrate-boundaries.md). Noise is one possible construction-specific intervention, not a guarantee of insight or validation.
