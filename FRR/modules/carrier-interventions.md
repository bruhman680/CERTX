# Optional carrier-intervention contract

Use when a claim depends on where a distinction persists after reset. This is a host experiment/review contract, not a universal FRR pipeline or a mechanism inferred from terminology.

## Declare the burden

Name focal state, candidate environmental carriers, private carriers, payload, reader, observation horizon, and allowed interventions. A field change may modify effective focal transitions without changing the full coupled operator. If claiming new laws, identify the rule-bearing variable separately.

## Describe actual operations

For each reset/erase/restore operation record intended targets, measured deletion fidelity, preserved channels, off-target changes, and observation-rule changes. Verify independent sentinel payloads where possible. A reset label is not evidence of successful deletion. Sentinels bound tested paths, not every hidden channel.

Cross field keep/clear with private keep/clear. Use no-write, pre-action, sham erase, blank-field erase, reader-blind, payload swap, and payload-only rescue when those comparisons change attribution. Preserve matched baselines and generate random draws independently by causal role. Common random replay across arms reduces variance; it is not empirical independence.

## Separate three claims

1. The operation changes the observed response.
2. A specified carrier is sufficient or necessary within a declared intervention family.
3. The historical mechanism or unique carrier has been identified.

The first does not by itself establish the latter two. Private persistence, unknown initial fields, and erase-induced reader damage can mimic a wake. If rival worlds agree on every available observation, additional samples do not resolve that structural ambiguity.

## Require room for meaningful failure

Run the diagnostic on private-only and off-target worlds, not only successful environmental examples. Declare attribution tolerance before evaluation. Record false attributions, missed small effects, and abstentions separately. A guarded rule that abstains frequently has not established universal detection.

Leave a handoff with current carrier alternatives, tested operator selectivity, raw observations, source ancestry, and the next intervention capable of distinguishing surviving rivals. Never promote synthetic success to real AI memory, semantic growth, or permanent substrate without a corresponding test.
