# Substrate continuity: source scout

Exploratory source contact, 5 October 2026. Search roles: external environmental memory; environment-mediated coordination; inheritance beyond actors; mechanism repair between individual response and collective trails. These sources inform the seed, not a universal wake-to-law theorem. Parallel agents share instructions and material; their agreement is not independent evidence.

## Established relatives

1. **Theraulaz & Bonabeau (1999), A Brief History of Stigmergy.** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10633572/ ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1162/106454699568700 . Inspected authoritative abstract. Historical review of environmental mediation in social-insect coordination, distinguishing quantitative and qualitative stigmergy and tracing the term to Grassé (1959). Contribution: actions can coordinate later actors without direct actor-to-actor communication. Limit: this is not proof that actors lack all internal memory or that environmental marks become permanent laws. Next test: substitute readers while retaining marks, then remove marks while retaining readers.

2. **Reid, Latty, Dussutour & Beekman (2012), Slime mold uses an externalized spatial “memory” to navigate in complex environments.** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3491460/ ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1215037109 . Full text inspected. Physarum avoids extracellular slime; masking its trail with substrate-wide slime worsened navigation through a U-shaped trap. Controls examined speed and food-cue response. Contribution: experimentally manipulable external trace, with fresh readers also avoiding prepared slime. Limit: brainless does not mean internally stateless; masking is not selective removal, and the study does not establish indefinite retention. Next test: prepared patterned trail, fresh reader, sham preparation, erased trail, and delay series; separate recognition from physical surface effects.

3. **Odling-Smee, Erwin, Palkovacs, Feldman & Laland (2013), Niche Construction Theory: A Practical Guide for Ecologists.** https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/669266 . Publisher abstract inspected; full text access unavailable here. Distinguishes environmental alteration from subsequent evolutionary response, and environmental legacies as ecological inheritance. Contribution: a recipient need not be the actor that produced the inherited environment; a changed field can alter opportunities or selection without encoding a readable message. Limit: evolutionary framework, not a universal information-storage mechanism or a claim that every environmental alteration constitutes adaptation. Next test: independently prepared recipients crossed with altered versus restored environments; distinguish immediate behavioral response from multigenerational effects.

4. **Perna et al. (2012), Individual rules for trail pattern formation in Argentine ants.** https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5827 ; published version https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002592 . Abstract inspected. Measured local pheromone response and connected individual turning rules with collective trail formation through noise and simulation. Contribution: a repair literature warning—collective nonlinear choice does not establish the same nonlinear rule inside individual actors. Limit: measured conditions and organism-specific behavior; abstract inspection does not substitute for detailed methods audit. Next test: measure both local reader response and collective persistence; compare candidate models that predict similar final trails but different responses to a transplanted mark.

## What changes in our inquiry

Substrate continuity has established relatives. Stigmergy concerns coordination through environmental change; ecological inheritance concerns consequential legacies encountered by later organisms; the slime-mold experiment pressures a particular external-memory mechanism. These overlap but do different work. Shared vocabulary and citation ancestry do not make them three independent confirmations of a universal mechanism.

A retained mark can guide a new actor, and inherited constraints can matter without any observer decoding a historical message. Thus distinguish **substrate carries a causal consequence** from **substrate preserves a recoverable distinction**. The latter requires a declared readout task and held-out inscriptions.

Best next controlled comparison: earlier writer absent, fresh reader blind to provenance, altered versus restored substrate, independently prepared sham, and a delay sweep. Record whether influence follows the substrate, reader, preparation apparatus, or maintenance network. Compare no logger, passive logger, and active probe if witnessing might change propagation. No universal phase boundary, novelty claim, or backward physical causation follows from these sources.

## Search limits and reopenings

Searches used stigmergy genealogy, ecological inheritance, external spatial memory, and individual-versus-collective trail mechanisms. Deneubourg et al. (1990) was located as an ancestor (DOI 10.1007/BF01417909), but its repository PDF could not be opened; it is a located connection, not an audited source here. Travelling modes, reaction–diffusion renewal, and observer-backaction need separate domain-specific scouting. Nothing in this scout closes those branches.
