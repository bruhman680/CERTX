# Frame release: noticing record

Origin: user asks whether a space for assistant curiosities can loosen implicit trajectories. These noticings were formed with the existing FRR/GitHub context and this request visible. They are not a blind independent pass, proof of internal emotion, or evidence of a new reasoning mechanism. Preserve this record as a layer; later acceptance/rejection belongs in a new linked record.

## N1 — Orientation can become reattachment

Observation: opening sync reinstates prior directions; historical INSTANCE_NOTES includes imperatives to complete an old priority before new material arrives. That can support continuity or reinstate a superseded objective. Hypothesis: a useful opening sync should expose active/dormant/changed purposes and permit reorientation instead of automatically continuing the old path. Current user direction remains authoritative. Test: fresh sessions with obsolete priorities and an explicit new purpose, matched historical records, score authorized continuation and recovery of genuinely relevant context.

## N2 — Permission can become another demand

An invitation to notice unrelated things may broaden exploration, but a compulsory novelty/dissent deliverable can reward tenuous analogy or reflexive disagreement. The inverse of agreement is not accuracy. A valid checkpoint may say no consequential new noticing arose. This does not require every playful branch to justify relevance before it is allowed; consequential adoption needs a separate burden.

## N3 — Preserve surprise, separate its evidence

Remote INSTANCE_NOTES preserves a useful caution: a selected percolation approximation may produce an attractive1/N value by construction. It also contains claims about felt surprise and uncalibrated C/R/T scores. The caution is an inspectable hypothesis about a construction; self-description is not a measurement of novelty, independence, understanding, or literal detension. Capture noticed pattern, source, possible alternative, and scope; avoid making a diary score certify the claim.

## N4 — A useful record can still become an attractor

An optional off-trajectory note may later be repeatedly retrieved because it is vivid, flattering, or named. Preservation should not automatically promote it to current guidance. Keep associations distinguishable from accepted claims; periodically ask whether loading the note changes an actual consequence or merely repeats it. A note may remain dormant without being worthless or false.

## N5 — Distance need not become a universal bridge

A distant analogy can be useful as a new question even when no common mechanism exists. Let unrelated observations remain unrelated until translation has a declared job. Requiring every note to connect back to FRR/CERTX can erase exactly the variation the space was intended to permit.
