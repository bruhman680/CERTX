## Qualifications retained alongside the original wording

- Recursive verification's philosophical trilemma is an orientation, not a theorem established by these attachments or a necessary reporting template. Its historical attribution has not been externally checked here.
- The claim that only one kind of evidence could ever exist for subjective reports is too broad. Chosen meaning and expressed preference differ from diagnoses or causal explanations; testimony can also have contextual evidence. Respect the report without universalizing its explanation.
- Palimpsest's “Revision is not correction” is useful as an image of recoverability, but operational revision can correct an error. Old wording remains historical evidence of what was said, not continuing support for a contradicted claim.
- Palimpsest's “only failure mode” cannot exhaust actual failures. Independent passes can share confounds, use poor instruments, or repeat a mistake. Independence is relative to shared dependencies, not total absence of common background.
- The stopped-clock illustration warns against treating bare agreement as strong support; it does not establish that such agreement is always worthless under every probabilistic question.
- The Reddit “Recursive Scale” passage says the chain continues “indefinitely” and then says not to assume infinite continuation. Preserve the source but use the qualified statement: recursion may continue where each level is justified.
- The older Mathematical Weave ends with a fixed list of outputs to present. Later integrated forms explicitly make these conditional. The runtime retains conditional presentation so exploration does not become a reporting checklist.

## Changes made

Runtime 0.2 adds four local obligations: explicit exposure when claiming independence; numerical provenance; separation of subjective meaning from external explanation; and a consequential stopping basis. Existing record version 0.1 remains unchanged. An optional pass record is documented separately rather than forcing extra fields into every conversation.

The five archived files are byte-preserved and hash-indexed. Candidate 0.1 prompts and documentation are preserved before updating the active files. Existing test results remain historical observations under 0.1, not evidence that the revised prompt was evaluated. Additional cases enter development only; no new held-out evaluation is claimed.
