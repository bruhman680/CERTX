## 1. The seed (Thomas's framing, in his words, compressed)

Started from Conway's Game of Life: simple rules produce hard-to-predict complexity that can still be traced back to source. The question: how does information at its simplest travel or move, and what constitutes a **node**: is a node the *fibre of trajectories that intersect or intertwine*?

Working answer so far: "node" has at least three non-equivalent meanings (section 6), and which one you get depends on the observable and the horizon you allow. So a node looks relational, not an object.

## 2. The FRR vocabulary for "where did an erased distinction go?"

When a coarse description merges things and a distinction later *returns*, we ask what carried it (provisional handles, not exhaustive, not independent dimensions):

- **shadow:** the distinction is still present *now* in the hidden configuration, suppressed by the observation.
- **echo:** carried by the *trajectory/history*; equal present states behave differently because of how they were reached.
- **reflection:** produced or selected by the *probe/intervention*.
- **wake:** persistence in an *altered environment* even after the focal system is reset.
- **mirror:** the same relational form appearing in another representation/substrate (translation; may be faithful or not).

Lesson repeated throughout: *a shared silhouette is not a shared mechanism*, and a failed argument does not falsify its conclusion (keep claim / argument / mechanism / construction separate).

## 3. Shadow ↔ echo experiments (exact + simulated)

**Model.** Visible bit y∈{0,1}. Hidden bit e picks a kernel K[e][y]: e=0 → [[.85,.15],[.10,.90]], e=1 → [[.55,.45],[.40,.60]]. Hidden update family on an edge: e' = e w.p. 1−h, e' = y_prev w.p. h.
- h=0 (**shadow**): e frozen → non-ergodic mixture of two Markov chains.
- h=1 (**echo**): e is a copy of the previous visible symbol → order-2 Markov in y.
- small h: shadow with slow leakage (also the seed of "wake").

**Results (exact word laws via forward algorithm unless stated):**
1. Shadow and echo are *not* the same passive process: finite-word TV between them is .3604 at depth 8 and .4545 at depth 12 (these two numbers were reproduced independently in the CERTX audit, same lineage caveat). An earlier draft claim "same passive statistics" is contradicted.
2. A continuous interpolation between shadow and echo exists *in this kernel family by construction*. It is not an identity between diagnostic modes.
3. Switch-rate spread over long single trajectories is bimodal for shadow (non-ergodic) and unimodal for echo. That probes finite-window mixing, **not** hidden-state identity.
4. Hankel rank / order-2 conditional gap separate the two cases exactly.

**Corrections received (important):**
- **My bug.** In `eps_sweep.py` the noisy-echo simulation reused the same uniform draw for the visible symbol and the hidden fair coin. For e=0,y=0,δ=.2 the joint P(y'=1,e'=1) should be .015; the code gave .03. Exact tables unaffected; only simulated switch-rate spreads for noisy echo were off. Codex's corrected run moves the δ=.2 mean from .258 to .264 (unpaired runs, so not a bias estimate by itself).
- **"Predictive-state counts" were mislabeled.** They were counts of *rounded one-step next-symbol probabilities*. In this specific K, the exact next-symbol probability identifies the last symbol and hidden belief, so one-step equality *does* imply full-future equality here. Rounding destroys that: two histories rounding to .63 differ by .01395 in two-step future TV (ε=.05, depth 12). Finite-prefix counts are not stationary infinite-past state counts. No finite count proves infinitely many predictive states.
- Literature: Jurgens & Crutchfield (arXiv:2102.10487) predictive states generically infinite for HMMs *under ergodic assumptions*; our shadow end is non-ergodic, so that result cannot be applied directly (abstract-level check). Grassberger (arXiv:2401.03279) disputes the infinite-state framing (abstract only).

## 4. Wake: reset experiments (exact)

Setup: reset the focal visible state y, keep vs clear the environment e (modeled as a field-like latent), and see what a fresh reader inherits.
- Persistence timescale ≈ 1/h; boundary dependence appears (what counts as "the environment" matters).
- A newcomer reader without the writer's history behaves like a shadow-like observer: it sees a mixture it cannot disambiguate.
- A history-enriched model can predict a wake *by using history as a proxy for the altered environment*, so predictive improvement does not locate memory.
- Belief predictor B equalled M10 by construction in one version (my error); fixed by adding truncated-window M2/M4 comparison (B ≤ M4 ≤ M2 held).
- The CERTX branch ran an independent-style carrier test (`wake_reset_transfer.py`): analytic one-step contrast TV = .3[1−(1−h)^passes]|1−2ε|^delay; clearing gives 0; transfer invariance is **stipulated by the reader API**, not discovered.

**Status:** exact results in toy models. "Wake" is a modeling stipulation until selective interventions back it up (see section 5).

## 5. NEW: the erase-artifact problem, with imperfect interventions (exact + sampled)

**Origin.** The CERTX branch has an exact counterexample: an *environmental carrier* and a *private carrier whose reader gate is closed by the erase action* produce identical keep/erase response laws. No amount of data from those two conditions separates them. Selective probes (field swap, private clear, restore gate) can, *if each probe really is selective*.

**What I added (`erase_gate.py`, exact enumeration; sampling only in the last block with independent generators per condition):**
- Reproduced the counterexample: pure-field and gated-private worlds both give keep=.128, erase=0; naive keep−erase credits "field" to both (false positive). Ungated private gives keep=erase (correctly not field). Field+gated-private gives .256.
- Imperfect private-clear probe (success s, off-target field damage d): single-probe estimator of field effect F has bias **−d·F + (1−s)·P** (P = private share). Matched enumeration to ~1e-16. First run had F=P, which made two bias cases cancel by coincidence; re-run with F≠P (F=.128, P=.06) gives biases of +.006 to −.034 (up to ~27% of F).
- Repeated clearing k times: contrast c_k = F(1−d)^k + P(1−s)^k (two-exponential sum). Prony on k=0..3 recovers F,P,s,d exactly in the noiseless law.
- **Identifiability boundary:** when s ≈ d the decay rates coincide and F cannot be split from P. At N=200k samples per k (F≠P run): median |F̂−F| ≈ .005 at |s−d|=.8, .020 at .5, .057 at .2, ≈ .060 (= P, i.e. no information) below |s−d|≈.05.

**Assumptions not tested:** probe selectivities are stipulated; an independent swap probe is assumed to label which decay belongs to the field; the linear exactly-solvable structure is built in; a private carrier that spoofs a field-like decay would still pass. Prony for exponential-sum mixtures is standard; **no prior-art search was done** for this specific identification problem.

## 6. Conway's Life nodes (4×4 torus, all 65,536 states, exact)

Script `life_nodes.py`. Three candidate meanings of "node":
1. **Merge node / preimage fibre:** states where trajectories intersect (in-degree ≥ 2); fibre sizes vary from 0 (Gardens of Eden) to 9,628. So "fibre *bundle*" is only a metaphor; accurate term is *preimage fibres of the successor map*.
2. **Recurrent node:** states on a cycle (what trajectories end in).
3. **Behavioral node:** class of states with identical *observable sequence* for t steps (partition refinement; deterministic analogue of grouping histories by identical futures).

Findings: the three are not the same; behavioral node count depends on observable and horizon: **1** for a constant observable, **65,536** for the full state, and **249** and **1,085** for two partial observables (popcount and left-window count; see script output for which is which). Popcount is *not* lumpable: the first-order macro-model has a large defect, and defect grows with horizon.

**Deferred (user paused it):** backward partition (group states by the set of possible pasts and compare with the forward partition), glider test on a larger sampled torus, literature on CA basin-of-attraction work, Garden-of-Eden theorem, reverse-time causal states. Unchecked-from-memory names: Wuensche & Lesser; Moore–Myhill.

**Link to the CERTX branch:** their fibre-measurement pass makes the same cut: separate the *abstraction A* (what is merged now) from the *target observable O* (what future must survive): d(A,O,t) = max over merged u,v of TV(row_u(P^t O), row_v(P^t O)). With fixed O, refining A cannot increase the defect. Their joint-only toy: three one-bit projections each exact, but the joint pair has defect 1: locally adequate scores do not compose.

## 7. Thermodynamics rebuild (exact)

**Why rebuilt:** the original eight-state construction divided each row by (S_i+1), giving stationary ∝ e^{−βE}(S_i+1), not Boltzmann (errors .034 / .053 / .087 / .110 at β=.5/1/2/5, then confirmed the predicted form and the fix to ~1e-13). **Fix:** one shared normalizer Z=1.05·max row sum, leftover mass as self-loops. At zero affinity, stationary = Boltzmann exactly and EPR=0.

**Setup (`thermo2.py`):** ring of N states, rates exp(−β(E_j−E_i)/2 ± A/(2N)); observer sees only a partition. Compare true entropy production rate σ with word-statistics estimators d_n = D_n − D_{n−1}, D_n = KL(P(visible words length n) || P(reversed words)).

**Results:**
- **Hidden dissipation is partition- and observer-relative.** Binary alphabets (3-ring, 4-ring, 6-ring halves/alternating): mostly total shadow; d_n≈0 for all n despite σ>0 in the 3-ring (visible process is alternating-renewal, time-reversible).
- Ternary partitions recover part at first order and more with longer words.
- Identity: d_2 equals the first-order lumped-chain EPR; first-order lumped EPR equals the dissipation on block-boundary edges (verified decomposition).
- **My wrong prediction, corrected:** I predicted d_n monotone and ≤σ. Contradicted (ternary 2-2-2 has d_3=1.100σ). Correct invariants verified: d_n ≥ 0, D_n ≤ (n−1)σ, limit ≤ σ. The *argument* failed (it assumed a Markov reference); the *code* was fine.
- Old draft claims contradicted: "100% hidden EPR" as a general result; "matches Teza & Stella / Busiello / Esposito" (those results concern specific constructions: Teza & Stella arXiv:2003.08674; Esposito arXiv:1112.5410; Busiello et al. arXiv:1810.01833; Harunari, Dutta, Polettini, Roldán arXiv:2203.07427, author list in the draft was wrong). Hauser & Bühlmann is a loose fit only.
- Related: Hartich & Godec arXiv:2011.04628; Roldán & Parrondo arXiv:1004.2831; Skinner & Dunkel; Martínez et al. 2019. All checked at abstract/summary level only.

**Not established:** any claim that fiber spread or CERTX scores track physical entropy production. The CERTX branch itself says this.

## 8. Lumpability and intertwining (conceptual spine)

- Quotient q: histories→effective states; compression is faithful only relative to a stated behavior, horizon, intervention family and tolerance.
- Defect D_t = P^tQ − QP̃^t (intertwining/commuting-square defect). It detects disagreement; it does **not** locate the residual (erased present variable vs history vs probe vs altered environment).
- The one-block quotient is always lumpable, so lumpability needs an external relevance criterion (trivial-collapse alternative).
- Relatives: Geiger & Temmel arXiv:1212.4375 (strong k-lumpability ≈ the echo case); Rogers & Pitman intertwining (secondary verification only); Rubenstein et al. 2017 and Beckers & Halpern arXiv:1812.03789 (commuting-square causal abstraction); Barnett & Crutchfield arXiv:1412.2690; Mori–Zwanzig; niche construction, stigmergy, performative prediction, model collapse (Shumailov 2024; Gerstgrasser arXiv:2404.01413) as *related candidates with different mechanisms*, not equivalents. A 2025 paper "Coarse-Graining via Lumping" (arXiv:2512.11974) surfaced but was not opened.
