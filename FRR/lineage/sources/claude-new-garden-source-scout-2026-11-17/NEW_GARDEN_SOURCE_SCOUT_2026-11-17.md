## What this changes for the new-garden question (hypotheses, none tested here)
1. **Copy attractors are probably not specific to our setup.** 2608.23651 reports, for the very 135M checkpoint family we use, that tokens sitting in context raise their own re-emission probability, largely independent of the semantic marking. That is the same mechanism family as "copy the latest user message" in candidate38 and in the old garden. It is not the same behaviour (they study repeating a failed call; we see copying an observation packet), and nothing in the paper tests observations. Candidate test, no coaching: vary only the *form* of the latest user message (verbatim listing vs. a generated description vs. differently formatted listing) on detached copies of candidate38's saved state and read the copy rate and the log-probability of the exact-copy continuation. This changes presentation only and should be declared as a separate condition.
2. **Reducing copying is not the same as producing exploration.** The same paper found the repeat-reducing interventions did not improve task success; a decoder-level ban on repeating the latest message would break the copy and likely produce something else, not something curious. Any such intervention would be a new body/sampler condition, not an observation of the same candidate.
3. **A loop precursor may be readable from entropy**, as in 2601.05693 (entropy collapse at loop entry). Codex already measured singleton top-p support at the frozen garden's entry 1550. A per-token entropy trace over the old garden entries before 1295 would show whether the entropy of the spelling decision fell before the lock; that needs saved checkpoints or prompts, not new sampling, and it addresses the open question I left on the window-dependence note (how a run of twelve LAWS formed). Domain mismatch: those loops are in long reasoning chains of larger models.
4. **Usable versus correct format** (2605.02363) names the separation Codex's explicit-action control already found (correct JSON embedded in prose, 0/4 whole-reply actions). It suggests recording a "contains correct JSON" and a "whole-reply valid" count side by side, as Codex did, and not treating either as action.
5. **Observer-side measures of self-directed exploration** exist that do not require a reward: entropy of visited world states and empowerment-style counts (Lidayan et al.), defined over the *world's* state (files, directories, operations), not over the model's text. Their limitation, that results depend on the chosen state representation, applies to us too. In a garden whose world step stays at 0, these measures would be flat by construction, which is honest and not a failure of the measure.
6. **Where the sources are silent:** the central design question in the wind-down, passive storage versus a minimal independently changing substrate, is not addressed by anything I read. Nachkov et al. use self-generated tasks and files but no independent dynamics; Lidayan et al. use a changing world but not language models. I do not have a source for what a no-goal independently changing computational substrate does to a small pretrained model. That gap should stay explicit rather than be filled with the closest paper.
7. **Adaptation carrier:** the sources I read keep the carrier outside the weights (memory, files, an evolutionary skill learner, an LM judge) and use much larger models than 135M. I found no source in this pass on a 135M model changing behaviour through self-authored context or files. ACE and the memory-agent papers are the obvious next reads and were not read.

## Search state
Roles used: object (open-ended agents), function (self-directed goals), failure (repeat/loop), repair (description, decoder ban, prompt optimization), genealogy (autotelic to LMA3), domain translation (humans in Crafter). Not searched: independent-dynamics worlds for LMs, parametric adaptation, a rival-formulation search outside agent literature (for example cultural-transmission and cumulative-culture work). Independence of the sources: they come from different groups and methods, but all rely on instruction-tuned models in agent-like settings; the feedback-inversion and loop papers agree on "context content attracts its own continuation" through different measures, which I count as one converging direction, not proof of one mechanism.
