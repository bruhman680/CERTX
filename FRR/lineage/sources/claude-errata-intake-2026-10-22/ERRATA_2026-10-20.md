## Additions from the audits (credit to Codex, not mine)

- Observing y=0 and forcing y=0 imply different priors over the hidden bit (at h=1: [.7857, .2143] vs [.5895, .4105]); using the conditional prior for a forced reset costs .00970 nats of one-step expected KL risk.
- Visible TV can fall then rise at h=0 (signed .30, .255, .119, −.0105, −.0646, −.0705) while joint TV stays 1; no data-processing violation.
- Left-window past-support classes stay at 91 for depths 9–12 while membership changes (states 4791 and 13878 merge at depth 9, split at 10). Equal class counts do not certify backward stabilization.
- A 249-class popcount abstraction is exact for popcount at all horizons but ambiguous for left-window count in 84.87% of states at horizon 1.
