# Thermodynamic boundary scouting — 5 October 2026

Search roles: genealogy (Landauer/Bennett), failure (equating logical with thermodynamic irreversibility), repair (generalized information thermodynamics). Opened the author/institution pages below; this note does not claim a full proof audit.

- [Bennett, The thermodynamics of computation—a review (1982)](https://research.ibm.com/publications/the-thermodynamics-of-computation-a-review): physical implementations matter; reversible computation and measurement distinguish acquiring a record from erasing it. Idealized low-dissipation constructions require assumptions concerning noise, precision, speed, and implementation.
- [Sagawa, Thermodynamic and Logical Reversibilities Revisited (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.1886): generalized bounds distinguish entropy production of the total thermal system from changes in logical memory states. A logically irreversible operation can be thermodynamically reversible in a quasistatic limit while respecting Landauer-type bounds.

FRR implication (our inference): “melting a wake,” deleting a logical record, flattening a transition bias, and physically dissipating energy are different operations. No universal entropy cost follows from the wake metaphor or from a quotient map alone. A physical cost claim requires a specified device, state distribution, thermal environment, protocol, and accounting boundary.

Remaining contact: choose a concrete physical memory or computational implementation before calculating work/heat. These sources discipline the proposed energy vocabulary; they do not validate a common mechanism across Garden, neural training, and context generation.
