# Imperfect reset source scout and falsifying controls

Research date: 2026-10-13 (user-facing date). Purpose: narrow what a retain/erase/private-reset experiment can establish when interventions have leakage, side effects, or shared ancestry. Source pass plus proposed design; no new simulation executed here.

## The distinction that changes the interpretation

Let A be earlier action, E retained environmental state, P private state, U common source/nuisance conditions, and Y fresh-reader output. Candidate paths include A→E→Y, A→P→Y, U→E and U→Y, and direct reset-device effects Z→Y. If Z changes both E and P or changes the observation rule, randomizing Z identifies the total effect of the reset device, not uniquely the effect mediated by E.

An illustrative linear case makes the failure explicit. Suppose a nominal field reset produces E'=aE+uP and P'=vE+bP, and the readout is Y=cE'+dP'+rZ+noise. The observed E coefficient is ca+dv; the P coefficient is cu+db. Without known or independently calibrated intervention coefficients, readout coefficients, and device effects, the same measured contrast can be explained by multiple locations of persistence. More samples shrink sampling error but do not resolve that population non-identifiability.

Use controlled, explicitly implemented interventions to ask whether a field can carry an effect after private reset. Do not label such a contrast a natural indirect effect or a uniquely reconstructed historical route without additional assumptions. A retained-field effect can coexist with private persistence or with a source common to both. The available intervention family defines the scope of the attribution.

## A falsifying control family

1. **Factorial carrier intervention.** Cross private keep/reset with field keep/erase. Clone the same pre-intervention snapshot across all four cells and replay the same externally drawn noise. This isolates contrasts in the toy's specified causal structure. Record every mutated variable, including caches, clocks, RNG states, decoder parameters, and persistent files. Both-off should eliminate action discrimination if these are the only carriers.
2. **Reset verification, not reset label.** Seed private state and field with independent sentinel payloads unrelated to the task. Probe each separately after every intervention. Report deletion fidelity, cross-carrier leakage, and reader integrity. A targeted reset should remove its sentinel while preserving the other. Sentinel pass only validates those channels; it does not prove arbitrary hidden channels are absent.
3. **Sham and device-only controls.** Run identical handling, allocation, synchronization, and timing without deleting the task carrier. Apply erase to a blank field or a field with unrelated content. If output changes there, the erase procedure has a direct effect or shifts a nuisance variable. This falsifies a claim that the whole keep/erase difference is mediated by the task trace.
4. **Rescue and transplant.** After erasure, restore only the saved field in an independently initialized reader environment. Action discrimination should return if the saved field is sufficient. Restore a matched field from the opposite action as a cross-over: output should follow the transplanted payload rather than donor identity, reset label, or original action. Sham restore and empty restore control handling effects. Rescue alone is still inconclusive if restoration also copies private metadata.
5. **Break source ancestry.** Generate actions independently of background fields, readers, labels, and random streams. Cross donor action with independently generated environment and reader identities; hold out complete donor/reader families rather than only trajectories. Include pre-action snapshots: if those already predict action, the design leaks or has common-source confounding.
6. **Fresh reader and no-write control.** Destroy/reinstantiate the focal actor, create a reader with no shared private state, and prohibit further writes during the readout. An actor that only observes but does not deposit a trace should not create the proposed field-mediated action signal. A later reader can otherwise reconstruct a wake during the test itself.
7. **Two reset implementations.** Compare independent mechanisms with measured, different side-effect profiles: direct field replacement versus serialization into a clean process, for example. Agreement supports robustness only if their ancestry and failure channels differ. Two wrappers around the same reset primitive are not independent controls.
8. **Imperfect-reset sweep.** Independently vary private deletion failure probability, field deletion failure probability, cross-carrier perturbation, direct reader damage, and common-source correlation. Predeclare an attribution rule, then score false field attributions as well as true detections. Include adversarial worlds where the field carries no action information but the reset damages a private-state readout. This can produce a keep/erase difference while the proposed wake mechanism is absent.

## Measurement plan and meaningful failure

Population targets should be specified first: distributions of Y under each verified joint intervention, or action-decoding accuracy on fresh held-out readers. If the toy has enumerable discrete states, calculate these distributions exactly before sampling. With imperfect operations, report the effect of the actual operation and sensitivity bounds across plausible leakage/side-effect parameters; do not substitute an intended perfect reset as the estimand.

Finite-sample estimates then need paired uncertainty intervals, independent action assignments and repetitions, and separation of threshold calibration from final evaluation. Correctly paired noise reduces variance; it is not independent empirical replication. A non-significant contrast does not prove absence; equivalence requires a declared tolerance and sufficient precision. A large contrast does not solve an identification gap.

A field attribution should fail or become unresolved when: field sentinel survives erasure unexpectedly; private sentinel is altered by field erase; blank-field erase changes outputs; no-write/pre-action fields predict action; swapped payloads fail to swap predictions; or a private-only rival produces the same diagnostic signature. Failure can preserve a useful reset tool while invalidating the interpretation placed on its effect.

## Next useful test

Build a small explicit factorial toy with ground-truth environmental and private carriers, then hide the ground truth from the attribution rule. Randomize reset fidelity and off-target reader damage. Compare a naive retain-minus-erase rule against a rule that requires sham, sentinel, and transplant checks. Measure how often each calls a private-only world an environmental wake. This changes the information available to the investigator rather than merely generating another successful field-memory example.
