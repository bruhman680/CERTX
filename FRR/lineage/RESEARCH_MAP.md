## The preserved inquiry

Can earlier action alter a substrate so that later, newly introduced actors encounter its consequences without inheriting the earlier observer's records? When does this become recoverable memory, maintained coordination, or a robust restriction on future behavior?

The literature supports several established relatives. It does not supply one universal wake-to-law threshold.

## Sources that materially change the seed

[Reid et al. (2012)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3491460/) manipulated extracellular slime in Physarum navigation. This is empirical contact with an external carrier in a particular task, not evidence of an internally stateless organism.

[Erhard, Franco, and Reis (2022)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10955-022-03031-0) prove eventual circuit trapping for a specified accumulating directed-reinforcement walk. Positive finite-time transitions can coexist with eventual trapping when reinforcement is unbounded. That mechanism does not transfer to a uniformly bounded, decaying field. [Cotar and Limic](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0612048) further distinguish almost-sure eventual attraction from manageable waiting times.

[Keim and Nagel (2011)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.2931) and [Paulsen et al. (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.4117) connect model and experimental multiple transient memories. Noise can stabilize otherwise disappearing distinctions in their systems. These share a research lineage; they do not establish that arbitrary perturbation improves reasoning.

[Chorin, Hald, and Kupferman](https://math.huji.ac.il/~razk/Publications/PDF/CHK00.pdf) provide a mathematical route from unresolved variables to reduced memory terms. [Shalizi and Crutchfield](https://arxiv.org/html/cond-mat/9907176v2) instead define predictive states through whole-future distributions. Both discipline faithful compression, while answering different questions.

[StreamingLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17453) shows attention sinks can support stable streaming. [Learning to Break the Loop](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/148c0aeea1c5da82f4fa86a09d4190da-Abstract-Conference.html) studies repetition reinforcement and repair. Their separation matters: attention concentration alone does not identify repetition's mechanism.

## Next contact suggested by the weave

Extend the existing substrate-witness toy into a predeclared discrimination experiment. Randomize two histories and initial substrate states; make immediate coarse summaries match; vary delay and maintenance. After resetting the writer, give fresh readers substrate only, earlier record only, both, or mismatched records. Cross these with carrier erasure, sham erasure, and probe presence. Fit any decoder only on calibration cases and freeze it before held-out evaluation. Compare a simple readout and a serious rival explanation based on initial preparation.

Measure two outcomes separately: recovery of the earlier distinction and changes in subsequent actions. A readable trace can fail to control behavior; a consequential inherited environment can fail to reveal its unique history. This proposed test has not been executed by the literature scout. The earlier toy remains a constructed mechanism demonstration.

## Dormant branches and search limits

Traveling waves and continual regeneration need their own substrate-specific dynamics. Physical geometry, graph accessibility, and literal topology are not synonyms. A full theorem for the supplied decaying graph model requires its complete coupled feedback Jacobian and explicit update stability conditions. FRR model-performance evaluation remains unperformed. No claim of novelty or an established open problem is made.
