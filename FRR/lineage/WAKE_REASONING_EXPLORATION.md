## Surviving generative movement

Trace -> preferential return -> reinforcement -> durable restriction -> effective background rule. The last step can change the description without changing the underlying physics: an evolving field becomes a parameter for a task whose horizon makes field evolution negligible. A riverbed is a boundary for a short flow calculation and a state for erosion modeling.

Retain three separate forms of crystallization: dynamically durable influence, task-relative behavioral restriction, and an interface made read-only. A frozen parameter file illustrates operational fixity; it does not by itself establish an infinite dynamical barrier or inflexible behavior.

## Operational boundary with a margin

In a two-choice softmax, a retained advantage Delta produces escape probability 1/(1+exp(beta*Delta)). If all reachable histories and admitted interventions preserve Delta >= Delta_min over T decisions, a union bound gives P(any escape) <= T*exp(-beta*Delta_min). Beta*Delta_min >= log(T/epsilon) is sufficient for an epsilon-effective restriction in this specific model.

The constructed fixed-gap example Delta=5,beta=2 gives about .453% probability of at least one escape in 100 independent choices, but about 36.491% in 10,000. The route never becomes exactly inaccessible. Computation is recorded in `evaluation/results/exploration-contact-2026-10-05.json`. Independence is needed for those exact repeated-choice probabilities, not for the union bound with uniform conditional bounds.

## Optimizer residue

Update-history consistency can be useful without being a curvature or correctness detector. Use the same normalized nonnegative temporal weights a_k for m=sum(a_k*g_k) and v=sum(a_k*g_k^2). Then m^2/(v+epsilon) lies in [0,1] by weighted Cauchy–Schwarz. A vector analogue uses squared norm of the weighted vector mean divided by weighted squared norms. The repair bounds the statistic; it does not determine whether consistent descent is useful or harmful.

Two quadratics f_a(x)=a*x^2/2 at x=1/a have identical initial gradient 1 but arbitrarily different curvature. Alignment alone cannot choose a universally safe step. Use trial loss change, measured directional curvature, or held-out behavior as additional discriminating contact. Momentum is optimizer state; weights are another persistence carrier. Reset them separately.

## Cross-substrate interface

Compare write, retain, read, reset, and repair operations rather than claiming identical physical mechanics. Reader sensitivity changes can suppress a retained mark; carrier edits can erase or rewrite it; new paths can bypass it. Restore the reader and test rebound to distinguish them. A mark also needs a later reader to carry an effect: actor substitution tests inheritance beyond a particular actor's history.

Persistence may arise through renewal rather than retention of one mark. An agent might regenerate an obsolete statement from summaries or retrieval even after one copy is deleted. Compare deleting the local mark with disabling its reconstruction routes, within a declared system boundary. This remains a candidate experiment, not an observed mechanism here.
