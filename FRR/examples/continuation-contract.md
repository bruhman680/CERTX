# Optional compact continuation contract

Use only when a consequential handoff or bounded artifact task needs it. Existing records may supply every field through links; do not duplicate a full protocol or impose this on open exploration. This is a human/agent-readable example, not a machine schema or automatic controller.

```text
Purpose and acceptance: requested task; exact quantity/check that permits completion.
Current status: supported under stated conditions / contradicted / unresolved.
Evidence and access: exact versions/paths; what was actually checked or loaded; missing input.
Assumptions and limits: supplied authority/calibration/roles; known transfer or scope limits.
Change and repair: old result; what invalidated reuse; proposed or verified replacement, if any.
Next action and stop: one discriminating step; authorized budget; completion/pause/reopening condition.
```

Do not force uncertainty into a false verdict, record a proposed correction as verified, or infer semantic truth from hashes. A reviewer or fresh agent should inspect the cited artifact when the next action depends on it. A changed acceptance target requires reassessment rather than silent reuse of an old pass. Public rationale/evidence suffice; private internal reasoning transcripts are not required.

Basis and limits: [artifact harness](../modules/artifact-execution-harness.md), [context checkpoints](../modules/context-checkpoints.md), [verification layer](../modules/verification-layer.md), [RB report](../evaluation/reference-bias-contact-v1/REPORT.md). Usable guidance; behavioral benefit and optimal compression remain untested.
