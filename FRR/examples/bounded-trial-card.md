# Bounded trial card

Copy for a measurable trial, or link an existing protocol containing these fields. Leave exploratory notes elsewhere when no comparison is intended. Reference source artifacts instead of copying their contents into every card.

- **Identity and timing:** stable trial ID; parent/candidate reference; prospective or retrospective record. Mark later amendments explicitly.
- **Question and prediction:** what changes, what consequence is expected, what would count against it; distinguish an attribution test from a joint configuration search.
- **Fixed comparison:** competent baseline; evaluator and data revisions; development versus held-out exposure. Record whether the actor has access to expected answers.
- **Budget:** planned calls/time/compute and relevant constraints; actual measured consumption and measurement method. Write unknown when not measured.
- **Evidence:** durable references to candidate content, evaluator, raw outputs and errors; environment/settings/seed where relevant. Record missing inputs. A filename or hash does not certify scientific validity.
- **Outcome and decision:** observed components separately, uncertainty or failed execution, acceptance rule, and whether to advance, retain an alternative, repair, or defer. Simplicity may matter when performance is comparable; state the tradeoff rather than silently changing the rule.
- **Continuation and repair:** next authorized action; what survives rollback; amendments and affected trials. An evaluator repair gets a new version and a record of which earlier decisions need reassessment. Preserve the original evaluation.

Preserve a rejected candidate's patch or immutable revision and its evidence before restoring working code. Keep archive status separate from selected-working-version status. Independently prepared confirmation remains necessary before extending a development claim.

The [continuation-pilot retrospective record](bounded-loop-retrospective.json) shows how the existing inquiry schema can reference a trial and an evaluator repair without introducing another mandatory schema.

For a consequential claim or delivery, use the optional [verification checkpoint](../modules/verification-layer.md): identify the claim/scope, criterion, evidence and checker; retain supported, contradicted or unresolved status with assumptions. Existing protocol fields can supply this record. Preserve checker/evaluator revisions and distinguish a completed check from a supported claim. Open exploration does not require a separate form.
