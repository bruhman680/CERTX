## Required corrections before implementation

- Resolve the evaluation memo's label **“Authored world event”** and description **“authored fact within that branch.”** In a record without canonical story truth, these could silently give an observer authority to establish fictional facts. Rename it “authored narrative contribution,” or explicitly say it identifies the author's apparent local framing only. A preserved utterance is a substrate fact; its narrated content acquires no host-certified truth. Contradiction annotations must never determine admission, rewriting, future access or rewards.
- Preserve root's explicit choice of a full-history first window. Learning/environment memos offer recent-record selection and recall; those remain alternatives for a later design. Importing either into the first implementation would change the evidence carrier and defeat the stated continuity interpretation. The blueprint should name the actual input bound and pause condition before any omission occurs.

## Optional alternatives and unresolved limits

An independent symbolic substrate remains an optional different world, not a prerequisite for worthwhile expression. Record order, exact quotation and reference already permit narrow checks; they do not imply causal-law discovery. If those opportunities never arise voluntarily, the result remains expressive history, not an ability failure.

Prefer one optional consequence-sensitive contact arising from actual play over an early suite of transfer tasks. Keep offered, accepted and interpretable outcomes separate; matching budgets does not cure unequal participation. The observer may learn that the interface is inaccessible without thereby learning that the model lacks the underlying capacity. Stop handling and host advance cues remain concrete blueprint decisions; a mandatory response or examiner-defined progress threshold would undo the current protection.
