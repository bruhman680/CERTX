## Five premises under pressure

**One understood system → other systems.** This omits the bridge: which structure is conserved, which representation carries it, and how the new system makes it accessible? A symbol-renaming success, novel composition, changed dynamics and a new action interface are different transfers. Lake and Baroni's [SCAN study](https://arxiv.org/html/1711.00350v3), §§2–4, supplies a concrete rival: supervised recurrent sequence models succeed on random held-out commands yet struggle when familiar primitives must enter systematically withheld compositions. They trained command-to-action mappings, searched recurrent architectures and evaluated distinct splits. This does not predict SmolLM2's result; it shows why familiar-domain competence cannot itself establish the missing bridge. Preserve a narrow transfer hypothesis with named changes, not universality.

**Language fluency → understanding.** Bender and Koller's [position paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.463.pdf), §§1,3–5, argues that form-only prediction lacks the relation to communicative intent required by their definition of meaning. This is a conceptual argument with thought experiments, not an empirical small-model failure result. Its useful challenge is definitional: fluent continuation, world-state discrimination and human-like understanding must not become synonyms. Its scope also prevents using it to dismiss formal learning or an interactive environment supplying new consequences.

The positive rival matters equally. Li et al.'s [Othello study](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.13382v5), §§2–4, trains an eight-layer GPT from random initialization on move transcripts, including a large synthetic legal-game dataset. Nonlinear probes recover board state; interventions on activations change legal-move predictions toward counterfactual boards. Sequence prediction can support a causally useful state representation in a bounded domain. This is not frozen-weight in-context discovery, natural-language communicative grounding, strategic play or transfer to unrelated systems. Also, the paper explicitly engages Bender/Koller; these are interacting arguments, not independent votes on one operational claim.

**Persistent story → autonomous world.** A durable inscription is genuinely external history. It need not be an independently evolving world. If every narrated event becomes true merely because it was said, apparent prediction may reduce to continuing a self-authored story. Preserve three records: authored utterance, substrate fact, and any process that changes substrate without the next utterance. The substrate can stay very simple. Append order, exact retrievability and link resolution already make predictions answerable against something outside current prose. They support archive literacy; they do not establish discovery of autonomous dynamics. At fixed weights, continuity may reside in supplied history or external storage rather than changed parameters.

**Coding failure → absent understanding.** The reports already resist this inference: plain arithmetic contains 12; one program actually writes four bytes; the sorting fragment never executes. Instruction compliance, executable transport, runtime assumptions and requested outcome separate. Tutorial-like code might reflect elicitation or learned habits, but neither its training origin nor an inner state is established. These single-reply cases do not test use of feedback or continuous symbol exploration. Conversely, fluent language cannot be credited as recovery from a code problem it never confronted.

**Training percentages → capacities.** Corpus token/row shares describe exposure under particular counting conventions. They are not proportions of representational capacity or measured skill. lg_training's cross-review narrows the surviving design implication appropriately: keep plain English available and avoid making code the sole entry route. The rival remains that familiar English could elicit assistant/tutorial habits rather than sustained voluntary exploration. Familiarity motivates accessibility as a hypothesis, not a language-garden advantage proven over code. The reconstructed percentages in the brief retain their curator and denominator caveats.

## One observation that could change the plan

Proposal only: after voluntary play produces an inspectable relation involving the model's own marks, preserve a checkpoint and compare two detached continuations. Both receive truthful substrate observations; one retains the relation and one changes a single structural fact, such as the adjacency of two marks, through a declared world operation. Keep budget, seed, history carrier and observation format matched; provide no source solution or instruction to discover the perturbation. Predeclare which spontaneously expressed expectation would differ and record all text and actual effects.

Discriminating result: does subsequent voluntary expression or action track the changed relation, or repeat the same claim despite visible contrary substrate? Difference would justify investigating consequence-sensitive state use; repetition would redirect attention toward observation accessibility and archive design. No relevant expression is an uninformative opportunity, not a failed obedience trial. One fork pair cannot establish mechanism or general transfer. Leave free play intact, keep scoring outside the model's instructions, and do not turn participation into a gate. First test whether history matters locally; only then ask what survives a change of system.
