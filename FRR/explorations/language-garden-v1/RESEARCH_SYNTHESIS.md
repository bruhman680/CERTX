## Learning and environment

The two mechanism/design reviewers distinguish durable external records, exact model-visible context and changed parameters. A saved story can support later input; it does not update weights. [Few-shot context work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165), [external memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.08913) and [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) illustrate distinct mechanisms on other systems; none demonstrates this garden's effectiveness or CPU-training feasibility.

**Decision:** recommend an append-only authored inscription space first. Every contribution remains recoverable; contradictions, hypotheses and invented events do not need semantic approval. Writing changes the durable record and possibly future input, not automatic external truth. An independent symbolic-state world remains an alternative if independently determined consequences are essential. We preserve this substantive choice rather than asserting the record alone satisfies every interpretation of “world.”

**Resolved architectural difference:** learning/environment memos offered exact recent records plus omission notices and optional ID recall. Root selects FULL exact history for a small initial window, pausing before a next call would exceed the serialized input bound with reserved generation capacity. Recent-window, retrieval, summaries and branching engines are later proposals, not silently selected components. All original departmental alternatives remain in their memos.

**Still open:** exact host advance cue, stop handling, first observation-window size and which independent consequences, if any, should exist initially. EOS alone is not a chosen rest. A model that receives no independent outcomes can still be observed for expression/record use; it cannot validate its own causal theories just by continuing them. A pure inscription substrate does offer narrow facts about append order, accessible text and references. These support a narrower claim than discovery of autonomous external dynamics.

Actual adapter training remains conditional: define examples, objective, gradients, optimization and saved changes; retain an original/no-update comparator and test fresh-context use and degradation. Preserve self-generated ancestry rather than treating repetitions as independent evidence. [Recursive-data study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11269175/) supplies a protocol-specific concern, not a theorem against every synthetic example.

## Generalization and measurement

The symbolic-transfer scout found evidence for partial nonlinguistic pattern transfer by larger pretrained models, with important dependence on representation, tokenization and supplied demonstrations. It does not establish the135M body's performance or that language understanding is the cause. [Mirchandani et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04721).

SCAN supplies a countercondition: familiar-split performance can coexist with severe novel-composition failures. It studies supervised recurrent models, so it cannot predict this model's result. [Lake and Baroni](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00350).

The challenge reviewer balances a conceptual warning about equating linguistic form with meaning against a bounded empirical example where sequence prediction yields useful board-state representations and interventions. These have different definitions and procedures; they are not votes on the same claim. [Bender and Koller](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.463/), [Li et al., Othello](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13382).

**Decision:** retain the broad language-to-systems idea as research motivation. Operationalize a particular bridge before claiming transfer. A proposed later contact pairs new symbols preserving a relation with a near-target changing one operation, holding target evidence fixed and separating source-history access from no-source/irrelevant-history controls. Correct target behavior alone establishes a conditional behavior, not language-caused acquisition, parameter learning or universal understanding. Participation and interpretability must be reported separately; no response is not an ability failure.

**Decision:** begin with a natural observation window, not a compulsory benchmark. Frame-aware annotations stay outside the model conversation and can remain unresolved. Record integrity and semantic assessment are different audits. Freeze any later prediction/held-out family before revealing answers; repeated prompt tuning against the same test can undermine evaluation. The adaptive-analysis and dataset-replication papers are methodological cautions, not garden performance guarantees. [Dwork et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629), [Recht et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00451).

## Cross-department correction

The first evaluation memo used “authored fact within that branch.” Environment and challenge reviewers identified a conflict with a record-first world having no automatic canonical story truth. Evaluation replaced it with “authored narrative contribution” and made the local interpretation provisional. Its example and full-history policy were clarified. Root accepts that revision: no annotation determines admission, deletion, reward or semantic access to the inhabitant's history.

This correction is a concrete benefit of cross-review in this session. It does not demonstrate that FRR or a swarm improves performance generally. Direct peer routing initially failed for the training/challenge exchange; root relayed it, and the pair subsequently completed its source-scope review. Paired review and cross-department review share prompts, sources and research ancestry.

Ready for discussion: a separate GitHub design section, an explicit candidate seed, a record-first recommendation, scoped alternatives, source-backed mechanism distinctions, external observation principles and a recoverable next-session plan.

Not yet built or tested: a new language runtime, turn scheduler, recall tool, new state substrate, language pilot, model training or transfer trial.
