## Mandatory implementation clarifications

1. **Stop interpretation needs a frame boundary.** A fictional character saying “stop,” a quotation, a hypothetical refusal and a resident-addressed request to end the interaction are different. Preserve the actual span and provisional interpretation. If uncertainty motivates a pause, record an operator precaution, not a confirmed model preference. Do not use a keyword detector, mandatory STOP form or a semantic agreement test as admission machinery. A contribution persists regardless.

2. **Freeze contact eligibility and observer feedback.** In any later optional contact, externally specify what counts as offered, engaged, ambiguous and interpretable before evaluating outcomes. These labels classify evidence; they do not determine whether prose enters history. Do not selectively prompt again to obtain participation or a scoreable prediction. A fixed-law receipt is independently produced evidence; an observer's semantic grade or repair suggestion is a new intervention requiring attribution.

3. **Make the side identifiability condition precise.** A permitted informative message or interaction must be capable of distinguishing the target given fixed nonmessage evidence. The receiver need not identify it before communication. Separately assess whether the actual transcript identifies it. Impossibility concerns the FULL allowed information, transcripts and operations, not bare graph symmetry. Two admissible worlds indistinguishable throughout those opportunities cannot require uniquely recoverable different answers. New anchors must be equally available in controls and target-independent; one anchor may leave residual ambiguity. Negotiating a new correspondence is not recovering the original hidden one.

## Unresolved design alternatives

One reply versus a continuing finite window, and expression versus independent mechanics or dialogue, remain substantive choices. Neither source study selects them for this body. English may immediately suffice for communication; retain that legitimate outcome without naming language emergence. Equal token budgets cannot equate engagement, actual information or host calls. Nonparticipation and absent predictions remain missing evidence. These limitations warrant a clearly frozen next specification, not compulsory tasks, a quality curriculum or further side expansion.
