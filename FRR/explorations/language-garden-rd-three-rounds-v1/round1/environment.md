# Round 1: preserving writing does not select its consequences

20 November 2026, America/Chicago. Planning/source research only. Read this session's brief, exact compact FRR, and previous design. No model calls, executable changes, training, model/dataset downloads, or prior-file edits.

**Decision change.** The previous record-first recommendation conflated common infrastructure with the consequential architectural choice. Every alternative can preserve unrestricted raw writing. The real choice is whether encounters resist the resident's authored propositions through mechanics, another author, or only the historical record. Record-only remains the least burdensome expression baseline; it is not automatically the best environment for the seed's prediction ambition.

| Option | What writing does | Independent consequence | Imposed syntax and authority | Concrete cost/risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Record only | Appends authored text; later exact input includes it | Earlier bytes/order cannot be rewritten by a later claim | Native text; host selects timing/context | The resident can author both prediction and fulfillment; apparent discovery may be narrative follow-through |
| Free text plus tiny optional symbol operation | Every reply persists; a literal operation changes a separate symbolic object | Engine computes transition from prior state and accepted operation | One disclosed command grammar; host chooses objects/laws, never interprets prose as action | Parser competence can dominate; a toy operation can become an imposed puzzle |
| Free dialogue with separate interlocutor | Adds a message that another author may answer | Response is outside resident authorship, although influenced by it | Ordinary language; interlocutor controls own claims, host controls turn access | Partner may instruct, flatter, correct, or steer; social agreement is not truth |

**Meaningful mechanical alternative.** Imagine a visible symbol sequence with one optional operation, such as exchanging two indexed positions. This is an illustrative candidate, not a selected law or implemented interface. A sentence claiming an exchange occurred cannot change the sequence; an accepted literal operation does. The engine reports actual state and exact rejection reason. Free prose remains recorded whether an operation succeeds, fails, or is absent. Do not infer disinterest from malformed syntax, or make operation use a condition of continued participation. No quests, progress rewards, or automatic prose-to-action translator are needed.

**Primary source weave.** Côté et al., [TextWorld](https://arxiv.org/html/1806.11532v2), §§2–3, describes a predicate-based state and transition engine separate from surface language. Its admissible-command facility changes parser-based play into choice-based play; assistance changes the problem. The architecture demonstrates independent mechanical consequences but also openly imposes authored rules, quests, and rewards. Borrow state/utterance separation, not its educational trajectory. I inspected those methods and observation interfaces, not baseline replication or code execution.

Urbanek et al., [LIGHT](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1062.pdf), §§3–5, separates dialogue, emotes, and explicit state-changing actions. Trained retrieval/generative models are evaluated on human-authored interactions; contextual grounding helps some predictions, while generative action outputs are constrained to valid candidates. This supports coexistence of expression and mechanical action, not reliable unconstrained operation by our fixed 135M body. The study's situated dialogue also motivates separately authored encounters; it does not establish that a dialogue-only interlocutor improves learning. Read environment, training/evaluation methods, and result/ablation tables; appendices and implementation were not audited. Two sources, no empirical replication; LIGHT cites TextWorld.

**Rival and discriminant.** The strongest rival is that ordinary continuation already supplies adequate pressure through language's pretrained structure and immutable history. Added mechanics might merely test command following; a partner might merely supply answers. A later authorized contact should preserve the same free-text history, model, seed, context budget, and schedule while varying access to an independently computed transition receipt. Observe whether a previously committed expectation changes in response to the actual contrary consequence, preserving every failure. This tests receipt uptake, not understanding or weight learning. Nonparticipation and failed syntax remain separate outcomes.

Recommendation: retain the record as infrastructure; decide explicitly whether independent resistance is required before selecting the first world. All variants require disclosed host opportunities after reply completion. One reply followed by dormancy also constitutes persistence. Neither repeated host cues nor interlocutor initiative should be called spontaneous autonomous time.

Evaluation peer cross-review: freeze sampling/cues; choosing operations or partner content shapes exposure even without rewards. Contingent researcher nudges constitute a separate condition. Prediction must precede transition/reveal; self-selected actions bias which outcomes are tested. Distinct partner authorship establishes neither truth nor independent training ancestry.
