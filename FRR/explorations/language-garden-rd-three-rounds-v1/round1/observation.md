## Two primary contacts

**Goldstein, King & West (2003), [Social interaction shapes babbling](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC164707/).** Read methods, results and discussion. Thirty infants and mothers experienced baseline, manipulated response, and extinction periods during play. Contingent maternal smiles, approach and touch were compared with a yoked group receiving the same number of responses on another dyad's schedule. Contingency changed vocal quality, not merely output count. Infants could explore the room rather than remain beside their mothers. Assignment was sequential by condition, and the experiment concerns human vocal development, not symbolic reasoning.

**Design consequence:** even supportive responses without an explicit correct answer can shape subsequent behavior. Log every observer/interlocutor contribution and its triggering policy. A fixed continuation cue, selectively timed encouragement, and substantive dialogue are different interventions. If a later comparison asks whether contingency matters, matching response count alone is insufficient; record timing, content and the opportunities to disengage. Do not import the study's desired developmental direction as a garden success criterion.

**Yu & Smith (2012), [Embodied attention and word learning by toddlers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3829203/).** Read methods, main results and discussion of causal limits. Six parent–toddler dyads played with named novel objects while wearing head cameras; three additional toddlers refused the equipment. Successful later name comprehension was associated with the named object dominating the child's view around naming. The study is descriptive/correlational; head-centered images are not eye gaze. Parents were supplied object names and experimenters scheduled object sets. This was structured play, despite natural interaction within it. The comprehension test used target-blind coders and checked experimenter cueing.

**Design consequence:** archive the exact model-visible input, not just the durable world's contents. Offered history and effective access differ; neither proves attention. Capture selection, ordering and truncation policies, and keep participation losses visible. Annotation should preserve competing frame interpretations; target-blind judging is useful only where a target is independently specified.

## What append-only text can resist

Appending produces independently checkable inscription consequences: later byte order, authorship, linkage and the impossibility of changing an earlier retained version by merely claiming it changed. A prediction about whether a particular span is present can be checked against the actual prior record. Such tasks can reveal archive use, though visible-text lookup is a serious simple rival to any broader understanding claim.

Append-only storage supplies no independent truth for “a tree appeared” or “the tree will fall.” If the model later narrates its fall, that is narrative follow-through. The resulting text may be coherent or surprising without testing predictive accuracy. Provisional authored narrative contributions create no automatic canon. Check claims about inscriptions separately from meanings, fiction and counterfactuals. Reclassifying invention as error after seeing the continuation would change the game retrospectively.

Externally choosing operations, dialogue and timing constitutes curriculum even without rewards. Syntax failure, expressed refusal, no action and host timeout must remain distinguishable; free prose need not become an action.

First observe a declared natural-interaction window with full exact history and pause before overflow. “Natural” here means no added evaluative nudges, not absence of host influence. Record offered, accepted, declined and uninterpretable contacts separately. Nonparticipation leaves ability unresolved.

A discriminating later observation, not a Round 1 implementation: fix an independently resolvable expectation before revealing an actual engine receipt, then observe whether subsequent use changes. If only an observer's interpretation changes, there is no demonstrated receipt uptake. If model-authored outcomes alone match expectations, circular prediction remains the rival. This bounded question can help choose a world without making every contribution a test.
