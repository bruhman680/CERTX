# Research results and remaining design choices

20 November 2026, America/Chicago. Source research and design review, not a new behavioral result. Three main rounds were actually sequential: each later assignment received frozen artifacts and decisions from the earlier round. A separate scout ran in parallel and widened once after root source inspection and critical review. All original drafts and disagreement/revision records remain available.

## Round1: separate the carrier from the consequence

The environment team challenged the old record-first recommendation. Every candidate world can preserve unrestricted raw writing. What differs is the source of resistance: earlier inscription bytes/order, a declared mechanical transition, or another author's response. Record-only is an expression baseline, not an automatically sufficient world for independently resolved prediction.

TextWorld supplies a formal state/transition layer behind language; LIGHT distinguishes dialogue from explicit actions. Their authored goals, learning procedures and constrained interfaces are not imported as evidence of fixed135M competence. Human interaction studies sharpen a separate concern: timing and content of observer responses can shape exposure without explicit correctness rewards. These are architectural/methodological contacts, not developmental equivalence to a model. Read scopes and limits are in round1/environment.md and observation.md.

The continuity/source team separated native chat, assistant-prefill and raw streams. All require host-chosen scheduling and input assembly. Removing an emitted EOS is different from resuming a host-truncated fragment. The native template carries an inherited helpful-assistant frame even if the user seed is the only substantive invitation. Cached metadata declares8192 positions; the existing body uses7936 input plus256 output. These limits do not establish useful retention or understanding over that horizon.

**Decision change:** immutable record is shared infrastructure; world type, independent consequences and host opportunities require separate choices. Round1/DECISIONS.json freezes the source artifacts and the exact questions passed downstream.

## Round2: make a minimal candidate explicit

The second round used those findings to specify a one-reply/manual baseline and a continuing candidate capped at four opportunities, counting the first. The candidate retains the native system and exact user-proposed symbol seed, then uses the literal host cue “Another turn is available.” That cue can influence behavior and is visibly attributed to the host. Four is a proposed resource limit, not a developmental timetable or evidence that four replies fit usefully.

No compulsory STOP word, response schema, recall command, branch engine, teacher, semantic approval or automatic parameter update is introduced. All returned expression persists, including apparent nonsense, code, contradictions and hypotheses. Full decoded history is re-rendered; raw IDs are separately archived. Context capacity is checked on the complete template rather than character counts. A token-cap or literal-empty reply pauses the window without a content retry.

Departmental language initially called for a human checkpoint before later calls. Root clarified that an operator checkpoint within an authorized finite window is an integrity/capacity/scheduling check, not a fresh user permission request or a judgment that the prose is sufficiently good. Observers can pause with attribution; absence of a refusal cannot establish willingness. Round2 drafts retain their original wording, while CANDIDATE.md records the integration.

Mechanical and interlocutor contacts remain separately declared alternatives. A receipt can offer independently determined evidence; a second author can disagree or clarify without being a truth oracle. Neither automatically supplies learning or independent training ancestry. Optional probes must not quietly become conditions for continued garden access.

## Round3: find the implementation and inference gaps

Three reviewers audited the actual frozen candidate and blueprints. They accepted the operator-checkpoint clarification and found no semantic admission gate in the integrated proposal. The following changes are preserved in REVISIONS.json and REVIEWED_BLUEPRINT.md.

**Stop frames.** A resident-addressed pause request differs from a fictional character saying stop, a quotation or a hypothetical. Interpretations remain provisional. An uncertainty pause is an operator action, not confirmed model preference. Every returned contribution remains admitted regardless of that reading.

**Interrupted-output recovery.** The inherited generation source accumulates IDs in memory, runs a final guard, then returns. It has no durable per-token journal. Cancellation or exception can therefore make generated partial output unavailable. Earlier preservation wording was a desired property, not a demonstrated capability. Before activation choose an honest fail-closed limitation or separately tested journal. Never substitute an empty resident message for an unknown trace or replay to fill it silently.

**Empty and replay policy.** The reviewed proposal uses a literally zero-character decoded string for empty. Whitespace-only text remains a nonempty inscription, not a quality failure or proof of preference. Archive raw EOS while disclosing that native decoded-history replay removes special tokens and inserts host message boundaries. This is not original-token replay. Exact runtime, model/checkpoint, renderer and decoder pins still need implementation freeze.

**Information adequacy.** A communication task must allow some informative message or interaction to distinguish alternatives given fixed nonmessage evidence; the receiver need not know the target before receiving a message. Then assess the actual transcript separately. Symmetry must be evaluated relative to ALL permitted evidence, history and operations. If two admissible cases remain indistinguishable but require different answers, neither more confidence nor another language label repairs the missing information. An orbit/set outcome or target-independent anchor is a different, predeclared task.

The audits validate a reviewable design and expose limits; they do not validate model behavior, transfer or learning. Round3/DECISIONS.json freezes their inputs and accepted corrections.

## Side trajectory: genuinely different starting assumptions

The exploratory role proposed three mechanisms: two asymmetric contexts exchanging symbols, a deterministic transformation workbench, and an archive organized around revisitable artifacts without a stable protagonist. None was required to preserve the record-first recommendation as its world selection. All retained the user's purpose and the distinction between hypotheses and evidence.

The strongest lead was information-asymmetric exchange. [Lazaridou et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07182) studies communicating networks trained through rewarded games, not optional frozen-weight SmolLM2 contexts. Root inspected the setup/training/results and the critical reviewer checked major rivals before permitting one further source contact. The bridge concerns an environment in which messages can change what another context knows. No efficacy or new-language claim was promoted.

The adjacent contact was [Clark and Wilkes-Gibbs, Referring as a collaborative process](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1980s/Clark,%20H.H.%20_%20Wilkes-Gibbs,%20D.%20_Referring%20as%20a%20collaborative%20process_%201986.pdf). Its repeated human task documents negotiated reference and repair under an explicit shared goal, feedback and existing human capacities. It does not isolate optional repair as the cause of success or establish transfer to this model.

**Changed proposal:** leave a reciprocal clarification channel available instead of assuming a one-message exchange suffices. Do not require agreement, force invented language, ban useful English, import timed goals or make game success a progression gate. Root's graph countercondition further required identifiability before model failures can be interpreted. More dialogue can repair an insufficient description; it cannot recover an absent hidden correspondence without new shared evidence. The human source informs repair affordances; the information-adequacy correction came from root/reviewer analysis, not a result falsely attributed to that paper.

A future optional encounter would compare reciprocal/one-way messages with matched nonmessage evidence, informative/shuffled/no-message controls, known information boundaries and separate participation counts. It must first specify targets, access and permitted operations. These are designs, not run protocols. Two contexts share weights, language and source ancestry; they are not independent minds or evidence sources.

## Did the assumption-open method work?

It yielded an actionable alternative and two consequential corrections in this session: optional reciprocal clarification and explicit identifiability. That warranted a single adjacent-field research expansion. It did not provide a controlled comparison against ordinary scouting, validate FRR, demonstrate the small model arrangement or justify indefinitely widening sources.

Expansion stopped after the adjacent study and critical review because the remaining issue is local feasibility and honest interface design. More papers cannot determine whether this body uses the offered channel. The side lead remains a research candidate, alongside the expression baseline and other unpromoted mechanisms. SIDE_METHOD.json retains promotion criteria, scope, ancestry and stopping reason.

## Current confidence and next work

Source methods, cached-source mechanics and the preserved-file record are inspectable at their stated scopes. The design is clearer, including where an independent consequence could arise and where recording can fail. Local language continuity, useful reciprocal repair, frozen-body generalization, reliable stop interpretation, effective long context, CPU training and method superiority remain unmeasured.

The next strongest engineering step is a small isolated recording/input-assembly specification: choose scheduling variant, freeze exact native rendering and stop/budget handling, and decide interruption policy. A recorder can be checked with authored fixtures before any resident model call. This is external infrastructure, not a growing list of behavioral instructions. Then a short explicitly bounded expression contact can be considered. No build, inference or training job is queued by this report.

All previous worlds remain preserved and paused. This research changed proposed observation mechanics and questions; it did not overwrite any garden season or enact a semantic world. Record integrity checks are separate from truth or behavioral performance, and peer review with shared inputs is not independent replication.
