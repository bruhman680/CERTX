# Side phase 1: critical review of asymmetric symbolic exchange

20 November 2026, America/Chicago. Research/design review only. I read BRIEF.json, exact compact FRR, the previous DESIGN.md and lg_transfer's candidate message. I inspected the lead primary source below beyond its abstract. No model contact, training, build, model/dataset download or old-record edit occurred. This review does not pre-complete main rounds 2 or 3.

**Recommendation: promote the asymmetric-view lead to one bounded adjacent-field research contact, not to an efficacy claim or implementation.** It identifies something an archive alone lacks: language can alter another context's access to a consequential choice. The training mismatch limits the claimed mechanism but does not invalidate the design question.

## Source/result/bridge

In [Lazaridou, Peysakhovich and Baroni (2017)](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.07182v2), §§2–5, independent feedforward sender/receiver networks play rewarded referential games over pretrained VGG image features. Both networks' parameters are updated with REINFORCE. Communication succeeds, including configurations using only two symbols. In §4.1, agents receive different image instances while target/distractor classes remain matched; coordination persists and raw category purity increases modestly. Adjusted purity does not uniformly improve across configurations. Human interpretability in §5 additionally uses supervised image-label training. Neither fluent human-like meaning nor arbitrary transfer follows from coordination.

The useful bridge is functional: remove shared surface particulars while retaining a shared relation, so a message must bridge a genuine information difference. A garden could expose two contexts to different views of a small persistent artifact, with optional communication affecting what the other can inspect or change. This is an architectural possibility, not the paper's demonstrated learning procedure. Fixed identical weights, optional engagement and no reward updates remove major ingredients of the reported emergence. Two contexts also share pretrained conventions; they are not independent evidence sources or newly independent minds.

## Serious simple rivals

First, ordinary pretrained English may solve the coordination immediately. That would be successful situated use, still worth observing, but not a newly invented language or learned general mechanism. Do not forbid English merely to manufacture emergence. A novel local convention requires evidence of history-dependent use beyond an existing label.

Second, the receiver might infer the target from order, IDs, shared prompt text, visible operations or previous public records. Message shuffling is informative only when the shuffled messages preserve the relevant distribution and the target is otherwise hidden. A no-message comparison must retain the same nonmessage evidence. Otherwise a gain could simply reflect added target information, without adaptation or abstraction. That elementary information-carrying result is legitimate but narrower.

Third, asymmetric views could merely make the interface harder. Lower participation or less interpretable output cannot be scored as absent capacity. Optional contacts require separate offered, accepted and interpretable denominators; matching nominal budgets does not remove selection effects.

## Design consequence and bounded next contact

Retain the archive as a carrier but allow research on interaction without a required continuing persona. Specify which context sees which facts and what mechanically happens when a message is sent. Keep free expression outside any scored choice; do not make solving a reference game the condition for further garden access. Doubling calls and maintaining separated contexts adds resource and leakage costs, currently unmeasured here.

One adjacent-field expansion is justified: inspect one primary human collaborative-reference/grounding study for how partners establish and repair reference with unequal views. The question is whether useful interaction mechanics can be borrowed without importing a compulsory task, reward or prescribed protocol. That contact can change whether the alternative needs clarification/repair affordances; it cannot validate this model. Root decides expansion after inspecting the lead.

The proposed discriminating contact remains hypothetical: matched same-view versus relation-preserving different-view encounters, with meaningful message/no-message/shuffled-message comparisons and all actual artifacts retained. Distinguish immediate information transmission, history-dependent convention and selective use under changed relations. None entails universality.
