# SIDE phase 1: symbols with consequences beyond a narrator

**Status:** hypothesis scouting only. Read the new BRIEF, exact compact FRR and prior DESIGN. FRR guides this research; it supplies no claimed cognitive upgrade. No model execution, training, builds or model/dataset downloads. The three mechanisms below are alternatives, not components to combine automatically. None is a demonstrated improvement for this body.

## Three different carriers and mechanisms

1. **Asymmetric-view exchange.** Replace the single autobiographical resident with two separately assembled contexts of the same fixed-weight model. Each receives a partial view of a shared symbolic artifact; they can exchange messages and optionally select or modify parts of it. The host enforces visibility and actual artifact operations, not semantic correctness of prose. Public artifacts and each context's accessible messages carry persistence. Symbols can matter because another context lacks the information they convey. Shared model weights mean these are not independent minds or replications.

2. **Transformation workbench.** Replace the record as the primary world with short symbol strings and a few deterministic composable operations. Operations alter persistent objects; arbitrary prose remains available but cannot rewrite operation laws. The useful candidate mechanism is repeated contact with operation order, reversibility and invariants, rather than prediction as an assigned goal. A minimal interpreter is genuine engineering, even if its laws are few. Restrict it to inert symbolic objects; no general shell. This would trade unconstrained prose consequences for inspectable external consequences.

3. **Archive without a permanent protagonist.** Make durable artifacts, links and retrieval the organizing substrate. Host calls instantiate temporary continuations around a selected artifact, without asking for stable personality, daily plans or a single coherent life. An explicit retrieval policy determines which older material returns; original records stay intact. This tests continuity through revisitable objects rather than continuously visible autobiography. Retrieval can simply impose apparent continuity; it is not evidence of internal retention.

## Source-to-design weave for the strongest lead

**Evidence.** Lazaridou, Peysakhovich & Baroni's [Multi-Agent Cooperation and the Emergence of (Natural) Language](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.07182v2), read §§1–6 including architectures, training, tables and human-grounding experiment, studies feedforward sender/receiver networks trained with REINFORCE for 50k games on pretrained VGG representations. A sender signals which of two images is the target. Both players receive success-dependent rewards. Agents coordinate nearly perfectly, sometimes with only two symbols. Giving players different images from the same object classes removes shared instance details and yields small raw purity gains, with mixed gains after chance adjustment. Human-readable meanings require additional supervised grounding; pure game success does not establish natural language or broad understanding.

**Precise functional bridge, proposed.** Retain the information constraint, not the paper's optimization machinery: one context has information another cannot obtain except through a message. Offer relation-preserving but differently rendered views of a small graph, with names independently permuted. The sender may refer to a selected substructure; the receiver may identify or act on its corresponding substructure. Shared relations, rather than coincident local labels, must do useful work. English, invented marks and silence remain possible. No requirement to invent a language or predict continuously.

**Rival/countercondition.** Existing English descriptions may solve everything immediately, yielding useful communication without emergent conventions. Shared views permit copying; IDs, order or common context may leak the target and make messages unnecessary. Even correct coordination can exploit an accidental shortcut. Most seriously, the source learns weights through many rewarded interactions; this project's fixed-weight 135M model may not adapt even locally. The bridge is a research candidate, not an extrapolated source result.

**Scope/resource/access.** Start conceptually with two serialized contexts sharing one cached body, a tiny host-owned graph and bounded messages. This requires neither multiple loaded checkpoints nor online services, but increases inference calls and bookkeeping; throughput and usable context remain unmeasured. There is no trained visual encoder here: symbolic views replace images, changing the evidence. Declining interaction remains possible. Host visibility restrictions are world laws; scoring stays observer-side.

**One falsifiable contact.** In detached, optional paired encounters, compare relation-preserving different-instance views against shared-instance views, with equal message budgets and shuffled/no-message controls retaining the same receiver evidence. Shuffled messages must preserve message distribution. Freeze the target before messages; counterbalance label permutations and ensure an informative message could distinguish the alternatives. If informative messages do not improve receiver selections over their controls, the proposed communicative channel has not demonstrated utility in this contact. If utility survives new renderings, it supports bounded relational communication, not acquisition of language or universal transfer. Unequal participation leaves causal comparison unresolved.

## Other source support and limits

[Mirchandani et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.04721v2), §§3–7 read in the preceding round, motivates the workbench: large pretrained models show partial random-token invariance and procedurally generated transformation competence. Tokenization, model scale and supplied context matter; it establishes no 135M capability.

[Park et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03442), introduction and §§4–7 read, motivates explicit retrieval carriers. Its memory/reflection/planning ablations improve human-ranked interview believability using stronger hosted models and preaccumulated full-architecture histories. Believability is not truthful recall or transfer; the persona-free archive is my inference, not their tested architecture.
