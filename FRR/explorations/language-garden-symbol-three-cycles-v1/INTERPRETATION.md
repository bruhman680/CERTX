# How to read the three comparisons

Cycle 1 changed the question from equality to naming the last symbol. Cycle 2 retained that new question and changed the display of the row. Cycle 3 retained the display and restored the original equality question. These are sequential operator decisions. Each call started in a fresh context and the model received no previous reply, result or correction.

The two scores answer distinct questions. Exact scoring requires the requested short output. Content scoring asks whether a longer reply unambiguously names the correct last symbol or answers the specified first/last equality. A sentence that accurately names the final symbol counts as content success even when the requested format is missed. A correct incidental substring is insufficient. A cap is recorded as a stop cause. It does not make unsupported or contradictory text correct.

All cycles reuse the four earlier rows. The fresh pair designation refers to those strings' status at the time of the earlier endpoint study. Their reuse here cannot count as a held-out transfer test. The intervention in Cycle 2 adds position labels, line breaks and input tokens at once. The model can read a supplied Last field without needing to infer the final position on its own.
