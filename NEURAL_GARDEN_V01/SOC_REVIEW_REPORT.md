# Independent verification society — seven probes, conservative loop count

**Scope:** Re-evaluate Neural Garden v0.1 and the reported `.50/.55` dynamics under fresh matched seeds and deliberate controls. Executed 52 distinct threshold-world runs (13 configurations ×4 fresh seeds 8101–8104) plus 12 equation probes and a 135-tick neural-controller checkpoint check. There are **seven labeled probes, but only four genuinely distinct new research questions (R4–R7)**. R1–R3 are necessary integrity/verification checks, not additional conceptual loops. Neither seeded simulation nor audit checklist item counts as a loop. Source and runtime fingerprints, all per-run snapshots, raw drifts, and action-resource covariance are preserved in `SOC_REVIEW_results.json`, generated by `SOC_REVIEW_probe.py`.

The review is a *simulator-level reproducibility check*. Its four seeds provide only weak extrapolation and no independent biological evidence.

## Loop R1 — Does the claimed exact budget actually hold?

**Pressure:** A false conservation equation would invalidate our threshold analysis.

**Probe:** Recomputed one-step resource and raw-energy balances across 2×2, 4×4, 8×8, and 16×16 toroidal worlds, three seeds each, randomized harvest actions, directly from the unchanged `garden.py` transition coefficients.

**Evidence:** The global diffusion term cancels; both residuals fall at floating-point rounding scale (see `exact_identities` in the JSON). This supports the *implemented-world* accounting, not an external physical law.

**Next question:** Can clipping make an apparently stationary cell energy metric misleading?

## Loop R2 — Are energy boundaries censoring harm and benefit?

**Probe:** Contrasted the same raw delta with realized delta at deliberately selected energy-floor and ceiling states. Recorded exact values and the maximum clipping gap.

**Evidence:** At energy zero, a raw negative reward can yield an observed change of zero. At the upper cap, positive gain can be hidden. The raw budget and realized energy must be kept separate; earlier Garden reports did so after the audit.

**Next question:** Are a learned network's metrics and checkpoint mechanically reproducible, rather than merely plausible?

## Loop R3 — Do learning state and checkpoint claims survive a direct integrity test?

**Probe:** Compared identical initialized learned/frozen v0.1 cells, ran 120 ticks, checked distinct learned action weights, ran the purported nonmutating off-policy probe, then checkpointed and resumed 15 ticks against uninterrupted continuation.

**Evidence:** Neural policy weights change when learning is enabled; the standardized prediction probe leaves full state unchanged; full checkpoint fingerprints match exactly after save/load and additional continuation. These results establish deterministic bookkeeping, **not useful intelligence**. The on-policy predictor does not observe its current sampled action, so MSE does not by itself establish an action-conditioned model.

**Next question:** Does `.55` look good only because earlier evaluations stopped too soon?

## Loop R4 — Can horizon reverse the ranking of simple policies?

**Probe:** Matched seeds with fixed thresholds .50 and .55 at 500, 2,000 and 5,000 steps, original unchanged laws, 12×12 grids.

| Threshold | t500 energy | t2000 | t5000 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| .50 | .6141 | .6386 | **.6714** |
| .55 | .5011 | .2726 | **.0369** |

**Evidence:** The attractive `.55` 500-tick snapshot was a transient; the ranking reverses decisively by t5000. The .55 tail raw energy budget remains negative (around −.000151/tick), and the t5000 floor fraction rises to ~11.3%. The `.50` tail budget in this particular 12×12 four-seed screen is near zero (−6.1e−7), not a proof of infinite stability.

**Next question:** Is the result a size-dependent artifact of the original 16×16 grid?

## Loop R5 — What changes with spatial scale and diffusion?

**Probe:** Under fixed .50 policy and 3,000 ticks, compared 8×8, 12×12, 16×16 worlds, then held 12×12 fixed while testing zero or larger diffusion. All use matched seeds and unchanged original regrowth/harvest terms.

**Evidence:** Final energies: 8×8 `.6551`, 16×16 `.6551` (rounding coincidence); no diffusion `.5545`, diffusion `.12` `.6844`. Corresponding action-resource covariance remains positive in all arms (~.0103–.0107). Thus this independent screen corroborates a diffusion contribution but does not localize its mechanism or guarantee scale invariance beyond three sizes. Diffusion=0 and .12 are explicitly modified-world interventions.

**Next question:** Is the apparent balance robust to a single coefficient change?

## Loop R6 — Does changing one physical coefficient overturn the policy?

**Probe:** Same .50 policy, 3,000 ticks, but regrowth `.06→.055` *or* harvest consumption `.18→.19`, compared with unchanged original world.

**Evidence:** Final mean energy collapses to `.0211` (reduced regrowth) and `.0164` (increased consumption), vs the original-world `.65`-ish range. Their tail raw drifts are strongly negative (about −.00182 and −.00113 respectively). This is a fragility result in the specified simulation family, not a general ecological law.

**Next question:** Can an abrupt resource loss leave a persistent mark even when physics later returns to baseline?

## Loop R7 — Does resource restoration imply energetic recovery?

**Probe:** Same .50 world over 3,000 ticks with one exogenous resource×.65 shock at tick1000, laws otherwise unchanged.

**Evidence:** Final energy `.5203` versus an unshocked reference around `.65` at the comparable 3,000-tick horizon. Tail raw drift is negative (−.0000866/tick), while resource/action covariance is reduced to `.00646` from `.01059` in the no-shock reference. A temporary resource shock can alter the historical energy distribution even after resources regrow. This does not establish permanent irreversibility or predict every seed.

## Adjudication and research-loop hygiene

1. **Supported:** raw energy/resource budget identities, exact full-state checkpoint continuation, nonmutating standardized probe, finite-horizon threshold sensitivity, resource-action covariance as a useful measured relationship, sensitivity to diffusion and law changes.
2. **Not supported:** neural ecological intelligence, an optimal universal threshold, indefinite viability, biological survival, or a scientific law derived from selected synthetic simulations.
3. **Selection caution:** .50 was chosen using earlier exploratory screens. These four new seeds check within-generator repeatability, not an untouched preregistered model selection test. A horizon chosen after inspecting results can create large ranking distortions.
4. **Replication unit:** seeds/whole runs, not independent cells or ticks; changing physics coefficients creates new experimental regimes and must be labeled as such.
5. **Unmeasured:** all-cell long-horizon survival, adaptive learning advantage against a tuned fixed-rule baseline under predeclared external regime shifts, and reliable autonomous continuation between chat sessions.

**Verdict:** The Garden's strongest evidence remains its narrow and fragile connection between local resource availability, harvesting decisions, and raw energy budget. These four research loops plus three integrity checks found corroboration and sharper limits—not a new broad intelligence claim. Next society should use predeclared regimes and same-history forks to distinguish adaptation from simply benefiting from a favorable resource cycle.
