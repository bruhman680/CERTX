## Three coupled questions

1. **Learning stability:** Can locally measured feedback gate updates, revert harmful changes, or retain useful earlier policy without a human rescue rule? Compare exact frozen policy, same update budget sham gates, matched randomness, and predeclared environments. Demonstrate learned-policy improvement separately from changes in stochastic action mode.
2. **Memory and social transfer:** Do retained experiences or neighboring cells carry transferable predictive/behavioral information under changing laws? Use no-memory/no-transfer and shuffled-transfer controls. Treat inherited parameters, predictive history, and actual task benefit as separate outcomes.
3. **Persistent ecology:** Can replayable, seasonally perturbed trajectory segments and raw energy budgets distinguish viable recovery from delayed collapse? Preserve the original `runs/living_world` checkpoint at tick100, and run independent forks without overwriting its weights, RNG, snapshots, or lineage.

## Design and interpretation rules

- Record exact source, settings, seeds, checkpoints and metrics when executable; do not turn simulation ticks or hyperparameter proposals into counted research loops.
- Compare mean energy **and** low-energy occupancy, raw late energy drift, per-harvest yield, and recovery under distribution shift. Energy and viability may rank policies differently.
- Separate discovery (exploratory tuning) from untouched-seed confirmation, or label post-selection honestly.
- Report failures, invariants, counterexamples and confounds. No claim of actual consciousness, independent autonomous agents within cells, biological emergence, or indefinite viability from synthetic short-horizon runs.
- The archival branch is `codex/frr-v05-explorations`; no overwrite of canonical historical checkpoint, baseline implementations, or unrelated repo branches.
