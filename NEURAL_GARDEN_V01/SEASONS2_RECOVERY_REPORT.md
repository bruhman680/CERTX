# Season recovery: held-aside exact-state interventions

**Executed.** Predeclared `SEASONS2_RECOVERY_PLAN.md`, exact source and individual outcomes in `SEASONS2_RECOVERY_probe.py`/`SEASONS2_RECOVERY_results.json`. Four *held-aside* seeds 41120–41123, each independently run for 12,000 repeated season ticks under fixed .51 harvest policy, then forked to three continuations from identical local states (resource+energy): 8,000 extra ticks at normal regrowth .06, an externally boosted .07, or another alternating sequence with 1,500-tick scarcity .048 intervals. The original canonical Living Garden was not read or modified.

## Loop S4 — Does ecological state recover on an 8,000-tick normal-weather extension?

From identical t12000 parent states (mean energy roughly .096), the normal-world extension increases mean energy to **.1305** after 1,500, **.1644** after 3,000, and **.2733** after 8,000 ticks; final depleted-cell fraction is **.0537**. The continued seasonal schedule ends at only **.0436** (depleted fraction **.0869**). Recovery is gradual with standard laws, interrupted by renewed scarcity, and therefore path-dependent on prolonged history.

## Loop S5 — Could an external resource-law intervention help more than neural policy changes?

Increasing regrowth from .06 to .07 at the same starting state raises final mean energy to **.9817** with **zero depleted cells**, versus **.2733** and **5.37% depleted** for normal regrowth. This is a large regime shift under the **synthetic simulator's** sharply balanced harvest laws, not a discovered biological principle. It is purely a designer-supplied change in ecology, not self-directed adaptation or a learned policy.

The fork isolates environmental causality because each seed starts the three arms with exactly the same resource and energy arrays and a fixed rule. It does not isolate details of later harvest patterns (those are downstream effects of the environmental intervention), nor claim universal benefit outside this regrowth range. Only four independently initialized worlds were tested.
