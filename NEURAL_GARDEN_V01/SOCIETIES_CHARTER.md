**Scope:** The user authorized a total of 2,000 FRR research loops, including a currently approved tranche of 200. The research ledger documented 31 genuine loops before this tranche's remaining **183**. Several parallel societies are coordinating bounded research passes. This charter authorizes work; it is not evidence that any scheduled loops have completed.

## A counted research loop

One loop must preserve the following in an inspectable record:

1. **Orientation:** a specific uncertainty, question, or competing explanation, grounded in previous evidence.
2. **Action:** an actual analytical derivation, executable experiment, independent test, or material algorithm change that could distinguish the alternatives.
3. **Observation:** what was measured, under which seeds/controls/laws, including unexpected or null outcomes.
4. **Revision:** a defensible next question, hypothesis update, or explicit decision to stop that line.
5. **Lineage:** source/config fingerprints or complete method, and whether observations are independent or derived from the same historical world.

Do **not** count: each random seed, cell, network step, model thought, rephrasing, untested suggestion, literature item, or routine tool invocation. Multiple runs can belong to one loop. Independent replay/audit can count only when it tests a distinct consequential uncertainty.
